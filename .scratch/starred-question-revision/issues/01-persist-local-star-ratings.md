# 01: Persist local star ratings

**What to build:** Guest learners can assign, change, and remove 1–3-star ratings from practice and chapter question lists. Ratings persist locally, work for unseen theory questions, and do not affect attempts or review scheduling.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately).

**Status:** resolved

- [ ] A learner can set, replace, and remove a star rating from both practice and a chapter question list, including for an unseen theory question.
- [ ] Star ratings persist with browser-local learning progress and survive a reload without creating or altering answer-derived learning records.
- [ ] Existing learning progress migrates safely to a rating-capable state; answering a starred theory question leaves its rating unchanged.
- [ ] The rating control is keyboard-accessible and communicates its current state to assistive technology.
- [ ] Focused tests cover rating changes, migration, local persistence, and preservation of current progress behavior.
