# Qualification and Ability of Drivers — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Two-point entries normally have a five-year deletion period

The statutory *Tilgungsfrist* is **five years** for a particularly
traffic-safety-impairing administrative offence assessed at two points. The same
five-year category generally covers traffic-related criminal decisions unless a
licence withdrawal or isolated disqualification moves the entry into the
ten-year category. [StVG §29(1) sentence 2 nos. 2–3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvg/__29.html)

For comparison:

- a one-point traffic-safety administrative offence is normally deleted after
  **2 years 6 months**;
- a two-point offence covered above is deleted after **5 years**; and
- a criminal decision involving licence withdrawal or an isolated ban on
  regranting is deleted after **10 years**.

These periods run independently according to the applicable start rule, normally
from finality of the criminal, court, or administrative decision. After deletion
maturity, specified entries can remain technically stored for a one-year
*Überliegefrist* with tightly limited uses; that does not change the five-year
exam answer. [StVG §29(1), (4), (6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvg/__29.html)

Two points are assigned to traffic-safety-related crimes not carrying the
three-point licence-withdrawal consequence and to particularly serious
traffic-safety administrative offences. [StVG §4(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvg/__4.html)

## Common exam traps

- Selecting 2 years 6 months, which is normally the one-point period.
- Selecting 10 years without a licence-withdrawal/disqualification decision.
- Counting from the day of the traffic act rather than the decision's finality.
- Confusing deletion maturity with the limited one-year technical overhang.

## Remember this

- **1 point:** 2 years 6 months.
- **2 points:** 5 years.
- **3-point withdrawal category:** 10 years.
- **Start:** normally when the decision becomes final.
