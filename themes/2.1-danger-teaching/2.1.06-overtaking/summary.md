# Overtaking — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### The complete manoeuvre must be visible and safe

<!-- questions: 2.1.06-003-B, 2.1.06-005-B, 2.1.06-008-M, 2.1.06-009, 2.1.06-021-M, 2.1.06-022-M, 2.1.06-024-M, 2.1.06-026-M, 2.1.06-027-M, 2.1.06-028-M, 2.1.06-029-M, 2.1.06-033-M, 2.1.06-035-M, 2.1.06-104-M, 2.1.06-106-M, 2.1.06-107-M -->

You may overtake only if you can see that, throughout the manoeuvre, oncoming
traffic will not be hindered and you can return safely. Do not start before a
blind bend, hill, narrowing, or other restricted view. A broken centre line does
not make an otherwise unsafe manoeuvre legal. [StVO §5(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

The required side clearance is part of the visible distance calculation. Keep at
least 1.5 m from cyclists, pedestrians, and users of small vehicles in built-up
areas and at least 2 m outside built-up areas. If oncoming traffic, a narrow lane,
roadworks, trees, or poor surface means that clearance cannot be maintained,
wait. [StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### Check what other road users may do

<!-- questions: 2.1.06-003-B, 2.1.06-005-B, 2.1.06-007-M, 2.1.06-012-M, 2.1.06-013, 2.1.06-015, 2.1.06-023-M, 2.1.06-024-M, 2.1.06-031-M, 2.1.06-035-M -->

Before pulling out, check mirrors and the blind spot and anticipate that the van
or vehicle ahead may itself begin overtaking. A vehicle already overtaking you
has priority over your new overtaking attempt: abandon your attempt and remain
behind. A fast vehicle behind flashing its lights does not give permission to
break the rules; complete a safe manoeuvre and merge only when it is safe. [StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

Do not pass a vehicle that signals or begins turning left. Abort when a tractor
or other vehicle will cross your path, since continuing could cause a collision
with it or obstruct oncoming traffic. [StVO §5(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### Overtaking on motorways and multi-lane roads

<!-- questions: 2.1.06-004-B, 2.1.06-010, 2.1.06-012-M, 2.1.06-013, 2.1.06-014, 2.1.06-015, 2.1.06-103-M -->

Keep right (*Rechtsfahrgebot*) and normally pass on the left. Passing on the
right is allowed only in the narrow statutory exceptions, including slow-moving
queues where the left lane is occupied and traffic on the right is moving only
slightly faster. A free left lane is not a queue, so do not undertake there.
 [StVO §7(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html), [StVO §5(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

If a lane is ending, move into the continuing lane in good time and do not allow
pressure from behind to push you into oncoming traffic. When already alongside
the vehicle being passed and a fast vehicle approaches behind, finish promptly
only if it remains safe; otherwise return safely without forcing anyone to brake.
 [StVO §7(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html), [StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### Help resolve a dangerous overtaking situation

<!-- questions: 2.1.06-006-B, 2.1.06-010, 2.1.06-011, 2.1.06-017-M, 2.1.06-018-M, 2.1.06-025-M -->

If an overtaking vehicle has miscalculated or moves toward you, brake immediately
and keep as far right as practicable; if necessary use the verge only when that
avoids a greater danger. If you are being overtaken, do not accelerate: reduce
speed and move right to give the other vehicle room to return. [StVO §1(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html), [StVO §5(6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### Queues, convoys, and narrow avenues

<!-- questions: 2.1.06-002, 2.1.06-030, 2.1.06-032, 2.1.06-033-M -->

Do not automatically overtake an entire queue. The last vehicle has the longest
overtaking path and must expect vehicles ahead to pull out. In a closed convoy
(*geschlossener Verband*), do not split the group by forcing an early merge; a
long convoy also hides the traffic situation ahead. [StVO §27(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__27.html), [StVO §5(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

Tree-lined avenues make lateral distance and changing light difficult to judge.
High vehicles may move around branches, and trees leave little escape space. On
a narrow road, select a speed allowing you to stop within half the visible
distance where oncoming traffic could be endangered. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Pedestrians, cyclists, and mopeds

<!-- questions: 2.1.06-009, 2.1.06-028-M, 2.1.06-029-M, 2.1.06-104-M, 2.1.06-105, 2.1.06-106-M, 2.1.06-107-M -->

Do not pass cyclists side-by-side when the road cannot provide the required
clearance. Near pedestrians, reduce to walking speed and keep right if passing
would be unsafe. At roadworks, a 30-km/h limit and a narrowed, poor surface may
make it impossible to pass a moped safely: do not pass merely because you could
travel faster than it. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html), [StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- A vehicle showing a left-turn signal must not be passed on the left; pass on
  the right only where the road and situation make the legal exception safe.
  [StVO §5(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)
- On motorways, right-side passing is not generally allowed merely because the
  right lane is empty. [StVO §7(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)
- A speed limit, lane marking, or a vehicle's slow speed never replaces the
  visibility, clearance, and no-hindrance tests. [StVO §3(1), §5(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

## Remember this

- **See the whole manoeuvre:** if not, wait.
- **Clearance:** 1.5 m urban, 2 m outside built-up areas for vulnerable users.
- **Left turn:** do not pass on the left.
- **Being overtaken:** slow and keep right; never race.
- **Motorway:** normally overtake on the left; right only under exceptions.
- **Danger:** brake, keep right, and create escape space.
