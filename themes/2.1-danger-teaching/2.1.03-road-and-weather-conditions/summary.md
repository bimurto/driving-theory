# Road and Weather Conditions — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Aquaplaning: prevent it with speed and tyres

<!-- questions: 2.1.03-015, 2.1.03-023, 2.1.03-034, 2.1.03-101, 2.1.03-102, 2.1.03-123 -->

Aquaplaning occurs when a water wedge separates the tyres from the road. Risk
rises with speed, water depth, worn tread, tyre/pressure condition, and water
collected in ruts or dips. Steering and braking ability can then fall almost to
zero and the vehicle may leave the road. Slow substantially before deep water;
there is no universal speed at which aquaplaning always begins.
[StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVZO §36(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__36.html) [FahrschAusbO Annex 1, section 2.7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fahrschausbo_2012/anlage_1.html)

If the vehicle aquaplanes while travelling straight, keep the steering wheel as
straight as possible and ease off the accelerator gently. Do not brake hard or
make a sudden steering correction; wait for tyre contact to return before making
smooth corrections. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [FahrschAusbO Annex 1, section 2.7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fahrschausbo_2012/anlage_1.html)

### Rain reduces grip, braking, and visibility

<!-- questions: 2.1.03-024-B, 2.1.03-025, 2.1.03-033, 2.1.03-036, 2.1.03-037, 2.1.03-103, 2.1.03-104 -->

Wet road and spray reduce tyre grip, lengthen braking distance, and make your
vehicle harder to see. A sudden change in tyre noise can signal a surface or
water-depth change. Reduce speed, increase following distance, use smooth
steering/braking/acceleration, and ensure required lighting is on.
[StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO §4(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html) [StVO §17(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

The first rain after a long dry period can mix water with dust, rubber, oil, and
pollen into a particularly slippery film. It can also smear the windscreen and
wet brakes before water clears it. Increase distance immediately and operate
wipers, demisting, and ventilation so your view remains unobstructed.
[StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### Snow and slush conceal ice and narrow the usable road

<!-- questions: 2.1.03-017-B, 2.1.03-029, 2.1.03-115, 2.1.03-122, 2.1.03-124 -->

Avoid overtaking through slush: it can hide ice, pull the vehicle sideways, and
be thrown onto another windscreen. Snowbanks conceal junction users, reduce
usable width and side clearance, and push pedestrians onto the carriageway.
Brake early, keep right, wait before an obstruction when changing lane is not
safe, and expect a much longer stopping distance. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO §5(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html) [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

## Side wind

### Reduce speed and make small, timely steering corrections

<!-- questions: 2.1.03-018, 2.1.03-020, 2.1.03-021-B, 2.1.03-022-B, 2.1.03-038, 2.1.03-044-M -->

Strong side wind can displace the vehicle laterally. Reduce speed and steer
smoothly against the wind. Expect sudden gusts on bridges, open ground, forest
firebreaks, and when leaving shelter. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO Annex 1, sign 117](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html)

When wind blows from the right, it pushes you left. Entering a truck's sheltered
area suddenly removes that force while you are countersteering, pulling you
toward the truck; leaving the shelter suddenly restores the push left. Hold extra
clearance, reduce speed before overtaking, and adjust steering progressively at
both transitions. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Fog and visibility

### Keep right, adapt speed, and use the correct lights

<!-- questions: 2.1.03-105, 2.1.03-106, 2.1.03-107, 2.1.03-117 -->

In fog, keep within your lane and orient to the right edge; never straddle the
centre line. Switch on dipped headlights during daytime fog, snowfall, or rain
that substantially impairs visibility. Use wipers, washer, outside-air
ventilation, heating, or air conditioning to clear droplets and mist before
spray or slush blinds you. [StVO §2(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html) [StVO §17(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html) [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

Use rear fog lights only when fog reduces visibility below **50 m**. In fog,
snowfall, or rain with visibility below 50 m, maximum speed is **50 km/h**, and
often less is necessary. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO §17(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Abort an overtake when the view disappears

<!-- questions: 2.1.03-111 -->

If weather or spray removes the clear view needed to overtake several trucks,
abort safely, merge back without forcing another vehicle to brake, and rebuild
an adequate following distance. Overtake only when the whole manoeuvre can be
completed without obstructing oncoming traffic and with sufficiently greater
speed. [StVO §5(2), (4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html) [StVO §4(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html)

## Narrow avenues and road damage

### Half the visible distance when meeting is difficult

<!-- questions: 2.1.03-027, 2.1.03-028, 2.1.03-030, 2.1.03-109, 2.1.03-110, 2.1.03-116-M, 2.1.03-120 -->

Tree-lined avenues are often narrow, cambered, root-damaged, and visually closed
by bends and alternating light/shadow. Tall vehicles may move toward the centre
to clear branches. Expect oncoming traffic partly in your lane, brake, keep as
far right as safely possible, and wait where necessary. Do not evade a pothole
across the centre in a blind bend. [StVO §2(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html) [StVO §6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__6.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

Where the road is so narrow that meeting vehicles cannot safely pass, you must
be able to stop within **half the visible distance**. This commonly prevents
overtaking even a cyclist when oncoming traffic is present; the required lateral
clearance and clear route must both exist. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Driver-assistance warnings

### Flashing ESP/ESC means grip is being used up

<!-- questions: 2.1.03-118, 2.1.03-121 -->

A flashing ESP/ESC lamp normally means the system is actively stabilising the
vehicle because wheels are nearing loss of grip. Reduce speed and smooth the
controls; the system cannot repeal physics. A lamp that remains on can indicate
the system is disabled or faulty and should be checked according to the vehicle
manual. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [FahrschAusbO Annex 1, section 2.7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fahrschausbo_2012/anlage_1.html)

### ACC can lose perception in bad weather

<!-- questions: 2.1.03-119 -->

Snow, spray, ice, or dirt can obstruct ACC radar/camera sensing and limit or
disable distance control. Monitor the system, take personal control of speed and
distance, and never assume automation sees what you cannot. Driver-assistance use
does not transfer the driver's responsibility. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html) [FahrschAusbO Annex 1, section 2.7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fahrschausbo_2012/anlage_1.html)

## Dataset warning

The explanation for **2.1.03-015** suggests aquaplaning starts “from 60 km/h.”
There is no fixed legal or physical threshold: it depends on water depth, tread,
pressure, tyre width, ruts, and speed. Treat 60 km/h as a warning mnemonic, not
a safe/unsafe boundary. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

## Remember this

- **Aquaplaning:** ease off, steering straight, no abrupt braking.
- **Rain/snow:** slower, farther back, smoother inputs.
- **Truck + wind:** force disappears entering shelter, returns leaving it.
- **Below 50 m:** rear fog allowed; max 50 km/h.
- **Narrow meeting road:** stop within half the visible distance.
- **ESP flashes / ACC limited:** reduce speed and take control.
