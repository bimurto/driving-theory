# Special Traffic Situations — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Anticipate and adapt

<!-- questions: 2.1.07-002-B, 2.1.07-004-B, 2.1.07-006-B, 2.1.07-008-M, 2.1.07-009-M, 2.1.07-010-M, 2.1.07-014-M, 2.1.07-015-M, 2.1.07-017, 2.1.07-018, 2.1.07-020, 2.1.07-022-M, 2.1.07-023-M, 2.1.07-025-M, 2.1.07-026-M, 2.1.07-028-M, 2.1.07-029, 2.1.07-111, 2.1.07-112, 2.1.07-113-M, 2.1.07-114-M, 2.1.07-115, 2.1.07-116, 2.1.07-117-M, 2.1.07-121-M, 2.1.07-123-M, 2.1.07-124-M, 2.1.07-202, 2.1.07-204, 2.1.07-205, 2.1.07-206, 2.1.07-207, 2.1.07-210-M -->

Drive only as fast as conditions permit and as slowly as necessary to stop within
the visible distance. Reduce further and prepare to brake near parking areas,
blind bends, narrow avenues, schools, animals, turning vehicles, and vehicles
emerging from side roads. Give way where necessary even if you technically have
priority: preventing a collision is the overriding duty. [StVO §1(2), §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

Narrow bends hide obstacles and motorcycles may cut the corner or enter your lane
when leaning. Keep right, slow down, and do not overtake cyclists on an incline
until oncoming traffic has passed and the legal side clearance is available.
 [StVO §3(1), §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Distance, speed, and reaction

<!-- questions: 2.1.07-028-M, 2.1.07-109, 2.1.07-110, 2.1.07-120-M, 2.1.07-208 -->

The theory-test rule of thumb is reaction distance `(speed ÷ 10) × 3`: at 50
km/h about 15 m, at 100 km/h about 30 m. Normal braking distance is
`(speed ÷ 10)²`: 25 m at 50 km/h and 100 m at 100 km/h. Doubling speed therefore
quadruples braking distance. These are estimates, not legal guarantees; maintain
a substantially larger gap at higher speed and restore it when cruise control
allows the gap to shrink. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html), [StVO §4(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html)

### Motorcycles, cyclists, and pedestrians

<!-- questions: 2.1.07-102, 2.1.07-117-M, 2.1.07-122, 2.1.07-123-M, 2.1.07-124-M, 2.1.07-203, 2.1.07-204, 2.1.07-205, 2.1.07-207 -->

Expect motorcycles to lean into your lane or cut corners; never rely on a
motorcyclist remaining exactly within the apparent line. Check the blind spot:
mirrors cannot show every vehicle or pedestrian, and adjust mirrors for the
current driver. Keep at least 1.5 m side clearance in built-up areas and 2 m
outside when passing cyclists or pedestrians. Before opening a door or letting
passengers out beside a cycle path, check for cyclists and warn passengers.
 [StVO §5(4), §14(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### Wind, tyres, and control

<!-- questions: 2.1.07-101, 2.1.07-106 -->

A strong gust of crosswind or a burst tyre can suddenly deflect a fast vehicle.
Hold the steering wheel firmly, ease off the accelerator, steer against the
deviation, and brake cautiously. After a motorway breakdown, stop on the hard
shoulder if possible, switch on hazard warning lights, put on a warning vest,
and place the warning triangle at a suitable distance while keeping away from
traffic. [StVO §15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15.html), [StVO §17(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Wildlife and accidents

<!-- questions: 2.1.07-023-M, 2.1.07-105, 2.1.07-119 -->

If wildlife appears, brake and keep your lane; a last-second swerve can lead to
a tree or oncoming collision. If impact is unavoidable, brake as hard as
possible and maintain direction. After hitting game, stop, secure the scene with
hazard lights, and report the accident to the police or the responsible forestry
office. [StVO §1(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html), [StVO §34](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__34.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- In a queue, use the vehicles ahead as visual cues and a slightly staggered
  position, but preserve distance and readiness to brake. [StVO §4(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html)
- If a vehicle ahead may continue moving, do not assume it will remain stopped;
  maintain braking readiness. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)
- Priority can be waived to avoid danger, but never used to justify entering a
  conflict at speed. [StVO §1(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

## Remember this

- **Uncertainty:** slow down and be ready to brake.
- **Collision:** brake hard, hold direction; swerve only if clearly safer.
- **Speed:** 50→100 km/h means reaction distance 15→30 m and braking 25→100 m.
- **Blind spot:** mirrors never show everything.
- **Wildlife:** brake, stay straight, secure and report any collision.
- **Breakdown:** hard shoulder, hazard lights, vest, warning triangle, safety.
