# Affective and Emotional Behaviour in Road Traffic — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Keep distance, wait, and overtake only when safe

<!-- questions: 2.1.11-003, 2.1.11-008, 2.1.11-009-M, 2.1.11-014-M, 2.1.11-017, 2.1.11-112, 2.1.11-113, 2.1.11-114, 2.1.11-118, 2.1.11-121, 2.1.11-122, 2.1.11-124, 2.1.11-125 -->

When a vehicle ahead is slow, adjust your speed and keep the distance required to stop if it brakes suddenly. [StVO §4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html) Do not close the gap, sound the horn repeatedly, flash persistently, or pressure the other driver. Such conduct can violate the duty of consideration and, when used to force another person to act, may constitute **Nötigung** (coercion) under StGB §240. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html) [StGB §240](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stgb/__240.html)

Overtake only when you can see that the manoeuvre can be completed safely and without substantially obstructing or endangering anyone. A bend or unseen oncoming traffic means you cannot make that assessment; wait. [StVO §5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html) After overtaking, return with adequate clearance rather than cutting in. If another driver is forcing into a queue, reducing speed and leaving room can prevent a collision; being “right” is not worth an avoidable crash. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

If a vehicle behind tailgates, remain calm, maintain an appropriate speed, and keep right where the road situation permits. On a motorway, finish an established overtake promptly, then return to the right lane when safe; do not brake to punish the follower or use hazard lights merely because someone is impatient. [StVO §2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html) [StVO §18](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

### Lane changes are deliberate, not a race strategy

<!-- questions: 2.1.11-018 -->

Every **Fahrstreifenwechsel** requires observation, signalling, checking the blind spot, and giving priority to traffic already in the lane. Frequent changes create more opportunities to overlook someone or misjudge a gap, while usually saving little time. Use the right-hand lane principle and change only for a clear traffic reason. [StVO §2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html) [StVO §7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

### Control the driver before controlling the car

<!-- questions: 2.1.11-010, 2.1.11-011, 2.1.11-012, 2.1.11-019, 2.1.11-020, 2.1.11-126, 2.1.11-127, 2.1.11-128 -->

Anger, rage, worry, sadness, excitement, and even exuberant happiness can narrow attention or encourage aggressive, timid, or risky decisions. If you are upset before departure, calm down before driving. If agitation occurs on the road, concentrate on traffic, drive in a way that does not let anger affect safety, and stop at the next suitable place if necessary. This applies the StVO’s constant-care and mutual-consideration rule. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

After emergency braking or a near miss, shaking hands or knees is a warning that you are not yet settled. Stop at the earliest safe opportunity, breathe and recover, and continue only when composed. Later, review what happened without blaming others: **vorausschauendes Fahren** (anticipatory driving) means learning from hazards and expecting mistakes. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

### Fatigue and reduced attention require a break

<!-- questions: 2.1.11-012, 2.1.11-127, 2.1.11-129 -->

After a long working day, concentration is lower and tiredness arrives sooner. Frequent yawning, difficulty keeping the eyes open, or trouble holding the lane are signs to interrupt the journey immediately; microsleep can turn a moment into a severe crash. Do not drive when too tired to control the vehicle. The driver remains responsible for ensuring that the vehicle and its operation are safe. [StVO §23](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### Time pressure never justifies a traffic violation

<!-- questions: 2.1.11-016, 2.1.11-111, 2.1.11-116 -->

Time pressure increases willingness to take risks, encourages excessive speed, and makes hazards easier to underestimate. Plan extra time instead. **Umweltschonendes Fahren** (eco-driving)—anticipating, avoiding unnecessary acceleration and high speed—also improves safety because the driver scans farther ahead and has more time to react. [StVO §3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) A red light, a safety distance, and a speed limit remain binding even when late. [StVO §37](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__37.html) [StVO §4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html)

### Young drivers and night-time group trips need extra margins

<!-- questions: 2.1.11-015, 2.1.11-115, 2.1.11-117, 2.1.11-119, 2.1.11-123, 2.1.11-133 -->

“Disco accidents” commonly combine little experience after dark, little experience with a fully occupied car, distraction by passengers, inappropriate speed, and high **Risikobereitschaft** (readiness to take risks). Drivers aged 18–24 may overestimate their ability while misreading hazards. A defensive speed, a sober plan, and agreed passenger conduct protect the driver from being carried along by group excitement. The vehicle must not be occupied or loaded so that safety, visibility, or hearing is impaired. [StVO §3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO §21](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__21.html) [StVO §23](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### Passengers may help—but must not distract

<!-- questions: 2.1.11-013, 2.1.11-130, 2.1.11-131 -->

A good driver is calm, relaxed, cooperative, and does not answer provocation or try to demonstrate superiority. Treat useful passenger feedback seriously: if someone says you are tailgating, increase the gap. As a rule of thumb, about three car lengths (roughly 15 m) in town or two seconds outside town gives a useful margin, but the legal test is whether you could stop if the vehicle ahead braked suddenly. [StVO §4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html)

Conversation about the driver’s behaviour must not become a moving distraction. If a passenger wants a serious discussion, stop safely before engaging. The driver is responsible for ensuring that people and the vehicle do not compromise traffic safety. [StVO §23](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### Do not be provoked or copy illegal behaviour

<!-- questions: 2.1.11-007, 2.1.11-120, 2.1.11-132 -->

Do not accept a street-race challenge, retaliate against an inconsiderate driver, or use the horn and lights to show superiority. A **Kraftfahrzeugrennen** (motor-vehicle race) on public roads is prohibited, and dangerous speed or aggressive conduct conflicts with the basic duties of care and consideration. [StGB §315d](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stgb/__315d.html) [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

If another road user filters illegally through a stationary queue, do not obstruct, stop, or honk at them; maintain safe control and do not create a second hazard. Your response to someone else’s mistake must remain lawful and predictable. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- “Safe opportunity” means the complete manoeuvre—not merely the instant of pulling out—can be seen and completed without danger. [StVO §5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)
- When pressured from behind, do not speed beyond what conditions allow and do not brake sharply without a compelling reason. [StVO §3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO §4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html)
- A motorway overtaking manoeuvre already underway should be completed safely; return right as soon as a safe gap exists. [StVO §18](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)
- Take a break at a safe stopping place, never by stopping unpredictably in a live lane. [StVO §12](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

## Remember this

- **Calm beats conflict:** wait, keep distance, and let pressure pass.
- **See the whole overtake:** a bend or hidden oncoming traffic means no overtake.
- **Right lane, safe gap:** complete a motorway overtake, then return right.
- **Angry, shaken, sleepy, or distracted:** stop and recover before continuing.
- **Passengers are not a second driver:** accept safety feedback; postpone distracting conversations.
- **Young night-time groups:** reduce speed, agree conduct, and expect inexperience and distraction.
- **Never copy an illegal manoeuvre:** keep your own driving lawful and predictable.
