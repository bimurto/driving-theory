# Motorways — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Motorways (*Autobahnen*) have high speeds, grade-separated junctions, and no
normal stopping or turning. Read signs early, keep right, maintain distance, and
make every merge or exit predictable.

## Learning goals

- join and leave without disrupting through traffic;
- manage queues, breakdowns, roadworks, and speed differences; and
- use lanes, signals, hazard lights, and the emergency corridor correctly.

## Core rules

### Joining, lane choice, and overtaking

<!-- questions: 2.1.08-005-B, 2.1.08-010, 2.1.08-011, 2.1.08-013, 2.1.08-018, 2.1.08-019, 2.1.08-021, 2.1.08-022, 2.1.08-024-M, 2.1.08-025, 2.1.08-028-M, 2.1.08-029-M -->

Traffic already on the motorway has priority. Use the acceleration lane to
match a suitable gap, but do not force through traffic to brake. Keep right when
possible; overtake on the left and return when safe. Climbing lanes can contain
slow lorries and large speed differences, so scan ahead and do not change lanes
into a vehicle already moving across. [StVO §7(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html), [StVO §18(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

### Exit planning and speed

<!-- questions: 2.1.08-006-B, 2.1.08-014, 2.1.08-015, 2.1.08-017, 2.1.08-026-M, 2.1.08-027-M, 2.1.08-030-M, 2.1.08-033-M -->

Stop overtaking in good time, signal, move to the exit lane, and brake there so
that motorway traffic is not impeded. After a long high-speed drive, check the
speedometer: speed can feel lower than it is. Expect tighter bends, broken-down
vehicles, people, fallen loads, and exiting traffic on the slip road. [StVO §7(3), §18(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

### Queues and emergency corridors

<!-- questions: 2.1.08-008-B, 2.1.08-016, 2.1.08-032-M -->

When a tailback forms, switch on hazard warning lights early if possible. Create
an emergency corridor (*Rettungsgasse*): on a road with at least two lanes in one
direction, vehicles in the left lane move as far left as possible and all others
as far right as possible, leaving the corridor between them. [StVO §11(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__11.html)

### Roadworks and wrong-way driving

<!-- questions: 2.1.08-102, 2.1.08-103 -->

Do not race past roadworks for a small time saving. Yellow temporary markings,
lane shifts, and vehicles crossing them increase collision risk. Follow every
temporary sign and deliberately confirm the permitted direction at slip roads,
especially when routing changes; never enter against the stipulated direction.
 [StVO §39(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__39.html), [StVO §18(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

### Recommended speed

<!-- questions: 2.1.08-031 -->

The motorway recommended speed (*Richtgeschwindigkeit*) is 130 km/h. It is not a
blanket permission to drive at 130: conditions, traffic, vehicle, and visibility
may require less. A consistent suitable speed can improve flow and reduce both
crash likelihood and crash severity. [StVO §1 Autobahn-Richtgeschwindigkeits-V](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bab_richtgeschwv_1978/__1.html), [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- Motorways are for motor vehicles designed to travel at more than 60 km/h;
  stopping, reversing, turning, and walking on the carriageway are prohibited
  except for emergencies. [StVO §18(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)
- Do not stop on the hard shoulder except for an emergency or where instructed;
  secure a breakdown and stay away from moving traffic. [StVO §18(8)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)
- Hazard lights warn following traffic of a sudden hazard or tailback; they do
  not replace slowing and observation. [StVO §16(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

## Common exam traps

- Treating the acceleration lane as having priority over motorway traffic.
- Braking sharply before the exit instead of on the exit lane.
- Forgetting the Rettungsgasse while queueing.
- Staying beside roadworks for negligible time savings.
- Missing temporary yellow markings or changed direction signs.
- Trusting the 130-km/h recommendation despite conditions or traffic.

## Remember this

- **Join:** motorway traffic has priority; fit into a gap.
- **Exit:** signal early, brake on the exit lane, check the speedometer.
- **Queue:** hazards early; emergency corridor between left and other lanes.
- **Roadworks:** follow temporary signs and markings.
- **Motorway:** keep right, overtake left, never reverse or turn.
- **130 km/h:** recommendation, not an unconditional target.
