# Darkness and Poor Visibility — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Use lights without dazzling

<!-- questions: 2.1.04-001, 2.1.04-101, 2.1.04-102, 2.1.04-103, 2.1.04-104 -->

At dusk, in darkness, or whenever visibility requires it, use the required
lighting. Dip the main beam early when a vehicle approaches, when following
closely, or whenever traffic safety requires it; if necessary, drive slower.
Do not answer glare with your own Fernlicht. Look toward the right-hand edge
of the road while keeping your lane, and reduce speed so you can stop; with
people beside the road, stop if needed. [StVO §17(1)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

On a wet road, lights reflect more strongly. Water drops, mist, and streaks on
the windscreen scatter light and worsen glare, so dipped beam is the safe choice
when meeting traffic in rain—not high beam. [StVO §17(2)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Visibility determines speed

<!-- questions: 2.1.04-001, 2.1.04-005, 2.1.04-104, 2.1.04-105 -->

Drive only as fast as you can constantly control the vehicle and stop within the
visible distance (*übersehbare Strecke*). On a carriageway so narrow that
oncoming traffic could be endangered, you must be able to stop within at least
half the visible distance (*halbe übersehbare Strecke*). This is why dazzling,
pedestrians, and an approaching vehicle require slowing or stopping rather than
accelerating. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

In an avenue (*Allee*) with a narrow carriageway, expect conflict with oncoming
traffic. Slow, move right, or reverse to a wider place when necessary. Tree roots
can lift the edge of the carriageway, creating unevenness; do not use the edge
as if it were reliably smooth. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Animals and pedestrians in the beam

<!-- questions: 2.1.04-002, 2.1.04-103, 2.1.04-104 -->

If game (*Wild*) appears in the Fernlicht cone, hold the steering wheel firmly,
brake, dip the lights, and sound the horn. Dazzled animals may remain in the
light beam. Avoid a violent high-speed swerve into a tree or oncoming traffic;
keep control and stop if required. The duty to adapt speed and use lights follows
from the general control and lighting rules. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html), [StVO §17(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

Main beam can dazzle oncoming road users and people ahead, making pedestrians
and cyclists hard to detect. It can also dazzle wild animals so they remain
stationary. It may improve recognition of a stationary vehicle, but that benefit
does not justify dazzling traffic. [StVO §17(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Light and shade, low sun, and blocked windows

<!-- questions: 2.1.04-005, 2.1.04-106 -->

An avenue's alternating light and shade makes other road users harder to
recognise. Drive with particular care and anticipate that you may be poorly
visible. Low sun ahead can dazzle you; increase attention, use the sun visor or
sunglasses without blocking the required view. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

Do not start with a misted (*beschlagen*) windscreen or iced side windows:
clear all windows so that the traffic environment is visible. [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- If fog, snow, or rain reduces visibility to less than 50 m, do not exceed 50
  km/h, and go slower if required; this is also why the rear fog lamp
  (*Nebelschlussleuchte*) is restricted to visibility below 50 m caused by fog.
  [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html), [StVO §17(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)
- Dipped beam is required in darkness and when visibility conditions require it;
  high beam is not allowed on continuously and adequately lit roads. [StVO §17(1)–(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)
- The half-visible-distance rule applies where oncoming traffic could be
  endangered by the narrow road, not merely because it is nighttime. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

## Remember this

- **Glare:** look right, slow down, and stop if needed.
- **Oncoming traffic:** dip early; never dazzle back.
- **Wild:** firm steering, brake, dip, horn—avoid a risky swerve.
- **Narrow road:** stop within half the visible distance.
- **Visibility:** clear every window before moving; low sun and wet glass can dazzle.
- **50 m fog visibility:** maximum 50 km/h, or less when conditions demand it.
