# Overtaking — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Overtaking is a complete sequence: observe behind and ahead, decide whether the
whole route is safe, signal, pull out, pass with enough speed difference and
lateral clearance, then signal and return without cutting in. If you cannot
guarantee every part, stay behind.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- identify when overtaking is prohibited or unsafe;
- perform the observation, signalling, passing, and return sequence;
- apply statutory lateral distances to vulnerable road users;
- overtake trams and horse-drawn vehicles correctly; and
- help following vehicles pass when driving unusually slowly.

## Core rules

### Clear route, clear speed advantage, no prohibited situation

<!-- questions: 1.2.05-101, 1.2.05-105, 1.2.05-115, 1.2.05-126-M -->

Overtake only if you can see that oncoming traffic will not be obstructed during
the entire manoeuvre and you can travel **substantially faster** than the road
user being passed. Do not exceed the applicable speed limit. Overtaking is
prohibited in an unclear traffic situation or where signs forbid it.
[StVO §5(2)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

It is also prohibited at a pedestrian crossing. A continuous line
(*Zeichen 295*) may not be crossed even partly, so remain behind when the safe
lateral distance cannot fit within the permitted road space. [StVO §26(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__26.html) [StVO Annex 2,
sign 295](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Observe, signal, pull out, and return

<!-- questions: 1.2.05-103, 1.2.05-104, 1.2.05-110 -->

Before pulling out, check mirrors and the blind spot directly so following
traffic is not endangered. Confirm that the oncoming lane remains clear for the
whole pass, signal clearly and in good time, and pull out smoothly. An indicator
from the vehicle ahead is information, not an invitation to overtake.

After passing, establish a safe gap, signal the move back, and return right as
soon as possible without obstructing the person passed. Both pulling out and
returning must be announced; the overtaker must protect following traffic and
must not obstruct the person overtaken. [StVO §5(4)–(4a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### When being overtaken, stay predictable

<!-- questions: 1.2.05-106 -->

Keep as far right as safely possible and do **not** increase speed. Accelerating
is expressly prohibited because it lengthens the manoeuvre and the overtaker's
exposure to oncoming traffic. [StVO §2(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html) [StVO §5(6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Numbers and formulas

### Lateral clearance: statutory vulnerable-road-user minima

<!-- questions: 1.2.05-003, 1.2.05-004, 1.2.05-005, 1.2.05-109, 1.2.05-117, 1.2.05-124, 1.2.05-125-M -->

For a motor vehicle overtaking a pedestrian, cyclist, or e-scooter user:

| Location | Statutory minimum lateral distance |
|---|---:|
| Built-up area (*innerorts*) | **1.5 m** |
| Outside built-up area (*außerorts*) | **2.0 m** |

Leave more where a cyclist is swaying uphill, wind or surface conditions reduce
stability, or your speed creates extra risk. If the required distance cannot be
maintained, do not pass. [StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

For motorcycles, cars, lorries, buses, and trams, the statute requires
**sufficient** lateral distance but does not set one universal numerical minimum.
Choose it from the vehicle's stability, size, speed, and circumstances. A
motorcycle needs generous space because it can move laterally or fall.
[StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Situations and exceptions

### Overtaking trams

<!-- questions: 1.2.05-107, 1.2.05-108 -->

Trams are normally overtaken on the **right**. Overtake on the left only when
the rails lie so far right that right-side passing is impossible, or on a
one-way carriageway. Parked vehicles alone do not create permission to pass on
the left; wait behind when neither lawful route is available. [StVO §5(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

Separate public-transport-stop rules can require caution, walking speed, or
waiting when passengers board or alight. [StVO §20](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__20.html)

### Overtaking horse-drawn vehicles

<!-- questions: 1.2.05-119, 1.2.05-120, 1.2.05-121, 1.2.05-123 -->

Watch the driver's indicator, extended arm, or signalling paddle for a direction
change. Pass slowly and quietly with as much lateral room as practicable, and
leave ample clearance before returning. A horn or high engine speed can startle
the horse; acoustic warnings are limited to the situations in StVO §16.
[StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html) [StVO
§16(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

### If you are the slow vehicle, release the queue

<!-- questions: 1.2.05-116, 1.2.05-122 -->

If several vehicles are trapped behind your slow vehicle, reduce speed at a
suitable place and, if necessary, wait so they can pass. A suitable shoulder,
lay-by, or parking area may help where its use is lawful and safe; this does not
authorise stopping on an Autobahn. [StVO §5(6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html) [StVO §18(8)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

Do not use hazard warning lights merely to announce normal slow driving; they
are reserved for warning of danger and the other cases stated in StVO §16.
[StVO §16(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

## Dataset warning

The explanations for **1.2.05-105**, **1.2.05-109**, and **1.2.05-117** present
non-statutory numerical shortcuts as general legal minima. Current StVO §5 says
the overtaker must be travelling “substantially faster” but does not prescribe a
general **20 km/h** difference. It specifies **1.5 m innerorts / 2 m außerorts**
only for pedestrians, cyclists, and e-scooter users; for motorcycles and other
vehicles it requires sufficient distance without a universal 1 m or 1.5 m
number. [StVO §5(2), (4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Common exam traps

- Checking only ahead and forgetting a vehicle already overtaking from behind.
- Treating another driver's right indicator as permission to pass.
- Using a small speed advantage and occupying the oncoming lane too long.
- Exceeding the speed limit to finish the manoeuvre.
- Cutting back before a safe gap exists.
- Applying 1.5 m outside built-up areas instead of 2 m.
- Treating a statutory minimum as enough for a visibly unstable cyclist.
- Overtaking a tram on the left merely because cars block its right side.
- Frightening a horse with horn or engine noise.

## Remember this

- **Whole route unclear:** stay behind.
- **Pull out:** mirror, shoulder check, clear route, signal.
- **Return:** safe gap, signal, do not cut in.
- **Being overtaken:** keep right; never accelerate.
- **Pedestrian/cycle/e-scooter:** 1.5 m innerorts, 2 m außerorts.
- **Tram:** normally pass right; limited exceptions for the left.
- **Slow vehicle with queue:** facilitate passing at a suitable safe place.
