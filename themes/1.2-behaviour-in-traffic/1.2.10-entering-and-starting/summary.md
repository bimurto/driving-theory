# Entering and Starting — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Entering flowing traffic from a property, petrol station, parking space,
traffic-calmed area, other road area, or across a lowered kerb carries an
especially strict duty: danger to every other road user must be excluded. You
wait; *right before left* does not give you priority.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- identify entry situations governed by StVO §10;
- protect pedestrians and cyclists before crossing their path;
- observe and signal before moving from the road edge; and
- use a guide where the view is insufficient.

## Core rules

### Property, petrol station, and lowered kerb: you wait

<!-- questions: 1.2.10-005, 1.2.10-006-M, 1.2.10-106, 1.2.10-107, 1.2.10-108 -->

When entering the road from a property or petrol station, from another part of
the road, or over a lowered kerb (*abgesenkter Bordstein*), behave so that danger
to other road users is excluded. Yield to vehicles on the road and to pedestrians
or cyclists whose pavement or cycle path you cross. *Rechts vor links* does not
override this entry duty. [StVO §10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__10.html)

Proceed only after observing both directions, checking the pavement and cycle
path in both directions, and confirming that the target lane is clear. If the
view is inadequate, obtain guidance from another person. A pedestrian may
clearly waive priority, but do not rely on an ambiguous gesture; move only after
communication is understood and nobody is endangered. [StVO §10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__10.html) [StVO §11(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__11.html)

A compulsory-direction sign at the exit may restrict you to turning right or
left, but it does not change the duty to yield while entering. Traffic signs
take precedence over general rules. [StVO §39(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__39.html)

### Leaving a traffic-calmed area is also an entry manoeuvre

<!-- questions: 1.2.10-104, 1.2.10-105 -->

When leaving a traffic-calmed area (*verkehrsberuhigter Bereich*, signs 325.1/
325.2), apply StVO §10: wait for pedestrians, cyclists, and flowing traffic,
then signal your intended direction and enter only without danger. The usual
right-before-left rule does not give the exiting vehicle priority. [StVO Annex 3,
sign 325.2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html) [StVO §10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__10.html)

### Starting from the road edge or a parking space

<!-- questions: 1.2.10-101, 1.2.10-109 -->

Before moving from the carriageway edge or a parking space into flowing traffic:

1. check interior and exterior mirrors;
2. signal clearly and early;
3. check the blind spot directly for cyclists, e-scooters, motorcycles, and cars;
4. wait for a sufficient gap; and
5. move out smoothly without forcing anyone to brake or swerve.

StVO §10 requires danger to others to be excluded and requires the intention to
enter or start to be announced with the indicator. [StVO §10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__10.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- **Lowered kerb:** the vehicle crossing it waits, even if it comes from the right.
- **Footway/cycle path:** check both directions before crossing.
- **Guide:** use one if buildings, gates, parked vehicles, or vegetation prevent
  adequate observation.
- **Waiver:** rely on it only after unmistakable communication; still exclude danger.
- **Indicator:** communicates intention but never creates priority.

## Common exam traps

- Applying *rechts vor links* at a property exit or lowered kerb.
- Watching motor vehicles but overlooking a pedestrian on the pavement.
- Checking a cycle path in only the expected direction.
- Treating a compulsory-right sign as permission to enter immediately.
- Signalling and assuming flowing traffic must make a gap.
- Moving from a parking space with mirrors only and no shoulder check.
- Relying on an uncertain wave as a safe waiver.

## Remember this

- **Property / petrol station / parking / traffic-calmed exit / lowered kerb:** you wait.
- **Standard:** exclude danger to every other road user.
- **Across pavement or cycle path:** pedestrians and cyclists first.
- **Starting:** mirrors, signal, blind-spot check, safe gap.
- **Poor view:** obtain guidance.
