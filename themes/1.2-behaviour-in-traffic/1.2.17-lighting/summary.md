# Lighting — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Lights must be approved, secure, ready, and clean

Only prescribed or expressly permitted lighting equipment may be fitted. It
must be installed securely and as required and remain ready for operation.
[StVZO §49a(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__49a.html)

Before driving in darkness or poor visibility, check dipped and main beam,
position and tail lights, brake lights, indicators, number-plate lighting, and
any required fog lights. Clean lenses and do not let dirt, snow, a load, or an
accessory cover them. StVO expressly requires the prescribed lights in darkness,
twilight, or visibility that demands them, and says they may not be covered or
dirty. [StVO §17(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Dipped beam can dazzle when aim changes

Dipped headlights may dazzle when:

- their vertical aim is set too high;
- a bulb is inserted incorrectly;
- a repair or replacement changes alignment; or
- a heavy rear load lowers the back of the vehicle and raises the beam.

Headlights must be adjustable and secured against unintended movement, while
dipped-beam intensity above the defined cut-off is legally limited. [StVZO
§50(3), (6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__50.html)

### Use load-level adjustment and approved light sources

Use the headlamp range control (*Leuchtweitenregulierung*) according to vehicle
load, following the vehicle handbook. After headlamp work or suspected impact,
have the setting checked. Use only the light source designed and approved for
that lamp; the wrong bulb type or seating can destroy the beam pattern.
[StVZO §49a(6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__49a.html) [StVZO
§50(8)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__50.html)

If oncoming drivers flash repeatedly, do not assume they are mistaken. Dip main
beam if relevant, reduce speed if your view is affected, and check load setting
and headlamp aim at the next safe opportunity.

## Situations and exceptions

- Automatic self-levelling systems reduce manual work but do not remove the
  duty to notice a defect, dirty lens, or wrongly installed light source.
- A working bulb is not enough: colour, approval, fit, alignment, symmetry, and
  beam pattern must also be correct.
- Cleaning the lens cannot correct a headlamp that is aimed too high.
- Load adjustment is temporary; incorrect basic alignment needs proper repair.

## Common exam traps

- Checking only whether the lamp illuminates.
- Assuming dipped beam can never dazzle.
- Forgetting to lower beam aim after loading the boot or rear seats heavily.
- Installing a bulb that fits physically but is not approved for the lamp.
- Skipping an aim check after headlamp repair.
- Leaving snow, dirt, or luggage over required lighting equipment.

## Remember this

- **Lighting:** approved, correctly fitted, operational, clean, and unobstructed.
- **Rear-heavy load:** beam rises; adjust headlamp range.
- **Bulb replacement:** approved type, correctly seated.
- **Repair:** check headlamp alignment afterward.
- **Dipped beam:** can still dazzle if aim, bulb, or load is wrong.
