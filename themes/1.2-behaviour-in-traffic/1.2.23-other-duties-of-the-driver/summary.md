# Other Duties of the Driver — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Driving duties begin before the vehicle moves. The driver must check that the
vehicle is safe to operate, react correctly if a dangerous defect develops,
avoid distraction, and use only a vehicle that has the required liability
insurance. The vehicle keeper (*Halter*) also has independent safety duties.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- distinguish the responsibilities of the driver and vehicle keeper;
- respond correctly when a vehicle becomes unsafe during a journey;
- recognise safety-critical tyre, lighting, and brake defects;
- apply the current rules for electronic devices; and
- state the roadworthiness and insurance conditions for public-road use.

## Core rules

### Driver and keeper both carry responsibility

<!-- questions: 1.2.23-001 -->

The **driver** (*Fahrzeugführer*) must ensure that the vehicle, combination,
load, and occupants comply with the rules and that traffic safety is not
impaired. The **keeper** (*Halter*) must not order or permit a journey when they
know, or should know, that the vehicle is unsafe or non-compliant. Liability
insurance does not perform these safety checks. [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html) [StVZO §31(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__31.html)

The keeper is also responsible for presenting the vehicle for its periodic
main inspection (*Hauptuntersuchung*, HU) on time. Passing an HU does not remove
the driver's duty to check the vehicle before and during use. [StVZO §29(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__29.html) [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### If safety is materially impaired, leave traffic by the shortest route

<!-- questions: 1.2.23-002 -->

If a defect arises during the journey, remedy it immediately where possible.
If that is not possible and the defect materially impairs road safety, take the
vehicle out of traffic by the **shortest route**. Do not treat “the next dealer”
as permission to continue a normal journey; normal driving may resume only once
the safety defect has been repaired. [StVO §23(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### Tyres, rear lights, and brakes are all safety-critical

<!-- questions: 1.2.23-003 -->

- **Worn tyres** reduce available grip and increase the risk of skidding; the
  prescribed main grooves must have at least 1.6 mm tread depth over the main
  tread area. [StVZO §36(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__36.html)
- **Defective rear lamps** make the vehicle's position and actions harder for
  following traffic to recognise. Required lighting equipment must remain
  properly fitted and continuously ready for operation. [StVZO §49a(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__49a.html)
- **Insufficient braking effect** lengthens stopping and can make collision
  avoidance impossible. Motor vehicles must have compliant, effective braking
  systems. [StVZO §41](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__41.html)

Any of these defects can endanger road traffic; do not select only the most
obvious mechanical defect. The driver must ensure that prescribed lighting is
ready and that the vehicle's condition does not impair traffic safety.
[StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### A call and the device operation can both distract

<!-- questions: 1.2.23-006 -->

The conversation itself can divert attention, and operating the telephone adds
visual and manual distraction. A hands-free system (*Freisprecheinrichtung*)
removes the need to hold the phone, but it does **not** remove cognitive
distraction. The legal permission to use a device never overrides the duty to
keep sight, hearing, and attention adequate for safe driving. [StVO §23(1), (1a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### Electronic-device use is device-neutral: do not hold it

<!-- questions: 1.2.23-007 -->

While driving, an electronic device may be used only when it is **not picked up
or held** and either voice control/read-out is used or only a brief glance,
adapted to the traffic, visibility, weather, and road conditions, is required.
Walking pace does not create an exception. [StVO §23(1a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

The rule does not apply while the vehicle is stationary and its engine is
**fully switched off**. An engine stopped automatically by a stop-start system,
or an electric drive merely resting, does not count as fully switched off.
Choose a suitable safe place before stopping to use a hand-held phone.
[StVO §23(1b)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### Public-road use requires safety and liability cover

<!-- questions: 1.2.23-008 -->

Before using a motor vehicle on a public road, ensure it is roadworthy
(*verkehrssicher*) and that the required motor-vehicle liability insurance
(*Kfz-Haftpflichtversicherung*) is in force. You do not have to own the vehicle,
but the keeper must maintain insurance covering the keeper, owner, driver, and
other protected users. [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html) [PflVG §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/pflvg/__1.html)

## Dataset warning

The explanation for **1.2.23-007** describes a hands-free unit as the decisive
condition while driving. That is incomplete under current StVO §23(1a): the
rule applies to electronic devices generally, forbids picking up or holding the
device, and additionally permits only voice/read-out use or a brief,
condition-adapted glance. The stationary exception requires the engine to be
fully switched off; automatic stop-start and a resting electric drive are not
enough. [StVO §23(1a), (1b)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

## Common exam traps

- Naming only the driver or only the keeper as responsible for vehicle safety.
- Assuming a current HU sticker replaces the driver's ongoing checks.
- Continuing to a preferred workshop despite a materially dangerous defect.
- Treating faulty rear lamps as minor because the vehicle can still move.
- Believing a hands-free call cannot distract.
- Believing holding a phone becomes legal at walking pace or at a red light.
- Confusing legal permission to drive someone else's vehicle with permission to
  use an uninsured vehicle.

## Remember this

- **Driver checks; keeper must not permit unsafe use.**
- **Dangerous defect:** remedy it or take the shortest route out of traffic.
- **Tyres, rear lights, brakes:** every one can endanger traffic.
- **Phone:** the call and the controls both distract.
- **Device:** do not pick it up or hold it; keep any glance brief.
- **Stationary exception:** engine fully off—not merely auto-stopped.
- **Public road:** roadworthy vehicle plus liability insurance.
