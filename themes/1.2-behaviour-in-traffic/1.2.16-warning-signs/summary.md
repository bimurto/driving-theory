# Warning Signals — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Hazard warning lights mean: expect an immediate hazard

Hazard warning lights may indicate a broken-down vehicle, a vehicle combination
being towed, a queue ahead, a very slow vehicle on a fast road, or a scheduled
service/school bus at a stop where their use is ordered. Reduce speed, increase
attention, and be ready for people, a tow rope or bar, a stopped vehicle, or
traffic braking suddenly. [StVO §16(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html) [StVO §15a(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15a.html)

Hazards on scheduled-service or marked school buses trigger separate rules: a
bus approaching its stop with hazards may not be overtaken; once stopped there,
pass only at walking speed under the passenger-protection conditions.
[StVO §20(3)–(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__20.html)

### Horn and headlight flash have limited purposes

Use an acoustic or light warning when you see yourself or another person in
danger. Outside built-up areas, you may also briefly announce an overtaking
manoeuvre with horn or headlight flash. Inside built-up areas, overtaking alone
does not permit horn use; there must be danger. Never use warning signals to
greet, express annoyance, or demand priority. [StVO §16(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

When flashing main beam to announce an overtake outside a built-up area, do not
dazzle oncoming traffic. [StVO §5(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Situations and exceptions

### Breakdown in a tunnel

Switch on hazard warning lights, move into an emergency or breakdown bay if
possible, stop the engine, and follow tunnel emergency signs and instructions.
Sign 327 says an available emergency bay should be used for an emergency or
breakdown; sign 328 reserves such bays for those cases. [StVO Annex 3,
signs 327–328](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

### Approaching a tunnel queue

Switch on hazard warning lights when approaching the end of a queue so following
drivers recognise the sudden danger. Reduce speed smoothly, preserve distance,
and stop the engine if traffic remains stationary. Do not turn around: U-turns
are prohibited in a tunnel. [StVO §16(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html) [StVO Annex 3,
sign 327](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

## Common exam traps

- Treating hazard warning lights as permission to stop anywhere.
- Passing a hazard-light bus at normal speed.
- Honking inside a built-up area merely to announce overtaking.
- Using the horn to greet, hurry, or reprimand someone.
- Flashing main beam while dazzling oncoming traffic.
- Stopping a tunnel breakdown in a running lane despite a reachable bay.
- Turning around when encountering a tunnel queue.

## Remember this

- **Hazards seen:** slow down and prepare for a hidden danger.
- **Horn/light warning:** danger anywhere; overtaking announcement only outside towns.
- **Bus approaching stop with hazards:** no overtaking.
- **Bus stopped with hazards:** walking speed and passenger protection.
- **Tunnel breakdown:** hazards, emergency bay if possible, engine off.
- **Tunnel queue:** hazards, controlled stop, no U-turn.
