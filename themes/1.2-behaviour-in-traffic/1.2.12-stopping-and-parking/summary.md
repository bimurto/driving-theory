# Stopping and Parking — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### When stopping becomes parking

You are parking if you leave the vehicle or stop for **more than three minutes**.
A traffic-forced wait—at a red signal, barrier, or in a queue—is not voluntary
stopping or parking under these definitions. [StVO §12(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

Leaving means no longer being able to intervene immediately, not simply opening
the door. For exam purposes, remember: **leave it or exceed three minutes =
parking**.

### Absolute stopping prohibitions

Stopping is prohibited at narrow or unclear road locations, in sharp bends, on
acceleration and deceleration lanes, on level crossings, and in front of
officially marked fire-service access roads. [StVO §12(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

Other sign and marking rules add prohibitions:

- at a taxi rank (**Zeichen 229**), only taxis held ready for passenger service
  may stop; [StVO Annex 2, sign 229](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)
- stopping is prohibited on a section marked with direction arrows
  (**Zeichen 297**); [StVO Annex 2, sign 297](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)
- where a suitable right shoulder exists, use it for stopping rather than
  stopping on the carriageway; [StVO §12(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)
- on Autobahns, stopping is prohibited even on the shoulder. [StVO §18(8)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

### Pedestrian crossings: keep the approach visible

Do not stop **on** a marked pedestrian crossing or within **5 m before** it in
your direction of travel. The clear approach allows drivers and pedestrians to
see one another. The special marking rule does not create the same fixed 5 m
ban after the crossing, although another rule may still prohibit stopping there.
[StVO Annex 2, sign 293](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Parking has additional prohibitions

Do not park if your vehicle prevents use of marked parking spaces, in front of a
lowered kerb, or at other places listed in §12(3). Outside built-up areas, do not
park on the carriageway of a priority road; use a lawful shoulder, bay, or car
park instead. [StVO §12(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html) [StVO Annex 3,
sign 306](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

### Use the right shoulder or parking lane

For parking, use the sufficiently paved right shoulder—including a parking
lane—when one exists; otherwise move to the right edge of the carriageway. This
normally applies to stopping too. Do not stop to the carriageway side of a
continuous boundary line when a usable shoulder is available beyond it.
[StVO §12(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html) [StVO Annex 2,
sign 295](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

Rails on the right are an exception: parking must not block the operating space
of rail vehicles, so left-side stopping/parking may be necessary where lawful.
[StVO §12(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

### Left-side parking is exceptional

Normally stop and park on the right. Left-side parking is allowed in a one-way
street and where rails lie on the right, subject to all other restrictions. In
a one-way street with suitable shoulders on both sides, either side may be used.
[StVO §12(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

## Numbers and formulas

### Junction and intersection distances

Measure from the intersection points of the carriageway edges:

- normally, parking is prohibited within **5 m before and 5 m after** a
  crossroads or junction;
- if a cycle path is structurally built alongside the right of the carriageway,
  the ban extends to **8 m before** the crossroads or junction, while the normal
  **5 m after** remains.

The extended approach distance protects visibility of cyclists. [StVO
§12(3)(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

### St Andrew's Cross distances

Parking is prohibited before and after **Zeichen 201 Andreaskreuz** within:

| Location | Distance on each side |
|---|---:|
| Built-up area | **5 m** |
| Outside built-up area | **50 m** |

The cross must also not be hidden by stopping within 10 m in front if the
vehicle would obscure it. [StVO Annex 2, sign 201](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Public-transport stop distances

Parking is prohibited within **15 m before and after** sign 224. A boundary
marking (*Grenzmarkierung*, Zeichen 299—the zigzag line) can extend, shorten, or
identify the ban. Stopping for at most three minutes may be possible only if it
does not obstruct buses, trams, passengers, or traffic and no other rule forbids
it. [StVO Annex 2, signs 224 and 299](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

## Situations and exceptions

### Taxi second-row exception

A taxi may let passengers board or alight alongside vehicles stopped or parked
at the shoulder or road edge if traffic conditions permit. This is a limited
passenger-service exception, not general second-row parking and not permission
created by hazard warning lights. [StVO §12(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

## Dataset warning

The explanations for **1.2.12-129** and **1.2.12-130** state an 8 m prohibition
both before and after a junction where a cycle path runs along the right. Current
StVO §12(3)(1) extends the distance to **8 m only before** the junction; after it,
the ordinary **5 m** distance applies. Question **1.2.12-133**, which asks only
for the distance before the junction, is correctly covered by 8 m. [StVO
§12(3)(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

## Common exam traps

- Calling any traffic-forced wait “parking” after three minutes.
- Forgetting that leaving the vehicle makes it parking immediately.
- Stopping on the carriageway despite a suitable right shoulder.
- Applying the pedestrian-crossing 5 m rule behind the crossing as well.
- Parking 5 m before a junction despite a right-side cycle path: use 8 m.
- Applying 8 m after that junction: current law uses 5 m after.
- Confusing the 5 m/50 m St Andrew's Cross rule by location.
- Treating hazard lights as permission for second-row stopping.

## Remember this

- **Parking:** leave vehicle or stop longer than 3 minutes.
- **Never stop:** narrow/unclear place, sharp bend, merge/diverge lane, level
  crossing, marked fire access.
- **Zebra crossing:** on it and 5 m before are clear.
- **Junction:** 5 m both sides; with right cycle path, 8 m before / 5 m after.
- **Andreaskreuz:** 5 m innerorts / 50 m außerorts before and after.
- **Stop sign 224:** no parking 15 m before or after.
- **Parking side:** normally right; one-way street and right-side rails are exceptions.
