# Use of Lanes by Motor Vehicles — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

A lane change is permitted only when danger to other traffic is excluded. When
a lane ends or an obstacle blocks it, Germany's zipper rule uses both lanes up
to the narrowing and merges vehicles alternately at that point. Predictability,
observation, and correct timing matter more than changing early.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- prepare and signal a safe lane change;
- apply the zipper merge (*Reißverschlussverfahren*) from both lanes;
- identify where zipper merging does not apply;
- obey tunnel lines and the U-turn prohibition; and
- abandon a desired turn rather than blocking another lane.

## Core rules

### Lane changes require an excluded danger

<!-- questions: 1.2.07-001 -->

Before changing lanes, check mirrors, look directly into the blind spot, assess
the speed and distance of following traffic in the target lane, and wait for a
sufficient gap. Signal clearly and in good time, but remember that indicating
does not create priority. Avoid abrupt braking or sideways movement.

Legally, a lane change is allowed only when danger to other road users is
excluded and must be announced clearly and early with the indicator.
[StVO §7(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

### Use the ending lane until the narrowing

<!-- questions: 1.2.07-103, 1.2.07-105-M, 1.2.07-112, 1.2.07-115 -->

When one of several same-direction lanes ends, or an obstacle prevents it from
continuing, drive along the usable ending lane up to immediately before the
narrowing. Then merge into the adjacent through lane. Passing slower traffic in
the through lane while lawfully continuing toward the merge point is part of
using all available road space; do it at a safe speed and do not force entry.

Moving over hundreds of metres early wastes capacity and creates a longer
single queue. StVO requires the transition immediately before the narrowing,
alternating one vehicle from each lane. [StVO §7(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

The zipper rule does **not** apply merely because an acceleration lane at an
Autobahn entrance ends. Traffic on the continuous Autobahn carriageway has
priority, so the entering driver must find a safe gap. [StVO §18(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

### Through-lane drivers must admit vehicles alternately

<!-- questions: 1.2.07-113 -->

If you are on the continuing lane, keep a suitable gap and admit **one** vehicle
from the ending or blocked lane immediately before the narrowing. Then continue
behind the vehicle ahead. Alternation is a duty, not optional courtesy.
[StVO §7(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

### Do not block a lane to rescue a missed turn

<!-- questions: 1.2.07-116 -->

If the right-turn lane is blocked by a queue and entering it would leave you
standing across your present lane or obstructing others, remain in your lane
and continue in its permitted direction. Reroute later. StVO §1 prohibits more
than unavoidable obstruction, and directional lane markings must be obeyed.
[StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html) [StVO Annex 2,
sign 297](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

## Situations and exceptions

### Tunnel with oncoming traffic

<!-- questions: 1.2.07-104 -->

Keep right, remain within your lane, and do not cross a continuous boundary
line. Do not make a U-turn in a tunnel. Sign 327 expressly requires dipped
headlights and prohibits U-turning; sign 295 prohibits even partial crossing of
a continuous line. [StVO Annex 3, sign 327](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html) [StVO Annex 2,
sign 295](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

Use a safe following distance, avoid staring into oncoming headlights, and obey
any separate overtaking restriction.

## Common exam traps

- Treating the indicator as a right to enter the next lane.
- Omitting the direct blind-spot check.
- Merging early because the ending lane feels impolite to use.
- Blocking vehicles that correctly continue to the merge point.
- Letting several vehicles enter instead of alternating predictably.
- Applying zipper merging to an Autobahn acceleration lane.
- Blocking a through lane while waiting to reach a desired turn lane.
- Crossing a tunnel's continuous line or attempting a U-turn.

## Remember this

- **Lane change:** exclude danger, then signal and move.
- **Ending/blocked lane:** use it to just before the narrowing.
- **At narrowing:** merge one-by-one in alternation.
- **Autobahn entry:** no zipper entitlement; through traffic has priority.
- **Missed turn:** continue and reroute rather than obstructing a lane.
- **Tunnel:** stay within markings; no U-turn.
