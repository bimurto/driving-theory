# Load — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Secure against every emergency movement

Use suitable restraints and load-securing equipment so the load cannot slide,
tip, roll, fall, or create avoidable noise—even under full braking or a sudden
evasive manoeuvre. Follow recognised engineering practice. Do not let the load
block your view, controls, mirrors, lights, or registration plate.
[StVO §22(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html) [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

Check the registration and vehicle documentation for permissible gross mass,
axle loads, roof load, and attachment limits. Distribute mass so no axle limit
is exceeded; a total below the gross limit can still overload one axle.

### Overloading harms control and components

Even a 20% overload can overstrain brakes and load-bearing components, degrade
steering, increase stopping distance, and alter the centre of gravity. Legal
mass and axle-load limits are maximum values, not approximate targets. Vehicle
operation must not damage the road or endanger anyone, and the driver's view,
hearing, and vehicle condition must remain safe. [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### Orange panels identify regulated dangerous-goods transport

Orange rectangular panels indicate a transport unit carrying dangerous goods
in a quantity or form requiring ADR identification. They alert other road users
and provide responders with hazard information; do not confuse them with the red
marker for an ordinary projecting load. German dangerous-goods law incorporates
the ADR orange-panel requirements. [GGVSEB §19(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ggvseb/__19.html)

## Numbers and formulas

### Rear projection and marking

A load may project up to **1 m beyond the rear reflectors** without the special
projection marker. Once the outer end projects **more than 1 m**, mark it with
at least one prescribed bright-red flag, sign, or cylindrical body. When
lighting is required, add a red light at the same location and a red reflector
no higher than 90 cm above the road. [StVO §22(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)

The general rear projection limit is **1.5 m**; for a journey up to 100 km it may
be up to **3 m**, while vehicle/train plus load may not exceed 20.75 m. The
marking threshold remains more than 1 m. [StVO §22(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)

### Forward projection

Up to a height of **2.50 m**, a load may not project forward beyond the vehicle
(or beyond the towing vehicle in a combination). **Above 2.50 m**, it may project
forward by at most **50 cm**. [StVO §22(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)

## Dataset warning

The explanation for **1.2.22-108** reverses the height condition by suggesting
that projection is permitted up to a vehicle height of 2.5 m. Current StVO
§22(3) says the opposite: no forward projection **up to** 2.50 m height; the
maximum 50 cm projection is allowed only **above** that height. [StVO §22(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)

## Common exam traps

- Checking only total mass and overlooking an overloaded axle.
- Securing for normal driving rather than full braking and sudden avoidance.
- Letting luggage interfere with mirrors, controls, lights, or the driver.
- Marking a projection only when it exceeds the 1.5 m general limit; marking
  starts once it exceeds 1 m beyond the rear reflectors.
- Using only a red flag in darkness and forgetting light plus reflector.
- Allowing a forward projection below 2.50 m height.
- Confusing orange dangerous-goods panels with projecting-load markers.

## Remember this

- **Secure:** full braking, sudden swerve, no slide/tip/roll/fall.
- **Weights:** total mass and every axle load must remain legal.
- **Rear marker:** more than 1 m beyond rear reflectors.
- **Darkness:** red light plus red reflector at the projecting end.
- **Forward:** none up to 2.50 m height; max 50 cm above it.
- **Orange panel:** regulated dangerous-goods transport.
