# Passing — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Passing a stationary obstruction (*Vorbeifahren*) is not the same as overtaking
a moving road user. The central rule is positional: if the obstruction is on
your side and you must move left around it, let oncoming traffic pass unless
signs regulate priority differently.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- identify which direction normally waits at a stationary obstruction;
- recognise the sign-controlled exception;
- observe and signal before moving around the obstruction; and
- preserve safe clearance and account for hidden pedestrians.

## Core rules

### Obstacle on your side: let oncoming traffic through

<!-- questions: 1.2.06-001, 1.2.06-002-B, 1.2.06-101-M -->

If roadworks, a barrier, a parked vehicle, or another obstruction is on your
side and you need to pass it on the left, wait for oncoming traffic. This applies
regardless of whether the oncoming road user is a car, motorcycle, cyclist, or
larger vehicle. [StVO §6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__6.html)

The exception is when signs regulate the narrow section differently:
**Zeichen 208** requires yielding to oncoming traffic, while **Zeichen 308**
gives priority over it. Even with priority, proceed only if avoidable danger is
excluded. [StVO §6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__6.html) [StVO Annex 2,
sign 208](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO Annex 3,
sign 308](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

### Do not enter an unseen oncoming path

<!-- questions: 1.2.06-006 -->

If a van, barrier, or works area hides the road beyond, wait until you can
confirm that no oncoming traffic is approaching and that the whole pass can be
completed safely. Speed must let you stop within the distance you can see.
[StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

Before pulling left, check following traffic, signal the move out clearly, and
signal the return. These observation and signalling duties are expressly part
of StVO §6. [StVO §6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__6.html)

### Space must remain for the oncoming cyclist

<!-- questions: 1.2.06-005 -->

Do not squeeze between parked vehicles and an oncoming cyclist. Wait until the
cyclist passes if the remaining width cannot provide safe lateral clearance.
The fixed 1.5 m/2 m figures in StVO §5 apply when a motor vehicle **overtakes** a
cyclist; in this meeting situation the general duties still require sufficient
space and no avoidable danger. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html) [StVO §6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__6.html)

### A large obstruction can hide people

<!-- questions: 1.2.06-004 -->

A stopped lorry both narrows the road and hides pedestrians who may cross in
front of it. Approach very slowly, search below and beyond the vehicle where
possible, and remain ready to stop. The pedestrian's view may be blocked too.
Your speed must reflect road, traffic, and visibility conditions and preserve a
visible stopping distance. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- **Unsigned obstruction on your side:** you normally wait.
- **Signs 208/308:** follow the signed priority direction.
- **Both vehicles already committed:** cooperate under StVO §1 rather than
  insisting on a positional rule that can no longer be applied safely.
- **Hidden route:** do not edge into the oncoming path without a complete view.
- **Cyclist or motorcycle:** width matters; wait rather than squeeze past.

## Common exam traps

- Calling passing a parked vehicle “overtaking” and applying the wrong rule.
- Assuming the larger oncoming vehicle must wait.
- Moving out without checking traffic behind.
- Forgetting to signal both moving out and returning.
- Entering because no oncoming vehicle is currently visible behind the van.
- Watching the obstruction but missing a pedestrian in front of it.
- Forcing an oncoming cyclist into an unsafe gap.

## Remember this

- **Obstacle on your side:** let oncoming traffic pass.
- **Signs 208/308:** signed priority overrides the default.
- **Before moving out:** look behind, signal, confirm the entire route is clear.
- **Hidden lorry/van:** expect pedestrians and oncoming traffic.
- **Insufficient width:** wait.
