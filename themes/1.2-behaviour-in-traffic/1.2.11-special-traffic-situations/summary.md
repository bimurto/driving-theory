# Special Traffic Situations — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Priority and a green light permit movement only when space is actually
available. In congestion, keep conflict areas clear; at narrowings, protect
oncoming traffic and cyclists; and around large vehicles, wait outside their
swept path. A safe driver solves the real situation, not merely the signal.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- keep junctions, crossings, and level crossings clear in congestion;
- apply signed priority at a narrowing;
- respect a marked convoy as one unit;
- wait behind cyclists where safe lateral space is unavailable;
- interpret a clear priority waiver; and
- position safely around trams and turning lorries.

## Core rules

### Green or priority does not allow you to block a junction

<!-- questions: 1.2.11-003, 1.2.11-004, 1.2.11-006, 1.2.11-108, 1.2.11-109, 1.2.11-112 -->

If traffic is queued, do not enter a crossroads or junction—even with green or
priority—when you would have to wait inside it. Stop before the line or conflict
area until there is enough room beyond to clear it completely. [StVO §11(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__11.html)

Also keep marked pedestrian crossings clear; queued traffic may not enter if it
would wait on the crossing. Do not queue on a level crossing, and enter only
when it can be crossed without delay. [StVO §26(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__26.html) [StVO §19(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__19.html)

Police signals and instructions take precedence over traffic lights, signs, and
ordinary priority rules. If a blue-light emergency vehicle using the siren
approaches, all other road users must immediately make a clear path.
[StVO §36(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__36.html) [StVO
§38(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__38.html)

### Signed narrowing: obey the arrows and stay off the pavement

<!-- questions: 1.2.11-005-B -->

At **Zeichen 208**, the red arrow indicates that your direction must yield to
oncoming traffic. Reduce speed and stop if necessary. Do not escape onto the
pavement: vehicle traffic must use the carriageway, and a pavement is reserved
for pedestrians unless a sign expressly permits another use. [StVO Annex 2,
sign 208](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO §2(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html)

### Do not break through a marked convoy

<!-- questions: 1.2.11-007-M -->

A clearly recognisable closed formation (*geschlossener Verband*) is treated as
one traffic unit. Do not interrupt it. Wait at the stop line until the entire
marked convoy has passed, not merely until a gap appears between its vehicles.
[StVO §27(1), (3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__27.html)

### Cyclists need room at narrowings

<!-- questions: 1.2.11-103-M, 1.2.11-105, 1.2.11-107, 1.2.11-110-M -->

Expect a cyclist to move left around an obstacle or as a lane narrows, even
without a hand signal. Slow down and remain behind if oncoming traffic prevents
the statutory overtaking clearance: at least **1.5 m inside built-up areas** and
**2 m outside** when a motor vehicle overtakes a cyclist. [StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

Do not use a right-hand lane or gap to bypass a queue near a junction unless a
safe, lawful lane change is possible. Any lane change is permitted only when
danger to other traffic is excluded and must be signalled clearly and early.
[StVO §7(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

### Wait outside a tram or lorry's path

<!-- questions: 1.2.11-104, 1.2.11-111, 1.2.11-201-M, 1.2.11-202-M -->

If tram rails cross your left-turn waiting area, stop before the rails and leave
the tram's path clear. A turning refuse lorry or other large vehicle may swing
into your lane; brake, stay behind it—on the right where appropriate—and wait
until the turn is complete. Rail vehicles must be allowed through where your
movement would obstruct them. [StVO §2(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html) [StVO §9(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)

If the large vehicle appears about to move into you, wait and use an acoustic or
light warning only if danger makes it necessary. [StVO §16(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

## Situations and exceptions

### A priority waiver must be unmistakable

<!-- questions: 1.2.11-113-M, 1.2.11-114-M -->

You may rely on another driver's waiver of priority only after clear
communication. A distinct hand signal can establish this; headlight flashing
alone can be ambiguous and may be intended as a warning. Even after a clear
waiver, proceed only if cross traffic is not endangered or substantially
obstructed. [StVO §11(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__11.html)

## Common exam traps

- Entering on green without space beyond the junction.
- Stopping on a zebra crossing or level crossing in a queue.
- Following a traffic light instead of a police officer's instruction.
- Driving onto the pavement to solve a signed narrowing.
- Entering a gap inside a clearly marked convoy.
- Squeezing past a cyclist because the lane is almost wide enough.
- Waiting on tram rails while trying to turn left.
- Assuming a headlight flash always waives priority.
- Driving immediately after one vehicle yields without checking cross traffic.

## Remember this

- **Blocked exit:** do not enter the junction, even with green or priority.
- **Crossing/level crossing:** never queue on it.
- **Sign 208:** your direction waits.
- **Convoy:** treat the marked formation as one unit.
- **Cyclist + narrowing:** stay behind if safe passing clearance will not fit.
- **Tram/turning lorry:** wait outside its path.
- **Waiver:** trust only clear communication, then recheck everyone else.
