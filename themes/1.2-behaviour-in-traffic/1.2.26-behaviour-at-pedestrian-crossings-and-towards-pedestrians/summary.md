# Behaviour at Pedestrian Crossings and Towards Pedestrians — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Approach at moderate speed and be ready to wait

When a pedestrian, wheelchair user, or mobility-scooter user visibly intends to
use a zebra crossing, enable them to cross. Approach only at **moderate speed**
(*mäßige Geschwindigkeit*) and wait if necessary. Watch closely, reduce speed
early, and be ready to stop—especially when someone is approaching quickly, the
view is restricted, or another vehicle is already waiting. [StVO §26(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__26.html)

The duty still applies when snow hides the stripes but sign 350 identifies the
crossing. Brake progressively rather than suddenly, both to show the pedestrian
that you are yielding and to reduce the risk of a rear-end collision. Continuous
caution and consideration remain mandatory. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html) [StVO Annex 3, sign 350](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

### Do not overtake at a pedestrian crossing

Overtaking is prohibited **at pedestrian crossings**. Do not pass a cyclist,
car, or truck in this area, including on a second lane of a one-way street. A
stopped vehicle may be hiding a person on the crossing; approach moderately and
ready to stop instead. [StVO §26(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__26.html)

### Keep the crossing clear when traffic queues

Do not enter a zebra crossing when congestion beyond it would force you to wait
on the markings. Stop on the approach side, leave the whole crossing free, and
move only when there is enough space beyond it. This remains true while waiting
for another vehicle to complete a turn. [StVO §26(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__26.html)

### Turning drivers must protect pedestrians

When turning, show particular consideration for pedestrians crossing the road
you are entering and wait when necessary. This applies whether turning right or
left, whether one person or several are crossing, and whether or not a zebra
crossing is marked. A green signal authorises movement only subject to these
turning duties. [StVO §9(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)

Before turning, also let straight-ahead cyclists and other vehicles named in the
rule pass when they are travelling in the same direction on or beside the
carriageway. [StVO §9(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)

### Expect mistakes at traffic islands and crossing aids

A traffic island (*Verkehrsinsel*) alone does not automatically give a
pedestrian or cyclist priority. Nevertheless, expect pedestrians to assume they
may cross and cyclists to enter without checking. Pedestrians normally must
cross promptly by a short route while observing vehicle traffic, but your duty
is still to avoid harming or endangering them. [StVO §25(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__25.html) [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

### A cyclist riding across has no automatic zebra-crossing priority

StVO §26 expressly protects pedestrians and people using wheelchairs or
mobility scooters; it does not give the same zebra-crossing priority to someone
who rides a bicycle across. A cyclist who dismounts and pushes is a pedestrian.
Even when a riding cyclist fails to yield, slow or stop and allow the crossing
if necessary—another person's mistake never permits a collision. [StVO §26(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__26.html) [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

## Numbers and distances

### Five-metre stopping zone

You must not stop **on a zebra crossing or within 5 m before it**. Parking is
also prohibited there because parking is leaving the vehicle or stopping for
more than three minutes. The no-overtaking rule applies at the crossing; keep
the approach visible rather than using it for stopping, parking, or passing.
[StVO Annex 2, sign 293](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO §12(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html) [StVO §26(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__26.html)

## Defensive approach

Reduce speed in good time and brake smoothly. The early, clear response reassures
pedestrians that you will wait and gives following traffic more time to react,
reducing both alarm at the crossing and rear-end-collision risk. This is the
practical application of the duties of constant caution, mutual consideration,
moderate approach speed, and waiting when necessary. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html) [StVO §26(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__26.html)

## Dataset warning

The explanation for **1.2.26-117** says a cyclist must always dismount before
crossing a zebra crossing. Current StVO §26 instead defines who receives
priority: a person riding a bicycle is not among the protected users, while a
person pushing a bicycle is a pedestrian. A rider may cross only while observing
the applicable vehicle priority, but the driver must still prevent danger. The
worksheet's safe response—allow the cyclist already crossing to finish—remains
correct. [StVO §26(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__26.html) [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

## Common exam traps

- Looking only at the stripes and missing sign 350 when snow covers the road.
- Passing a stopped vehicle because it is in another lane.
- Entering the crossing before checking that there is space beyond it.
- Assuming a green light removes the duty to wait for turning pedestrians.
- Assuming every traffic island gives pedestrians automatic priority.
- Assuming a riding cyclist has zebra-crossing priority—or using their error as
  a reason not to avoid danger.
- Braking late and sharply instead of signalling your intention early.

## Remember this

- **Visible intent:** moderate speed; wait if necessary.
- **Hidden person:** never pass the vehicle concealing the crossing.
- **Queue:** stop before the markings and keep them completely clear.
- **Turning:** special consideration for pedestrians; wait if needed.
- **Stop/park:** not on or within 5 m before the crossing.
- **Cyclist riding:** no automatic zebra priority, but prevent the collision.
