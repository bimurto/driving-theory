# Level Crossings — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Rail traffic priority and the moderate-speed approach

Rail vehicles have priority at crossings with **Zeichen 201 Andreaskreuz**, at
crossings over pedestrian, field, forest, or cycle paths, and at industrial or
port-area crossings where the entrance sign declares rail priority. Approach
these crossings only at moderate speed. [StVO §19(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__19.html)

Even with barriers open, look and listen along the tracks, expect the barrier or
signal to change, and allow for bumps or rails in the surface. Unprotected
crossings are especially likely on field and forest tracks, lightly used roads,
and in industrial or port areas. At a path crossing without an Andreaskreuz,
users must still wait for rail traffic as required. [StVO §19(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__19.html)

An Andreaskreuz with a lightning symbol additionally warns of an electrical
overhead line. The central meaning remains that rail traffic has priority.
[StVO Annex 2, sign 201](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### These signals always mean wait

Wait when:

- a rail vehicle approaches;
- red flashing, red, or yellow lights are shown;
- barriers begin lowering or are closed;
- a railway employee orders a stop, including with a white-red-white flag or
  red lamp; or
- an audible signal such as an approaching train's whistle sounds.

At a crossing with an Andreaskreuz, vehicles wait **before the cross**. A still-
open half barrier does not cancel a red light; stop immediately and do not try
to beat the barrier. [StVO §19(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__19.html)

At a crossing without rail priority, a railway employee's white-red-white flag
or red lamp still requires waiting at a safe distance. [StVO §19(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__19.html)

### Stay stopped until the signal releases you

After a train passes, remain stopped until the red flashing or red light goes
out and barriers and other signals release the road. Another train may follow.
Do not copy a driver opposite who starts prematurely. [StVO §19(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__19.html)

### Never queue on the rails

Enter only if traffic beyond the crossing leaves enough room to clear the rails
quickly and without stopping. If not, wait before the Andreaskreuz even when
lights are off and barriers are open. [StVO §19(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__19.html)

## Numbers and formulas

### Posted 10 km/h crossing

If the crossing's sign combination imposes **10 km/h**, that limit applies both
while approaching the covered section and crossing it as indicated. It is a
posted legal maximum, not a target; drive slower if visibility or surface
conditions require it. Traffic-sign commands override general rules, while the
adapted-speed duty remains. [StVO §39(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__39.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

## Situations and exceptions

### Waiting position and lighting

With a closed barrier in a built-up area, stop before the Andreaskreuz, leaving
nearby junctions clear. Where there is no cross, wait at a safe distance from
the crossing. At night, ensure the vehicle remains visible but do not dazzle
anyone with its headlights; parking/position lights may be enough while waiting
where the general lighting rules permit. [StVO §19(2), (6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__19.html) [StVO §17](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Remote signal before a junction

If a red level-crossing light is placed before a side junction, stop at the stop
line. If there is no line, stop before the signal. This position intentionally
keeps the junction and the approach to the rails clear. [StVO §37(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__37.html) [StVO Annex 2,
sign 294](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Directional red arrow

When a red flashing or red light has an arrow shape, only traffic intending to
travel in the arrow's direction must wait. A right-pointing arrow therefore
stops right-turning traffic. [StVO §19(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__19.html)

### Suspected barrier failure

Do not cross or manoeuvre around barriers that remain closed unexpectedly.
Another train or technical fault may be present. Stay clear and report the fault
to the police or by the provided crossing contact/emergency system; follow any
official instruction. The legal waiting duty continues while the barriers are
closed. [StVO §19(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__19.html)

## Common exam traps

- Continuing because the half barrier is still open despite a red light.
- Starting as soon as the first train passes while the red light remains on.
- Treating an open barrier as proof that no train can approach.
- Entering with no space beyond the rails.
- Waiting after the Andreaskreuz instead of before it.
- Assuming a path crossing without a cross gives road users priority.
- Copying another waiting driver instead of reading your own signal.
- Driving around a barrier that appears stuck.
- Dazzling others while waiting at night.

## Remember this

- **Approach:** moderate speed, look and listen.
- **Red/yellow light, lowering/closed barrier, staff stop, train signal:** wait.
- **Waiting point:** before the Andreaskreuz; otherwise safe distance.
- **Open barrier:** still observe and enter only if fully clear.
- **Queue beyond:** stay off the rails.
- **After train:** wait until every signal releases you.
- **Fault:** never bypass; report it.
