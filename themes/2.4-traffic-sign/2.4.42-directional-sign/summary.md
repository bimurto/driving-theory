# Directional signs — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

German directional and information signs (*Richtzeichen*, *Wegweiser* and
*Hinweise*) tell you which road or facility you are approaching, where a route
leads, or which local traffic rules begin. Read the pictogram and colour scheme
as a whole; a sign can mark an obligation or simply provide orientation.

## Learning goals

- distinguish the end of an *Autobahn* from the end of a *Kraftfahrstraße*;
- use motorway route and exit numbers and follow a motorway diversion;
- apply the local rules that begin at an *Ortstafel*;
- recognise parking, energy-fuelling, Park + Ride and accessible-parking signs; and
- use a *Schutzstreifen* lawfully when passing an obstruction.

## Core rules

### Motorway, expressway and route signs

<!-- questions: 2.4.42-001, 2.4.42-002, 2.4.42-004 -->

The blue motorway symbol **Zeichen 330.1 (Autobahn)** marks the start of
motorway rules; the crossed-out version **330.2 (Ende der Autobahn)** marks its
end. Do not confuse it with **331.2 (Ende der Kraftfahrstraße)**, the end of a
motor-traffic road. These signs are defined in StVO Annex 3, section 7
([official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)).
On an Autobahn, only vehicles capable by design of more than 60 km/h may use
the road, and entry is only at marked junctions (*Anschlussstellen*) (StVO
§18(1)–(2), [official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)).

<!-- questions: 2.4.42-005, 2.4.42-006 -->

White-on-blue motorway numbers identify the current route. The number in a
motorway exit panel (for example **26**) is the running number of that
Autobahn's exit, junction or interchange; it helps match road maps and
navigation directions. StVO Annex 3 expressly describes this numbering for
orientation ([official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)).
The yellow/black motorway diversion sign (*Umleitung für den Autobahnverkehr*)
directs motorway traffic onto a signed route when needed, for example during a
congestion-related closure; follow it until it returns you to the Autobahn.
Diversion signs for particular traffic are listed in StVO Annex 3, section 11
([official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)).

### Town sign and lane choice

<!-- questions: 2.4.42-007, 2.4.42-008-M, 2.4.42-101 -->

The front of **Zeichen 310 (Ortstafel)** starts a *geschlossene Ortschaft* and
the back, **311**, ends it. From the sign, the rules for traffic inside or
outside a built-up area apply (StVO Annex 3, section 2,
[official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)).
The general built-up-area maximum is **50 km/h**, unless another sign or the
conditions require less (StVO §3(3),
[official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)).

On a road with several marked lanes in one direction, a motor vehicle up to
**3.5 t permissible total mass** may choose any lane inside the built-up area
(except on an Autobahn). In that situation it may travel faster in the right
lane than in the left lane; the 50-km/h maximum still applies. This is the
inner-city exception in StVO §7(3) ([official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)).

After entering the town, do not use the horn to announce an overtaking
manoeuvre: warning sounds are permitted for overtaking only outside built-up
areas (StVO §16(1), [official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)).
Parking lights (*Standlicht/Begrenzungsleuchten*) may not be used for driving,
but a parked vehicle may be marked on the road-facing side with a parking
light where StVO §17(4) permits it; adequate street lighting can remove the
need for its own light ([official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)).

### Parking orientation and accessible parking

<!-- questions: 2.4.42-102, 2.4.42-110 -->

Where a parking sign shows a vehicle diagram, park in that depicted direction
and arrangement. The sign's pictorial arrangement controls how the parking
space is used; a vehicle below the displayed **2.8 t** threshold does not
override that instruction (StVO Annex 3, sign 314,
[official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)).

An accessible space marked with the wheelchair supplementary sign is reserved
for a person holding the relevant **EU parking card** (*EU-Parkausweis für
schwerbehinderte Menschen*). You may use it with your own card or while
transporting the card-holder; display the card clearly. The reservation and
display requirement appear in StVO Annex 3, sign 314/315,
[official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html).

### Energy and public-transport information

<!-- questions: 2.4.42-103, 2.4.42-104, 2.4.42-105, 2.4.42-106, 2.4.42-107 -->

Square blue information signs identify nearby facilities:

- **H₂** identifies a hydrogen filling station (*Wasserstofftankstelle*).
- **CNG** means compressed natural gas (*komprimiertes Erdgas*), a natural-gas
  filling station (*Erdgastankstelle*).
- **LPG** means liquefied petroleum gas (*Flüssiggas/Autogas*), an autogas
  station (*Autogastankstelle*).
- A plug and cable identify an electric-vehicle charging station
  (*Ladestation für Elektrofahrzeuge*).
- **P + R** (*Park and Ride*) identifies parking with a connection to public
  transport (*ÖPNV*).

These pictograms are information signs in the StVO sign catalogue (Annex 3,
including the facility symbols, [official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)).
The vehicle technologies behind LPG, CNG and hydrogen are also expressly
recognised in StVZO §41a ([official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__41a.html)).

### Protective bicycle lane

<!-- questions: 2.4.42-109-M -->

The dashed **Schutzstreifen für den Radverkehr** (cycle advisory/protective
lane, marking 340) is not a continuously drivable car lane. You may cross it
only when necessary—for example, to pass a truck or avoid oncoming traffic—and
only after excluding danger to cyclists. Do not stop on it. These are the
specific rules in StVO Annex 3, marking 340
([official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)).

## Common exam traps

- Treating 330.2 (end of Autobahn) as the end of a *Kraftfahrstraße*.
- Reading an exit number as a speed limit or route number.
- Applying free lane choice to an Autobahn or to a vehicle over 3.5 t.
- Thinking the 50-km/h town limit disappears because the right lane is faster.
- Honking to announce an overtake inside a town.
- Driving along a Schutzstreifen continuously, or endangering cyclists while
  passing an obstruction.
- Assuming any car may use a wheelchair-marked space without the EU card.

## Remember this

- **330.2:** end of *Autobahn*; **331.2:** end of *Kraftfahrstraße*.
- Motorway exit numbers are running orientation numbers.
- *Ortstafel* changes the built-up-area rules; normally **50 km/h**.
- Inner-city free lane choice: marked lanes, vehicle up to **3.5 t**, not an
  Autobahn; right may be faster than left.
- *Schutzstreifen*: cross only when necessary and only without endangering
  bicycle traffic; never stop on it.
- EU accessible-parking card must be displayed and may belong to the person
  being transported.
