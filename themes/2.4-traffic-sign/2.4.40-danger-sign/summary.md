# Danger Signs — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

German **danger signs (Gefahrzeichen)** warn of a hazard ahead; they are a
prompt to reassess speed, position, following distance, and readiness to brake,
not permission to continue at the previous speed. The sign catalogue and the
official meanings are in StVO §40 and Annex 1 (Anlage 1), including **Stau**
(congestion), **Kurve** (bend), **Seitenwind** (crosswind), **Wildwechsel**
(wildlife crossing), and hazards from trees. [StVO §40](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__40.html)
[StVO Anlage 1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html)

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- react safely to congestion, bends, wildlife, crosswinds, and tree hazards;
- connect each warning sign with a concrete speed, distance, lighting, or
  steering response; and
- recognise why swerving can create a greater danger than braking in lane.

## Core rules

### Congestion warning: create time and warn following traffic

<!-- questions: 2.4.40-002 -->

The **Stau** warning sign means you should become especially alert, reduce speed
as necessary, and increase the distance to the vehicle ahead. Look beyond the
next vehicle and keep an offset view where possible so that stopped traffic is
detected earlier. On a motorway (**Autobahn**), when approaching the end of a
queue, switch on the **hazard warning lights (Warnblinkanlage)** to warn traffic
behind; §16 expressly permits this when warning others of a traffic jam. Never
use the hard shoulder (**Standstreifen**) as a queue lane: it is not a normal
driving lane. [StVO Anlage 1, sign 124](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html)
[StVO §16(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)
[StVO §2(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html)

### Dangerous bend: slow before it and keep right

<!-- questions: 2.4.40-003 -->

For a **dangerous bend (Kurve)**, reduce speed before entering the bend and
drive as far to the right as practicable. This preserves a safety margin from
oncoming traffic, which may drift toward the centre while negotiating the bend,
and gives more room to react to a stopped or obstructed vehicle beyond the
curve. A warning sign announcing a bend does not itself mean that you are
turning at a junction: following the road through the bend therefore does not
require a turn signal (**Fahrtrichtungsanzeiger**). The general duty remains to
choose a speed that keeps the vehicle under control and fits visibility and
road conditions. [StVO Anlage 1, signs 103–105](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html)
[StVO §2(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html)
[StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)
[StVO §9(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)

### Wildlife crossing: brake in your lane, do not make a dangerous swerve

<!-- questions: 2.4.40-004 -->

The **wildlife crossing (Wildwechsel)** sign marks a section where animals may
enter the road. Reduce speed and scan the verges; be ready to brake (**bremsbereit**).
If an animal appears, brake and dip the headlights (**Abblendlicht**) so that
you do not dazzle it or other road users. If it suddenly runs into the lane,
make a firm emergency stop while keeping control. Do not swerve into the
opposing lane when that would endanger oncoming traffic: staying in lane and
braking is normally the safer response. This follows the StVO's constant-care
and mutual-consideration rule and its requirement to adapt speed to conditions.
 [StVO Anlage 1, sign 142](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html)
[StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)
[StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)
[StVO §17(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Crosswind: expect changed handling and correct it early

<!-- questions: 2.4.40-005, 2.4.40-006 -->

The **crosswind (Seitenwind)** sign warns of a section exposed to lateral wind,
often a bridge or elevated roadway. Expect the vehicle's responsiveness to
change: a gust can push it sideways or require **counter-steering (Gegenlenken)**
to stay in its lane. Reduce speed to the level the wind and road conditions
allow, keep both hands ready on the wheel, and make smooth corrections rather
than abrupt steering. The legal basis is the duty to adapt speed to weather,
road, visibility, and vehicle control; the sign is not an aviation warning.
 [StVO Anlage 1, sign 117](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html)
[StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Trees beside the road: anticipate blocked sight and falling debris

<!-- questions: 2.4.40-007 -->

The tree hazard warns that tall vehicles may have to brake or swerve left to
avoid overhanging branches, and that branches can be torn off and lie on the
roadway. Increase observation and following distance, reduce speed when needed,
and be prepared for both a sudden manoeuvre by the vehicle ahead and an
obstruction in your lane. Do not overtake into a conflict; the general rule is
to drive so that nobody is endangered and to remain able to stop within the
visible distance. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)
[StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- A warning sign does not set a fixed speed. The appropriate speed may be much
  lower than the general or posted maximum when the hazard, visibility, wind,
  traffic, or road surface requires it. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)
- Hazard warning lights approaching a traffic jam are for warning following
  traffic; they do not authorise driving on the hard shoulder. [StVO §16(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)
- Wildlife and debris call for controlled braking and observation. Swerving is
  only safe if it does not endanger another road user. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

## Common exam traps

- Treating the congestion sign as an instruction to use the hard shoulder.
- Waiting until the bend to brake, or signalling merely because the road curves.
- Swerving into oncoming traffic to avoid wildlife.
- Reading the crosswind sign as an aircraft/aviation sign.
- Forgetting that crosswind can change steering response and require
  counter-steering.
- Following a tall vehicle so closely that its brake or branch hazard is hidden.

## Remember this

- **Stau:** slow, increase distance, look through traffic, and warn behind with
  hazard lights when approaching the queue.
- **Kurve:** brake before the bend and keep right; no signal for simply following
  the road.
- **Wildwechsel:** slow and scan; dip headlights and brake; do not endanger
  oncoming traffic by swerving.
- **Seitenwind:** expect altered response, reduce speed, and counter-steer
  smoothly as needed.
- **Trees:** expect sudden braking/swerving vehicles and branches on the road.
