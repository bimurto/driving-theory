# Regulatory Signs (Vorschriftszeichen) — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Regulatory signs (*Vorschriftszeichen*) impose a command or prohibition. Their
red rings normally mark a *Verkehrsverbot* (traffic prohibition), while blue
signs can impose a required use. Once the sign applies, every road user must
obey it; the sign normally takes effect at its position or at the distance
shown on a supplementary sign. [StVO §41](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__41.html)

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- recognise restrictions on mass, axle load, width, height, and length;
- apply snow-chain, lane, and overtaking signs; and
- distinguish actual mass (*tatsächliche Masse*) from permitted maximum mass
  (*zulässige Gesamtmasse*).

## Core rules

### Keep right and obey lane closures

<!-- questions: 2.4.41-004-M -->

The general rule is to drive as far right as practicable (*Rechtsfahrgebot*).
On a motorway, move into the right-hand lane when there is no vehicle to
overtake. A lane marked as closed is not available—even a breakdown does not
make it a usable driving lane; stop only where permitted and follow the
emergency procedure. [StVO §2(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html)

### End of a temporary speed restriction

<!-- questions: 2.4.41-005-M -->

After passing the hazard or construction area and the sign marking the end of
the speed limit (*Ende der zulässigen Höchstgeschwindigkeit*, Zeichen 278),
the previous limit no longer applies. If the lane is clear and conditions allow
it, accelerate progressively—but never above another applicable limit or a
safe speed. [StVO Anlage 2, lfd. Nr. 56](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html), [StVO §3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Snow chains

<!-- questions: 2.4.41-101 -->

Zeichen 268 (*Schneeketten vorgeschrieben*) means the road may be used only
with snow chains fitted; it is not a sign about tracked vehicles. Even in the
best conditions, a motor vehicle with snow chains may not exceed **50 km/h**.
Check fitted chains promptly if the vehicle pulls, vibrates, or makes unusual
noise. [StVO Anlage 2, lfd. Nr. 42](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html), [StVO §3(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Mass restrictions: read the symbol precisely

<!-- questions: 2.4.41-102, 2.4.41-103, 2.4.41-104 -->

Signs 262–263 prohibit traffic whose **actual** stated limit is exceeded,
including load (*einschließlich Ladung*):

- Zeichen 262 (*tatsächliche Masse*) concerns the vehicle's current mass—the
  empty vehicle plus occupants, cargo, and other load.
- Zeichen 263 (*tatsächliche Achslast*) concerns the actual load on the axle;
  a 2-t sign therefore excludes a vehicle whose axle load exceeds 2 t.
- A sign showing the white truck silhouette with a mass on a supplementary
  plate can instead refer to *zulässige Gesamtmasse* (the permitted maximum
  mass in the vehicle documents), not the vehicle's current weighing.

The red-ring prohibition applies at the stated threshold: do not enter when
the relevant actual mass or axle load is greater than the number. [StVO Anlage
2, lfd. Nr. 36–37](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Width, height, and length limits include the load

<!-- questions: 2.4.41-105, 2.4.41-106, 2.4.41-107 -->

The dimensional signs prohibit a vehicle or combination exceeding the stated
**actual** dimension, including its load:

- Zeichen 264 (*tatsächliche Breite*) includes the exterior mirrors. A 2 m
  sign is not a 2 m road width and does not prescribe a 2 m side gap.
- Zeichen 265 (*tatsächliche Höhe*) protects low tunnels, bridges, and cables.
  A 3.8 m vehicle cannot pass a 3.8 m limit safely; the vehicle's measured
  height must be below the displayed limit.
- Zeichen 266 (*tatsächliche Länge*) applies to the total length of a vehicle
  combination (*Gesamtlänge*). Thus an 11 m car-and-caravan or 12 m tractor-
  trailer is barred by a 10 m sign, while a 9 m truck is not.

Check mirrors, roof load, and trailer when judging dimensions. [StVO Anlage 2,
lfd. Nr. 38–40](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Overtaking prohibitions

<!-- questions: 2.4.41-108, 2.4.41-109 -->

Zeichen 276 (*Überholverbot für Kraftfahrzeuge aller Art*) means a multi-track
motor vehicle may not overtake either single-track or multi-track vehicles.
The prohibition is not limited to cars or to one kind of vehicle. [StVO Anlage
2, lfd. Nr. 53](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

Zeichen 277 (*Überholverbot für Kraftfahrzeuge über 3,5 t*) normally applies to
motor vehicles over 3.5 t permitted total mass, including trailers, and to
tractors; cars and buses are normally excepted. A supplementary plate reading
*auch* with bus and car-with-trailer symbols removes those exceptions, so a
coach/bus is covered in the worksheet situation. Always read the plate with
the main sign. [StVO Anlage 2, lfd. Nr. 54 and 54.2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

Overtaking remains forbidden until the sign ends, a stated supplementary
distance is complete, or an end sign (Zeichen 280/281) appears. Independently,
an overtaking manoeuvre is legal only when the whole manoeuvre can be completed
without endangering or obstructing oncoming traffic. [StVO §5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Common exam traps

- Confusing *tatsächliche Masse* with *zulässige Gesamtmasse*.
- Treating a 2 m width sign as road width or a required lateral distance.
- Forgetting mirrors and load when measuring width, and trailer plus towing
  vehicle when measuring combination length.
- Reading a 3.8 m height sign as a suggestion rather than a prohibition.
- Assuming Zeichen 277 always covers buses; check the *auch* supplementary sign.
- Treating Zeichen 276 as allowing overtaking of motorcycles or bicycles.
- Accelerating after a limit ends without checking a new limit, traffic, or
  visibility.

## Remember this

- **268:** chains required; with chains, maximum **50 km/h**.
- **262 / 263:** actual mass / actual axle load.
- **264 / 265 / 266:** actual width (mirrors), height, / length (combination).
- **276:** overtake no vehicles with a multi-track motor vehicle.
- **277:** normally >3.5 t and tractors; *auch* can add buses and cars with
  trailers.
- Red ring plus a number means do not exceed the relevant threshold.
