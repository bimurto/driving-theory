# Traffic Facilities — Class B Study Summary

## Emergency telephone marker

The arrow on a roadside emergency-telephone marker points **towards the nearest
emergency telephone**. Follow the arrow; it does not point to the next exit or
to the direction of travel.

**Remember:** *arrow = nearest phone.*
