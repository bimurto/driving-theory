# Traffic Facilities — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Red-and-white diagonal warning plates (*Warntafeln*) make an unusual vehicle
outline conspicuous. Their meaning depends on how and where they are fitted: they
can mark a parked vehicle or trailer, or draw attention to exceptional width.
They are not the orange panels used for dangerous goods.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- recognise a red-and-white park warning plate;
- identify parked-trailer and excessive-width uses; and
- distinguish these plates from dangerous-goods identification.

## Core rules

### Red-and-white plates can mark parked or over-width vehicles

<!-- questions: 1.4.43-101 -->

Park warning plates have 100 mm red-and-white stripes running outward and
downward at 45 degrees. They show the side boundary of a parked vehicle or
combination and may be used to mark vehicles and trailers parked on the
carriageway within a built-up area instead of the otherwise required lighting.
[StVZO §51c(1)–(2), (5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__51c.html) [StVO §43(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__43.html)

Similar approved warning plates also mark exceptional vehicle width. For
specified agricultural tractors and combinations wider than 2.75 m, plates are
required at the outer sides front and rear so the true width is conspicuous.
[35th StVZO Exemption Regulation §1(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzoausnv_35/__1.html)

Orange rectangular panels have a different function: they identify regulated
dangerous-goods transport. Do not infer hazardous cargo merely from red-and-white
diagonal vehicle-outline markings. [GGVSEB §19(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ggvseb/__19.html)

## Common exam traps

- Assuming red-and-white diagonal plates always mean an over-width vehicle.
- Forgetting that a parked trailer within a built-up area may carry them.
- Confusing red-and-white outline warnings with orange dangerous-goods panels.

## Remember this

- **Parked trailer:** red-and-white plate can show its traffic-side boundary.
- **Exceptional width:** plates can mark the outer vehicle outline.
- **Dangerous goods:** orange panel, not red-and-white diagonal stripes.
