# Directional Signs — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Directional signs (*Richtzeichen*) guide traffic and often create binding rules:
priority, special-road status, parking permissions, pedestrian-crossing duties,
or traffic-calmed-area behaviour. Read the symbol, any supplementary diagram,
and road markings together; a blue information sign is not automatically merely
advisory.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- distinguish one-junction priority from a continuing priority road;
- apply Autobahn, motor-road, tunnel, and emergency-bay rules;
- interpret parking, traffic-calmed-area, and special-facility signs;
- use guide lines and cycle protective lanes correctly; and
- recognise orientation signs for towns, federal roads, diversions, and services.

## Core rules

### Sign 301 gives priority once; sign 306 continues it

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-001, 1.4.42-012, 1.4.42-101 -->

Sign 301 (*Vorfahrt*) gives priority only at the **next** intersection or
junction. It does not establish a continuing priority road and has no effect at
the junction after that. [StVO Annex 3, sign 301](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-102, 1.4.42-134 -->

Sign 306 (*Vorfahrtstraße*) establishes a priority road until sign 205, 206, or
307 ends it. Outside built-up areas, parking on its carriageway is prohibited;
stopping at the right edge and parking on a suitable hard shoulder remain
possible unless another rule forbids them. [StVO Annex 3, sign 306](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-104 -->

Parking and stopping are different: leaving the vehicle or stopping longer than
three minutes is parking. The outside-town sign-306 prohibition covers parking
on the carriageway, not lawful brief stopping or parking on a usable side strip.
[StVO §12(2), (4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html) [StVO Annex 3, sign 306](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-106 -->

Sign 307 ends the priority road. Reassess the next junction from its lights,
signs, or general priority rule rather than carrying the former priority forward.
[StVO Annex 3, sign 307](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

### Turning priority road: follow the thick line and signal

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-136, 1.4.42-141 -->

The supplementary diagram under sign 306 shows an *abknickende Vorfahrtstraße*;
the thick line is its course. If it bends left and you follow it, signal left
early and clearly. The sign grants priority but does not force you to follow the
bend. Give pedestrians special consideration and wait if necessary.
[StVO Annex 3, sign 306 supplementary diagram](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

### Priority over oncoming traffic still requires caution

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-107 -->

Sign 308 gives priority over oncoming traffic at a bottleneck. Enter only when
the narrow section is clear enough; be ready to stop and allow a vehicle already
inside to leave rather than enforcing priority into a collision. [StVO Annex 3, sign 308](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html) [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

## Road status and facilities

### Autobahn and motor road

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-005 -->

Sign 330.1 marks the start of an Autobahn; the special rules of StVO §18 apply
from the sign onward. [StVO Annex 3, sign 330.1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html) [StVO §18](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-116 -->

Autobahns and motor roads (*Kraftfahrstraßen*, sign 331.1) may be used only by
motor vehicles whose design maximum speed exceeds 60 km/h, and trailers carried
there must likewise be designed for more than 60 km/h. Thus the minimum stated
whole-kilometre design speed is **61 km/h**. [StVO §18(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html) [StVO Annex 3, sign 331.1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

### Tunnel: dipped headlights even when brightly lit

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-129, 1.4.42-131, 1.4.42-132 -->

Sign 327 marks a tunnel. Use dipped headlights (*Abblendlicht*) throughout,
including by day in a brightly illuminated tunnel; daytime running lights alone
are insufficient. Remove sunglasses before entry when needed for adaptation and
visibility. U-turning is prohibited. [StVO Annex 3, sign 327](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html) [StVO §17(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Emergency stopping bay

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-130, 1.4.42-146 -->

Sign 328 identifies an emergency stop and breakdown bay
(*Nothalte- und Pannenbucht*). Use it only for an emergency or breakdown, expect
that another disabled vehicle may already occupy it, and never use it for an
ordinary rest. [StVO Annex 3, sign 328](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

## Traffic-calmed areas and pedestrian protection

### Start and rules of a traffic-calmed area

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-011, 1.4.42-140, 1.4.42-142 -->

Sign 325.1 begins a *verkehrsberuhigter Bereich*. Drive at **walking speed**;
pedestrians may use the whole road, children may play everywhere, and vehicle
traffic must neither endanger nor obstruct pedestrians and must wait if needed.
Park only in marked spaces, except brief boarding/alighting or loading/unloading.
[StVO Annex 3, sign 325.1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-133 -->

Sign 325.2 ends the traffic-calmed area. On exiting, apply StVO §10: exclude
danger to other road users and yield as necessary; the exit does not grant
priority. [StVO Annex 3, sign 325.2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html) [StVO §10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__10.html)

### Pedestrian crossing

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-138 -->

At sign 350 and the zebra crossing, approach at moderate speed when a protected
user visibly intends to cross, allow the crossing, and wait if necessary. Do not
overtake there and do not enter when a queue would make you stop on the crossing.
[StVO §26(1)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__26.html) [StVO Annex 3, sign 350](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

### Traffic helper

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-139 -->

Sign 356 warns of traffic helpers, often school crossing guards. Expect traffic
to stop and children to cross more frequently; reduce speed and remain ready to
brake. [StVO Annex 3, sign 356](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html) [StVO §3(2a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

## Parking permissions

### Resident and disability parking

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-109, 1.4.42-110 -->

Sign 314 with a resident-permit supplement reserves parking for the specified
permit number. A wheelchair-symbol space is reserved for holders of the required
official disability parking permit, including an authorised person transporting
the eligible permit holder. Other drivers may not park there even briefly.
[StVO Annex 3, sign 314](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

### Parking ticket

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-111 -->

Where a readable parking ticket is required, display it so it can be checked and
do not exceed the paid expiry time. A parking disc is not a general substitute;
when a machine is out of order, use a disc only under the statutory fallback
conditions and obey the posted maximum. [StVO §13(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__13.html) [StVO Annex 3, sign 314](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

### Pavement parking

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-112 -->

Sign 315 permits pavement parking only in the depicted arrangement and only for
vehicles with permissible total mass up to **2.8 t**; motorcycles are included
within the permission. Do not park differently or obstruct the remaining path.
[StVO Annex 3, sign 315](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

## Markings and cycle protective lanes

### Shark teeth highlight an existing waiting duty

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-009-M, 1.4.42-010-M -->

Sign 342 “shark teeth” (*Haifischzähne*) can highlight a waiting duty arising
from *rechts vor links*. They make the conflict visible but do not reverse or
independently create priority; approach cautiously and yield according to the
underlying rule. [StVO Annex 3, sign 342](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html) [StVO §8(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__8.html)

### Guide lines and protective lanes

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-145 -->

A broken guide line (*Leitlinie*, sign 340) may be crossed to overtake or pass an
obstruction only when this does not endanger traffic. It separates lanes but is
not the absolute crossing ban of a solid line. [StVO Annex 3, sign 340](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-137, 1.4.42-147-M -->

A cycle protective lane (*Schutzstreifen*) marked by sign 340 may be driven on
only when necessary—such as to meet oncoming traffic—and only without endangering
cycle traffic. Stopping on it is prohibited. [StVO Annex 3, sign 340](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

## Orientation and information signs

### Dead end, first aid, and street lighting

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-119, 1.4.42-120, 1.4.42-125 -->

Sign 357 identifies a dead end, not a general access prohibition. Sign 358 points
to first aid. The red lantern ring (sign 394) marks lighting that does not operate
all night; where a vehicle otherwise would be hard to see, illuminate it as the
parking-light rules require. [StVO Annex 3, signs 357, 358, and 394](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html) [StVO §17(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Federal road, alternative route, and diversions

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-126 -->

Sign 401 identifies a numbered federal road (*Bundesstraße*); the number is a
route identifier, not a speed limit. [StVO Annex 3, sign 401](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-128 -->

An orange diversion arrow suggests an alternative Autobahn route around a
congestion-prone section. It is guidance, not a mandatory direction.
[StVO Annex 3, sign 467.1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-135 -->

A numbered diversion sign identifies which signed detour route to follow; keep
matching the displayed number at later decision points. [StVO Annex 3, diversion signs](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

### Customs and built-up-area end

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-143 -->

Sign 392 announces a customs post. Expect checks and queues, reduce speed, and
follow authorised instructions. [StVO Annex 3, sign 392](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.42-144 -->

The reverse locality sign 311 marks the end of the built-up area; a destination
shown above names the next place. Outside-town rules apply from the sign, subject
to any separate restriction. [StVO Annex 3, sign 311](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

## Dataset warning

The explanations for **1.4.42-009-M** and **1.4.42-010-M** call the shark-tooth
markings waiting lines. Current Annex 3 distinguishes sign 342
(*Haifischzähne*), which highlights an existing right-before-left waiting duty,
from sign 341 (*Wartelinie*), which recommends a waiting position. [StVO Annex 3, signs 341–342](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

The explanation for **1.4.42-111** says a parking disc replaces a ticket whenever
a parking meter is out of operation. Current StVO §13 requires a parking disc
only where no other nearby functioning meter or ticket machine is available.
[StVO §13(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__13.html)

## Common exam traps

- Carrying sign 301 priority beyond the next junction.
- Parking on an outside-town priority-road carriageway instead of the shoulder.
- Treating sign 308 priority as permission to enter an occupied bottleneck.
- Using daytime running lights instead of dipped headlights in a tunnel.
- Using an emergency bay for a rest.
- Leaving a traffic-calmed area as though you had priority.
- Crossing a protective lane without necessity or stopping on it.
- Confusing shark teeth with a stop line or a new independent priority rule.
- Reading a route number as a speed limit.

## Remember this

- **301:** next junction only. **306:** continuing priority road. **307:** end.
- **Motor road:** design maximum must exceed 60 km/h.
- **Tunnel:** dipped headlights, even by day.
- **Traffic-calmed:** walking speed, pedestrians everywhere, marked parking only.
- **Protective lane:** cross only if needed and safe; never stop on it.
- **Shark teeth:** highlight an existing waiting duty.
