# Danger Signs — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Danger signs (*Gefahrzeichen*) warn early enough for you to recognise a hazard,
reduce speed, increase attention, and be ready to brake. They usually do not
grant priority or impose a single fixed speed; the symbol, supplementary sign,
road layout, weather, and traffic determine the safe response.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- recognise the principal German danger-sign symbols;
- interpret distance, length, direction, and condition supplementary signs;
- choose a safe response for surface, visibility, people, and animal hazards;
- distinguish a warning from a priority or prohibition sign; and
- read railway-crossing warning beacons and distances.

## Core rules

### Symbol—supplement—situation—response

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-101 -->

Read the triangle's symbol first, then every supplementary plate. Outside built-up
areas, a danger sign generally stands **150–250 m before** the hazard; inside
built-up areas it generally stands shortly before it. A supplementary sign may
change the distance, indicate the hazard's length, direction, or condition, or
identify a specific danger. [StVO §40(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__40.html) [StVO Annex 1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html)

Adapt speed so the vehicle remains controllable and can stop within the visible
distance. Increase attention and distance, avoid abrupt inputs, and expect other
road users to react late or incorrectly. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO §4(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html)

## Junctions, road shape, and traffic

### Uncontrolled junction ahead

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-001 -->

Sign 102 warns of an intersection or junction where *rechts vor links* applies.
Reduce speed, be ready to brake, and watch the right for priority traffic while
also anticipating a mistake from the left. The sign warns; the priority itself
comes from StVO §8. [StVO Annex 1, sign 102](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §8(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__8.html)

### Downhill and uphill gradients

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-002, 1.4.40-106, 1.4.40-156 -->

Sign 108 warns of a steep **descent**. Expect a longer braking distance and brake
fade from prolonged service-brake use. Select a low gear early to use engine
braking and never coast with the clutch disengaged. A length plate with opposing
horizontal arrows means the gradient continues for that length—for example
800 m—rather than beginning 800 m away. [StVO Annex 1, sign 108](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-107, 1.4.40-157 -->

Sign 110 warns of a steep **ascent**. With a manual gearbox, change down in good
time if the engine cannot maintain an appropriate speed; do not wait until the
vehicle labours and obstructs traffic. [StVO Annex 1, sign 110](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §3(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Narrow road and lane ending

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-004, 1.4.40-013, 1.4.40-114, 1.4.40-115 -->

Signs 120 and 121 warn that the carriageway narrows, symmetrically or on one
side. Reduce speed, avoid overtaking, assess oncoming traffic, and wait if the
remaining width cannot be shared safely. A distance plate such as 50 m tells you
where the narrowing begins. [StVO Annex 1, signs 120–121](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__6.html)

If one of two same-direction lanes ends, use the zipper method immediately before
the restriction, alternating one vehicle at a time. This does not itself decide
priority against oncoming traffic. Sign 208, not a danger triangle, expressly
requires yielding to oncoming traffic at a narrow section. [StVO §7(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html) [StVO Annex 2, sign 208](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Unexpected oncoming traffic

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-005 -->

Sign 125 warns of oncoming traffic where it may be unexpected, such as after a
one-way section or through roadworks. Keep right, adapt speed and clearance, and
expect headlamp glare at night. The two black arrows do not assign priority.
[StVO Annex 1, sign 125](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §2(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html)

### Curves

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-105, 1.4.40-143 -->

Signs 103 and 105 warn of a sharp bend or a sequence of bends; the symbol shows
the first direction. Reduce speed before the bend, keep right, expect vehicles
ahead to brake, and anticipate oncoming traffic cutting the corner.
[StVO Annex 1, signs 103–105](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Queues and traffic lights

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-118 -->

Sign 124 warns of congestion. Reduce speed, enlarge following distance, watch
beyond the vehicle ahead, and be ready for sudden braking. On Autobahns and
multi-lane rural roads, form a rescue corridor once traffic reaches walking pace
or stops. [StVO Annex 1, sign 124](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §4(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html) [StVO §11(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__11.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-121 -->

Sign 131 warns of traffic signals that may be hard to see or unexpected. Look
for the signal early, reduce speed as necessary, and be prepared for the queue
or junction behind it. [StVO Annex 1, sign 131](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §37](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__37.html)

## Surface, weather, and roadside hazards

### Uneven road

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-108, 1.4.40-109 -->

Sign 112 warns of an uneven surface. Reduce speed before grooves or bumps and
avoid sudden steering or braking. Excess speed can cause loss of grip, skidding
into oncoming traffic, suspension or axle damage, and damage to the load.
[StVO Annex 1, sign 112](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Slippery road, snow, and ice

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-112 -->

Sign 114 warns of skidding danger, especially when the surface is wet or dirty.
Slow down early, increase distance, and make steering, braking, and acceleration
smooth. [StVO Annex 1, sign 114](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-103, 1.4.40-133, 1.4.40-137, 1.4.40-148 -->

A snowflake, winter-sport, or snow/ice supplementary plate specifies the danger:
expect packed snow, black ice, skiers or tobogganers, and possibly limited winter
maintenance. Ice can occur even at **+3 °C**, especially on bridges. Reduce speed
and increase distance; the condition plate applies when the stated hazard is
present. [StVO §39(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__39.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Loose gravel

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-138, 1.4.40-150 -->

Sign 116 warns of loose chippings. Avoid heavy acceleration and sudden steering,
keep the greatest practicable distance from the vehicle ahead, and expect stones
to reduce grip or be thrown up. [StVO Annex 1, sign 116](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §4(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html)

### Side wind

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-012 -->

Sign 117 warns of side wind. Reduce speed, hold the steering wheel securely, and
expect sudden lateral displacement—especially after leaving shelter or when
passing large vehicles. [StVO Annex 1, sign 117](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Roadworks

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-116, 1.4.40-136 -->

Sign 123 warns of roadworks; a distance plate such as 100 m locates the start.
Expect workers, construction vehicles, tools, materials, dirt, narrowed lanes,
and sudden queues even when no work is visible. Slow down and be ready to stop.
[StVO Annex 1, sign 123](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Falling rocks

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-141, 1.4.40-153 -->

Sign 115 warns of falling or fallen rocks. Expect stones on the carriageway and
vehicles ahead to brake suddenly. Avoid stopping or parking in the exposed area
unless an emergency requires it, and leave extra distance. [StVO Annex 1, sign 115](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §4(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html)

### Movable bridge and waterfront

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-139, 1.4.40-151 -->

Sign 128 warns of a movable bridge. Expect the road to be interrupted for water
traffic and obey barriers or signals. [StVO Annex 1, sign 128](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-140, 1.4.40-152 -->

Sign 129 warns that the road leads directly to a waterfront or quay. Reduce
speed, especially in darkness, fog, or poor visibility when water may be mistaken
for continuing road. [StVO Annex 1, sign 129](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

## People and animals

### Children

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-006 -->

Sign 136 warns that children may enter the road, commonly near schools,
kindergartens, or playgrounds. Use maximum attention, reduce speed, and remain
ready to brake for sudden, unpredictable movement. Drivers must reduce speed and
exclude danger where children require special care. [StVO Annex 1, sign 136](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §3(2a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Pedestrians and a pedestrian crossing

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-010, 1.4.40-011, 1.4.40-146, 1.4.40-155 -->

A pedestrian or pedestrian-crossing warning sign/combination means people may
cross and the crossing may be hard to see. Approach at moderate speed, watch
closely, and be ready to wait. At a marked zebra crossing, enable pedestrians and
protected mobility-device users who visibly intend to cross, and do not overtake.
[StVO Annex 1, sign 133](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §26](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__26.html)

### Cyclists

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-144 -->

Sign 138 warns that cyclists may cross or enter the carriageway, for example
where a cycle route ends. Reduce speed, scan both sides, and be ready for a rider
whose approach is partly hidden. [StVO Annex 1, sign 138](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

### Horses, carriages, and livestock

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-008, 1.4.40-009, 1.4.40-147 -->

Sign 101 with a rider or carriage supplementary symbol warns of horses, riders,
or horse-drawn vehicles. Slow down, pass only with ample clearance, and avoid
noise or abrupt manoeuvres that may startle an animal. The supplementary symbol
defines the otherwise general danger. [StVO §39(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__39.html) [StVO Annex 1, sign 101](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-134, 1.4.40-149 -->

Sign 140 warns of livestock. Expect animals and handlers on the road and a dirty,
slippery surface left by the herd; reduce speed and avoid frightening animals.
[StVO Annex 1, sign 140](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Aircraft

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-145, 1.4.40-154 -->

Sign 144 warns of low-flying aircraft. Expect sudden noise, distraction, and
vehicles ahead braking unexpectedly. Keep attention on the road and increase
distance rather than looking at the aircraft. [StVO Annex 1, sign 144](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §4(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html)

## Level crossings

### Direction, distance, and beacons

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-127, 1.4.40-130, 1.4.40-131, 1.4.40-132, 1.4.40-142 -->

Sign 151 warns of a level crossing; a directional arrow can show that it lies on
the road turning off rather than straight ahead. Three-, two-, and one-stripe
beacons indicate approximately **240 m, 160 m, and 80 m** remaining respectively.
Approach with increased attention and be ready to stop for rail traffic.
[StVO Annex 1, signs 151 and 156–162](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO §19](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__19.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.40-135 -->

If the three-stripe beacon is combined with a no-overtaking sign, both messages
apply: the crossing is about 240 m ahead and the indicated overtaking prohibition
begins at the sign and continues until lawfully ended. Do not infer a prohibition
from the warning beacon alone. [StVO Annex 1, three-stripe beacon](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_1.html) [StVO Annex 2, sign 276](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

## Dataset warning

The explanations for **1.4.40-004** and **1.4.40-114** can make the zipper method
sound like a general rule for every narrow-road warning. Current StVO §7(4)
requires zipper merging when a lane ends or cannot be continuously used; meeting
oncoming traffic at a bottleneck is a different problem governed by available
width, signs 208/308 where present, and safe passing. [StVO §7(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html) [StVO §6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__6.html)

## Common exam traps

- Treating a danger sign as a grant of priority or a fixed speed limit.
- Ignoring a supplementary plate that changes distance, length, or direction.
- Coasting downhill and overheating the service brakes.
- Applying zipper merging to oncoming traffic at every bottleneck.
- Braking or steering abruptly on gravel, ice, or a slippery surface.
- Looking at aircraft or roadworks instead of the traffic ahead.
- Reading one/two/three railway stripes backwards.
- Assuming a railway beacon itself creates a no-overtaking prohibition.

## Remember this

- **Outside towns:** danger usually 150–250 m ahead unless supplemented.
- **Triangle warns:** adapt speed, attention, distance, and braking readiness.
- **Gradient:** low gear; protect brakes downhill.
- **Surface danger:** smooth steering, braking, and acceleration.
- **People/animals:** slow early and expect unpredictable movement.
- **Rail beacons:** 3 = 240 m, 2 = 160 m, 1 = 80 m.
