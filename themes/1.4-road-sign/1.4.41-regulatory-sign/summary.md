# Regulatory Signs — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Regulatory signs (*Vorschriftzeichen*) impose duties, prohibitions, limits, or
permissions. Read the main sign together with every supplementary sign, time,
distance, arrow, lane placement, and road marking. A permission never removes
the duty to protect other road users or adapt to actual conditions.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- distinguish mandatory directions, access bans, and special paths;
- apply stopping, parking, speed, and overtaking restrictions;
- interpret start/end arrows, distances, lengths, times, and wet-road plates;
- follow STOP, give-way, bottleneck, roundabout, and rail-crossing rules; and
- apply bicycle-street, bicycle-zone, one-way, and road-marking rules.

## Core rules

### Read sign, supplement, placement, and conditions as one instruction

A supplementary sign restricts, extends, or explains the main sign. A distance
without arrows normally locates the rule ahead; a length plate with opposing
arrows states how long it applies. A time plate limits the rule to that period,
and a condition such as *bei Nässe* activates it only under that condition.
[StVO §39(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__39.html)

## Priority, railways, and bottlenecks

### Rail traffic has priority at the crossbuck

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-001 -->

At sign 201 (*Andreaskreuz*), wait when a rail vehicle approaches, a railway
employee signals with a white-red-white flag, red or yellow lights show, barriers
move/close, or the crossing cannot be cleared without stopping on it. Stop before
the crossbuck or other prescribed point and cross only when safe and continuous.
[StVO §19(1)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__19.html) [StVO Annex 2, sign 201](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Give way, STOP, and advance warning

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-146, 1.4.41-153, 1.4.41-155, 1.4.41-156, 1.4.41-157 -->

Sign 205 means give way: approach at moderate speed and enter only without
endangering or substantially obstructing priority traffic. Sign 206 means make a
**complete stop** at the stop line, or at the sight line if none exists, then
give way—even when no cross traffic is visible. An advance sign with “100 m”
warns that sign 206 applies 100 m ahead; it is not the stopping point itself.
[StVO §8(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__8.html) [StVO Annex 2, signs 205–206](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-031, 1.4.41-158 -->

Sign 307 expressly ends a priority road. Sign 205 or 206 also ends sign 306's
continuing priority and imposes a new duty to yield. Sign 301 instead grants
priority at only the next junction; a danger triangle merely warns.
[StVO Annex 3, signs 301, 306–307](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html) [StVO Annex 2, signs 205–206](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Yield to oncoming traffic at the bottleneck

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-026-M, 1.4.41-102 -->

Sign 208 requires you to give priority to oncoming traffic where the road is too
narrow for both directions. Wait early rather than entering against a vehicle
already approaching the bottleneck. [StVO Annex 2, sign 208](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Right on red and STOP both require stopping

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-032 -->

Sign 206 always requires a full stop. A static green-arrow sign beside a red
light also permits a right turn only after stopping at the stop line or before
the junction and ensuring nobody is obstructed or endangered. [StVO Annex 2, sign 206](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO §37(2) no. 1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__37.html)

## Mandatory directions and special paths

### Blue direction signs prescribe the permitted movement

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-004, 1.4.41-005, 1.4.41-006, 1.4.41-007, 1.4.41-106, 1.4.41-152, 1.4.41-159, 1.4.41-160, 1.4.41-162, 1.4.41-173 -->

Signs 209–214 require the direction shown: left, right, straight, or one of the
displayed alternatives. Signs 222 require passing an obstacle on the indicated
side. Signal every actual turn even when the sign leaves only one legal option;
the sign does not replace the turn signal. A round blue straight-ahead sign is
mandatory direction, not the rectangular one-way sign. [StVO Annex 2, signs 209–214 and 222](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO §9(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)

### Footpath, cycle path, bridleway, and bus lane

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-114, 1.4.41-145, 1.4.41-147, 1.4.41-149, 1.4.41-154 -->

Signs 237, 239, and 238 designate mandatory cycle paths, footpaths, and
bridleways for the pictured users; other traffic is excluded unless a
supplementary sign permits it. A delivery-traffic exception on a footpath allows
only genuine delivery access during the stated permission; drive at pedestrian
speed, give pedestrians priority, and avoid obstruction or danger. Children up
to the statutory ages retain the special pavement rules for cycling.
[StVO Annex 2, signs 237–239](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO §2(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-115 -->

A marked special lane for regular-service buses is reserved for the traffic
specified by sign 245 and any supplements. Other vehicles may use it only when
expressly permitted. [StVO Annex 2, sign 245](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

## Access bans and exceptions

### No entry, no vehicles, and class-specific bans

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-012, 1.4.41-013, 1.4.41-029, 1.4.41-033, 1.4.41-118, 1.4.41-119, 1.4.41-120, 1.4.41-121, 1.4.41-148 -->

Sign 267 prohibits entry from that direction; expect vehicles to emerge, and
do not enter by bicycle unless a supplement permits it. Sign 250 prohibits
vehicles of every kind, although bicycles and motorcycles may be pushed. Symbol
bans apply only to the depicted class—for example bicycles, motorcycles, mopeds,
or multi-track motor vehicles. A “100 m” plate places the prohibition 100 m
ahead. [StVO Annex 2, signs 250–267](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-010 -->

Sign 242.1 begins a pedestrian zone. Vehicle traffic is prohibited unless a
supplement grants an exception; ownership or residence alone is not permission.
Permitted vehicle traffic must protect pedestrians and adapt to pedestrian
speed. [StVO Annex 2, signs 242.1–242.2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-116 -->

*Anlieger frei* permits people whose journey has a genuine destination or
business on the restricted road—including residents, their visitors, customers,
patients, and delivery callers. It does not permit through traffic.
[StVO §39(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__39.html)

## Bicycle streets, bicycle zones, and contraflow

### Bicycle street and authorised motor traffic

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-018, 1.4.41-028-M, 1.4.41-168, 1.4.41-169 -->

Sign 244.1 creates a bicycle street (*Fahrradstraße*). Bicycle and
electrokleinstfahrzeug traffic is allowed; other vehicles need a supplementary
exception such as *Anlieger frei*. The maximum is **30 km/h**, cycles may ride
side by side, and authorised motor traffic must neither endanger nor obstruct
them and may need to drive slower. Junction priority is still controlled by
lights, signs, or the general rule. [StVO Annex 2, signs 244.1–244.2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Bicycle zone

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-021, 1.4.41-171 -->

Sign 244.3 creates a bicycle zone. Cycles and electrokleinstfahrzeuge may enter;
other traffic requires a supplement. The maximum speed is **30 km/h**, cycling
side by side is permitted, and motor traffic must not impede cycling. Unless
otherwise regulated, junctions use *rechts vor links*. [StVO Annex 2, signs 244.3–244.4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Two-way cycling beside a one-way road

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-017, 1.4.41-022 -->

A cycling supplement with arrows in both directions warns that cycles—and
electrokleinstfahrzeuge where their cycle-facility rules apply—may approach from
both left and right. On a one-way road opened to contraflow cycling, expect a
cyclist coming toward you. When overtaking a cyclist inside a built-up area,
leave at least **1.5 m** lateral clearance. [StVO Annex 2, sign 220 provisions](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html) [eKFV §10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ekfv/__10.html)

## One-way streets and road markings

### One-way street positioning and parking

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-030 -->

On a signposted one-way road, position as far left as practicable before turning
left. Stopping and parking are permitted on the left where no other rule forbids
them, because there is no opposing traffic lane. [StVO §9(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html) [StVO §12(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

### Yellow temporary markings replace white markings

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-019 -->

Temporary yellow markings, commonly used at roadworks, replace the applicable
white markings. Follow the yellow route rather than the superseded white line.
[StVO §39(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__39.html)

### Restricted areas and solid lines

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-020-M, 1.4.41-165, 1.4.41-166, 1.4.41-167 -->

Do not drive on a hatched restricted area (*Sperrfläche*, sign 298). A solid
lane boundary (sign 295) may not be crossed or straddled. With a combined solid
and broken line, only traffic on the broken-line side may cross when safe—for
example to overtake. Outside built-up areas, parking on the carriageway of a
priority road is also prohibited. [StVO Annex 2, signs 295–298](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO Annex 3, sign 306](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

## Stopping, parking, and designated places

### Absolute versus restricted stopping prohibition

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-014, 1.4.41-015, 1.4.41-025 -->

Sign 283 prohibits voluntary stopping, including boarding and loading. Sign 286
prohibits parking: you may stop up to three minutes and may stop longer only
while actively boarding/alighting or loading/unloading. Traffic-required waiting
is not voluntary stopping. [StVO Annex 2, signs 283 and 286](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO §12(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

### Arrows show beginning, continuation, and end

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-023, 1.4.41-024, 1.4.41-027 -->

On a sign mounted on the right, an arrow pointing toward the carriageway marks
the beginning; an arrow pointing away marks the end; opposing arrows mark the
continuation, so the restriction applies before and after the sign. A
*werktags* plate applies on working days, including Saturdays unless that day is
a public holiday; Sundays and public holidays are excluded. [StVO Annex 2, signs 283–286](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO §39(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__39.html)

### Reserved disability parking and side strips

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-016, 1.4.41-134 -->

A parking sign restricted by the wheelchair symbol and permit details reserves
parking for authorised permit holders. Others may stop only where the accompanying
restriction still permits it—for example briefly to board or alight under sign
286. A supplement showing carriageway and hard shoulder extends a stopping ban
to both surfaces. [StVO Annex 2, signs 283–286 and 314](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Restricted-parking zones

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-135, 1.4.41-136 -->

Sign 290.1 begins a zone-wide restricted stopping prohibition; stopping up to
three minutes and active boarding/loading remain allowed unless another rule
forbids them. Sign 290.2 ends that zone, but it does not cancel an independent
local stopping prohibition. [StVO Annex 2, signs 290.1–290.2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Bus/tram stops and taxi stands

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-008, 1.4.41-109, 1.4.41-161 -->

Near sign 224, expect buses, trams, passengers, and children. Parking is
prohibited within **15 m before and after** the sign. Approach children ready to
brake. When a bus displays hazard warning lights at a stop, pass only at walking
speed with enough distance and without endangering passengers; stop if necessary.
[StVO Annex 2, sign 224](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO §20](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__20.html) [StVO §12(3) no. 4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-163 -->

At sign 229, expect taxis to stop and pull away and passengers to enter the road.
Other vehicles may not stop at the taxi stand except where expressly allowed.
[StVO Annex 2, sign 229](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

## Speed, overtaking, and route restrictions

### Maximum-speed signs and zones

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-124, 1.4.41-126, 1.4.41-151, 1.4.41-175 -->

Sign 274 sets the displayed maximum from the sign onward; **60** means no faster
than 60 km/h, while lower speed may be required. Sign 274.1 begins a 30 km/h
zone and sign 274.2 ends it. [StVO Annex 2, signs 274 and 274.1–274.2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-127, 1.4.41-172 -->

A *bei Nässe* plate activates the limit when the carriageway has a continuous
water film, not merely isolated damp patches; on a dry road that conditional
limit does not apply, though all other limits do. A time plate such as 22–6 h
applies the limit only during that period. [StVO §39(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__39.html) [StVO Annex 2, sign 274](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Minimum speed

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-128, 1.4.41-170 -->

Sign 275 sets a prescribed minimum speed—for example 60 km/h—when road, traffic,
visibility, and weather permit. Do not enter the signed road or lane if your
vehicle, load, or legal restriction cannot achieve it. Safety conditions always
justify slower travel. [StVO Annex 2, sign 275](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Overtaking prohibition and its distance/length plates

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-129, 1.4.41-130, 1.4.41-131 -->

Sign 276 prohibits motor vehicles from overtaking multi-track motor vehicles and
motorcycles with sidecars; a single-track motorcycle without sidecar or bicycle
is not within that sign's target class, though overtaking must still be safe. A
“3 km” plate with opposing arrows gives the prohibition's length; “200 m” without
those arrows places its start 200 m ahead. [StVO Annex 2, sign 276](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO §5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### Ending restrictions

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-132, 1.4.41-133 -->

Sign 278 ends the displayed maximum speed. Sign 282 ends all route-based speed
limits and overtaking prohibitions applying on that stretch, but does not end
stopping or parking restrictions. [StVO Annex 2, signs 278 and 282](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Traffic-calmed commercial-area combination

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-125 -->

Read every sign in the combination: the displayed zone speed (for example
20 km/h), restricted-parking zone, parking-disc requirement, and maximum parking
time all apply together. Expect dense and distracted pedestrian traffic and
reduce speed further when necessary. [StVO §39(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__39.html) [StVO §45(1d)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__45.html)

## Roundabouts and U-turns

### Signed roundabout

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-140-B, 1.4.41-141, 1.4.41-142, 1.4.41-143, 1.4.41-144 -->

With sign 215 below sign 205, traffic on the circular carriageway has priority.
Yield on entry, do **not** signal on entry, and signal right when exiting. Stopping
on the circular carriageway is prohibited; no general walking-speed rule applies.
[StVO §8(1a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__8.html) [StVO Annex 2, sign 215](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### U-turn prohibitions

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-150, 1.4.41-174 -->

Sign 272 prohibits turning around but does not by itself prohibit an ordinary
left turn. U-turning is also prohibited in a signed tunnel and on an Autobahn;
recognise any of those signs as a reason not to reverse direction.
[StVO Annex 2, sign 272](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO Annex 3, sign 327](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html) [StVO §18(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

### Winter-sport permission

<!-- questions: 1.4.41-164 -->

A danger sign with a “winter sports permitted” supplement means winter-sport
users may use the road as stated. Expect skiers or tobogganers and adapt speed
and attention; the supplementary plate defines the permission and hazard.
[StVO §39(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__39.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

## Dataset warning

The explanation for **1.4.41-012** uses “motorized bicycle,” “moped,” and “light
motorcycle” inconsistently. Read the pictogram: sign 255 prohibits *Mofas*; bans
for motorcycles or other motor vehicles use different symbols. [StVO Annex 2, signs 255 and 260](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

The explanation for **1.4.41-125** calls a commercial 20 km/h zone a
*verkehrsberuhigter Bereich*. That legal term normally refers to sign 325.1 and
walking speed. The depicted combination instead must be read by its individual
zone-speed and parking signs; do not transfer sign 325.1 rules to it.
[StVO Annex 3, sign 325.1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html) [StVO §45(1d)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__45.html)

## Common exam traps

- Reading the main sign but ignoring a time, distance, length, or exception plate.
- Confusing mandatory straight ahead with the rectangular one-way sign.
- Rolling through STOP or a static green arrow.
- Treating *Anlieger frei* as permission for through traffic.
- Confusing a footpath, bicycle street, and bicycle zone.
- Following white markings when temporary yellow markings replace them.
- Parking in a 15 m bus-stop zone or stopping at a taxi stand.
- Treating a maximum as a target or a minimum as unconditional.
- Signalling when entering a signed roundabout.
- Assuming sign 282 ends parking restrictions.

## Remember this

- **STOP:** wheels still at stop line; sight line only when no line exists.
- **Blue direction:** only shown movements; signal any real turn.
- **250:** no vehicles; cycles/motorcycles may be pushed.
- **283:** no stopping. **286:** no parking; brief/active stop allowed.
- **Yellow markings replace white.**
- **Fahrradstraße/Fahrradzone:** max 30; cycling has protection.
- **274:** maximum. **275:** minimum if conditions permit.
- **Roundabout:** yield in, no entry signal, signal out.
