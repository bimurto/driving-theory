# Probationary Period and Driver Fitness — Class B Study Summary

- Normal **Probezeit: 2 years**. An ordered advanced seminar (ASF) extends it
  to **4 years**.
- A serious A offence (or two B offences) during probation can mean a register
  entry and an ASF order. ASF builds considerate, risk-conscious driving and
  risk awareness; it does **not** erase points.
- Ignore an ASF order and the driving licence is withdrawn.
- Unsupervised learner practice on public roads is driving without the required
  licence: expect criminal prosecution and possible review of fitness to drive.
  Before a licence is issued, the authority may also withdraw the order
  authorising the applicant's examination.

**Remember:** *2 years; A/2B → ASF; ignore ASF → licence gone.*
