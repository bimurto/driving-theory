# Qualification and Ability of Truck Drivers — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

German licensing law treats the first period after a licence is issued as a
probationary period (*Probezeit*). It also requires applicants and drivers to be
personally and physically fit (*Eignung*) and to hold the required licence before
driving on public roads.

## Learning goals

- remember the normal probationary period;
- distinguish serious A violations from the escalation consequences in probation;
- understand what the advanced seminar (*Aufbauseminar für Fahranfänger*, ASF) is for;
- know the consequence of ignoring an ASF order; and
- recognise the licensing and criminal consequences of unlicensed practice.

## Core rules

### The normal probationary period is two years

<!-- questions: 2.8.01-001 -->

The normal *Probezeit* lasts **two years** from the grant of the first driving
licence. It is not normally one or three years. If the authority orders an ASF
because of a relevant offence, the probationary period is extended by two years
(to four years), not by three. [StVG §2a(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvg/__2a.html)

### A serious probation offence triggers ASF and a register entry

<!-- questions: 2.8.01-009 -->

Ignoring the mandatory Stop sign (*Zeichen 206: Halt. Vorfahrt gewähren*) and
endangering others is a serious offence (*A-Verstoß*). A relevant final offence
is recorded in the driving fitness register (*Fahreignungsregister*, FAER; often
called the points register), and one A offence—or two B offences—during
probation leads the licensing authority to order an ASF. The theory question's
correct consequences are therefore both the register entry and the seminar;
resitting the theory test is not the automatic consequence. [StVG §2a(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvg/__2a.html), [StVG §28](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvg/__28.html)

### ASF improves risk awareness; it does not erase points

<!-- questions: 2.8.01-010 -->

The ASF is intended to analyse the novice's driving behaviour and develop a more
considerate, risk-conscious attitude (*risikobewusstes Verhalten*) and better
danger awareness. It is a corrective training measure, not a points-reduction
course. [StVG §2a(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvg/__2a.html)

### Ignoring the seminar order costs the licence

<!-- questions: 2.8.01-011, 2.8.01-013 -->

If a novice does not complete an ordered ASF within the deadline, the licensing
authority must withdraw the driving licence (*Entziehung der Fahrerlaubnis*).
This is not replaced by an extra FAER point or an ordinary public fine. The
withdrawal consequence applies whether the worksheet calls the person a new
driver or learner driver. [StVG §2a(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvg/__2a.html)

### Driving without the required licence affects both prosecution and fitness

<!-- questions: 2.8.01-012, 2.8.01-014 -->

Practising practical driving on a public road without the required licence and
without an authorised instructor is not made lawful by being enrolled at a
driving school or by having a licensed passenger. Driving without a licence is
a criminal offence (*Fahren ohne Fahrerlaubnis*) punishable under StVG §21.
[StVG §21](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvg/__21.html)

The conduct can also raise doubts about the applicant's fitness to drive. The
licensing authority may refuse to proceed with the examination process or order
evidence/testing of fitness (*Eignungsprüfung*, including medical or
psychological clarification where the statutory conditions are met). A driving
licence requires the applicant to be fit and suitable; a driving-school
registration is not a substitute. [FeV §11](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__11.html), [FeV §2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__2.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- The two-year period is the normal period; an ASF order extends it by two
  years.
- ASF is triggered by one A offence or two B offences during probation, subject
  to the statutory process and finality of the underlying decision.
- ASF improves behaviour and risk awareness; it does not reduce FAER points.
- Failing to attend an ordered ASF leads to licence withdrawal. The person must
  later satisfy the requirements to obtain a new licence.
- Do not confuse *Fahrerlaubnis* (permission to drive) with the physical
  *Führerschein* card. The criminal offence concerns driving without the required
  permission.

## Common exam traps

- Choosing one or three years instead of two for normal *Probezeit*.
- Forgetting that one A or two B offences trigger ASF.
- Saying ASF removes points.
- Choosing a new fine or points instead of withdrawal for failing to attend.
- Assuming a licensed companion makes unsupervised learner practice legal.
- Treating unlicensed driving as only an administrative offence.

## Remember this

- **Probezeit: 2 years; ASF extends it to 4.**
- **1 A or 2 B offences → ASF.**
- ASF builds considerate, risk-conscious driving and risk awareness; it does not
  erase points.
- **Ignore ASF → driving licence withdrawn.**
- Driving without the required licence can mean **criminal prosecution plus a
  fitness review**.
