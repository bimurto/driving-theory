# Driving Operation, Physics, and Technique — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Speed dominates cornering force

The lateral/centripetal force needed to hold a vehicle on a curve is proportional
to **speed squared** and inversely proportional to curve radius:

`F = m × v² / r`

Doubling speed therefore requires four times the lateral force; halving the bend
radius doubles it. Available tyre grip must supply that force, so brake to a safe
speed **before** a tight bend and use smooth steering and acceleration within it.
The law expresses the practical result: speed must remain adapted to road,
traffic, visibility, weather, and personal/vehicle capability.
[StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [FahrschAusbO Annex 1, section 2.7 — driving physics](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fahrschausbo_2012/anlage_1.html)

### Dry wet drum brakes cautiously

Water inside a drum brake can reduce friction and braking response. When traffic
conditions permit, apply the brake **lightly and briefly while driving** so heat
dries the friction surfaces. First check mirrors, maintain enough distance, and
never surprise following traffic with unnecessary braking. Then confirm normal,
even response; if braking remains weak or uneven, stop safely and have the system
checked rather than continuing normally. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html) [StVO §23(1)–(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html) [StVZO §41](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__41.html)

## Common exam traps

- Thinking cornering force rises only in direct proportion to speed.
- Focusing on speed but forgetting that a smaller radius increases force.
- Braking hard in the bend instead of reducing speed beforehand.
- Drying brakes without checking following traffic.
- Continuing after the brake response remains impaired.

## Remember this

- **Curve formula:** speed squared, divided by radius.
- **Double speed:** four times the required lateral force.
- **Tighter bend:** reduce speed before entering.
- **Wet drum:** light controlled braking, traffic permitting; verify response.
