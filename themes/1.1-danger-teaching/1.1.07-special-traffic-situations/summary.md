# Special Traffic Situations — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

“Special” situations are ordinary rules under unusually small safety margins.
Large vehicles hide or occupy space, cyclists disappear into blind spots,
weather reduces grip, tunnels constrain escape, and animals or people move
unpredictably. The unifying method is to detect what is hidden, reduce speed,
create space, and give up formal priority whenever that is needed to avoid harm.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- anticipate the space and visibility problems created by large vehicles;
- resolve unclear junction, obstruction, and lane-change situations safely;
- protect cyclists, pedestrians, children, and two-wheeler riders;
- react correctly around buses, construction sites, wildlife, and accidents;
- apply the statutory tunnel rules and emergency routine; and
- choose collision avoidance over insistence on priority.

## Core rules

### Large and agricultural vehicles need more space than they appear to

<!-- questions: 1.1.07-001, 1.1.07-011, 1.1.07-013, 1.1.07-101, 1.1.07-118, 1.1.07-119, 1.1.07-146 -->

A lorry, combine harvester, or tractor may slow sharply and first swing left to
turn right. Its rear wheels cut a tighter path, attachments can swing outward,
and a high or wide load can conceal indicators and lights or fall during
braking and turning. Keep well back, never slip down the inside of a turning
vehicle, and overtake only when its intentions and the whole route are clear.
Turning must be announced and performed without endangering others; loads must
be secured against falling even under sudden braking or evasive action.
[StVO §9(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html) [StVO §22(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)

In farming areas also expect slow, excessively wide vehicles and mud on the
road. Headlights do not reliably reveal the full width of equipment or trailers,
so reduce speed before meeting them. Vehicle and load dimensions must remain
within the legal limits unless specifically authorised, but safe driving still
requires allowance for the actual width. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO §32(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__32.html)

### Do not infer priority from road appearance

<!-- questions: 1.1.07-002, 1.1.07-005-B, 1.1.07-006-B, 1.1.07-148, 1.1.07-149, 1.1.07-160-M, 1.1.07-161-M, 1.1.07-171, 1.1.07-175-M, 1.1.07-205-M -->

A wide, well-built road is not automatically the priority road. Read signs,
signals, and markings at each junction; if none establishes another rule,
**right before left** (*rechts vor links*) applies. Wet leaves or poor visibility
increase the need to approach slowly but do not change priority. [StVO §8(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__8.html)

With **Give way** (*Zeichen 205*), approach at moderate speed and wait for the
priority route—including a signed cycle crossing—to be clear. A road user may
still violate your priority or be unable to stop; brake and relinquish priority
when necessary, because avoiding danger takes precedence over enforcing it.
[StVO Annex 2, sign 205](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html) [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

### Restricted view means reduced speed and no speculative move

<!-- questions: 1.1.07-008, 1.1.07-014-M, 1.1.07-015-M, 1.1.07-017, 1.1.07-022, 1.1.07-103-M, 1.1.07-105-B, 1.1.07-132, 1.1.07-136, 1.1.07-138, 1.1.07-139, 1.1.07-142, 1.1.07-152-M, 1.1.07-153-M, 1.1.07-162-M, 1.1.07-166-M, 1.1.07-168-M, 1.1.07-169-M, 1.1.07-174-M, 1.1.07-176-M, 1.1.07-204-M, 1.1.07-206-M -->

A crest, bus, lorry, parked vehicle, bend, or construction vehicle can hide
oncoming traffic, signs, people, and vehicles pulling out. Slow enough to stop
within what you can see; on a dangerously narrow road, within half the visible
distance. Keep right and wait in your lane rather than entering an unseen or
occupied lane. [StVO §2(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

When leaving a parking row, driveway, property, or other road area, do so only
when danger to other road users is excluded; obtain guidance if necessary.
Large parked vehicles can prevent both sides seeing each other until very late,
so edge forward cautiously and be prepared to stop. [StVO §10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__10.html)

Expect doors to open, a parked or reversing vehicle to move, a cyclist to pull
around an obstruction, and an oncoming vehicle to overtake into your lane. If a
collision is developing, brake—including sharply when necessary—and hold a
predictable line rather than making a blind evasive lane change. Constant
caution and non-endangerment remain mandatory even when another person caused
the problem. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

### Protect children, cyclists, pedestrians, and two-wheelers

<!-- questions: 1.1.07-009, 1.1.07-016, 1.1.07-019-M, 1.1.07-020-M, 1.1.07-021, 1.1.07-023, 1.1.07-025-M, 1.1.07-111, 1.1.07-137, 1.1.07-141, 1.1.07-147, 1.1.07-155-M, 1.1.07-158, 1.1.07-164-M, 1.1.07-172-M, 1.1.07-201-M, 1.1.07-202-M, 1.1.07-203-M, 1.1.07-207-M -->

Near a school, reduce speed and remain ready to brake for children running or
cycling into the road. German law specifically requires drivers to reduce speed
and maintain braking readiness so that danger to children is excluded.
[StVO §3(2a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

Before turning, check mirrors and the blind spot directly. Let straight-ahead
cyclists, e-scooter users, and relevant oncoming traffic pass; show special
consideration to pedestrians and wait if necessary. Do not rely on cyclists
using the expected side, having lights, signalling, or maintaining speed: an
ending cycle facility or parked vehicle may force a sudden lateral move.
[StVO §9(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)

A cycle **advisory lane** (*Schutzstreifen*, Zeichen 340) may be crossed by motor
vehicles only when needed, without endangering cyclists, and may not be used for
stopping. Plan a right turn early without cutting off or blocking cyclists.
[StVO Annex 3, sign 340](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

E-bikes and motorcycles can be visually mistaken for slower vehicles; their
speed is often underestimated, while their riders are less protected in a
collision. Give them full observation, time, and space. When a cyclist or
scooter user enters your path unexpectedly, brake decisively rather than relying
on your priority. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

### Construction sites, accidents, and reported queues

<!-- questions: 1.1.07-107-B, 1.1.07-163, 1.1.07-170 -->

At roadworks, expect dirt, loose or uneven surfaces, narrowed lanes, equipment,
workers, and construction vehicles in the road. Obey temporary signs and reduce
speed for the actual conditions. Work affecting traffic must be officially
signed and controlled, but you must still react to the real hazard. [StVO
§45(6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__45.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

At an accident scene or where a queue has been reported, expect sudden braking,
pedestrians, gawpers, and stationary traffic beyond a bend or crest. Continue
monitoring traffic information, reduce speed early, and preserve stopping
distance. If approaching a queue, hazard warning lights may be used to warn
following traffic. [StVO §16(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

### Hazard lights and buses

<!-- questions: 1.1.07-010, 1.1.07-109-B, 1.1.07-157-M -->

Hazard warning lights can mark a broken-down vehicle or warn of danger such as
the approach to a queue. On scheduled-service and marked school buses they also
trigger special stop rules where their use has been ordered. [StVO §16(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

A bus approaching its stop with hazards on may not be overtaken. When stopped
there with hazards on, pass only at walking speed, at sufficient distance, and
without endangering or obstructing passengers; the rule also applies to
oncoming traffic on the same carriageway. [StVO §20(3)–(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__20.html)

Allow a scheduled-service or school bus indicating away from a marked stop to
move off, waiting if necessary. Anticipate people crossing behind it and the
rear of the bus swinging as it moves. [StVO §20(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__20.html)

## Situations and exceptions

### Wildlife: slow, brake straight, and expect more animals

<!-- questions: 1.1.07-113, 1.1.07-114, 1.1.07-117, 1.1.07-140, 1.1.07-150, 1.1.07-151, 1.1.07-156-M, 1.1.07-173 -->

Expect wildlife especially at dawn and dusk and on roads through forest habitat.
One deer may stop, turn back, or be followed by others. If an animal is near the
road, reduce speed and prepare to brake; do not make an unnecessary emergency
stop while danger is only potential. Once it enters your path and a collision
is imminent, brake hard, hold the steering wheel firmly and straight, and avoid
swerving into oncoming traffic or a tree. A horn may be used where you see
yourself or others endangered. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO §16(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

### Tunnel entry and normal driving

<!-- questions: 1.1.07-120, 1.1.07-122, 1.1.07-123, 1.1.07-125, 1.1.07-154, 1.1.07-167 -->

At sign **327 Tunnel**:

- use dipped headlights (*Abblendlicht*) even by day and in a lit tunnel;
- do not make a U-turn; and
- in an emergency or breakdown, use an emergency bay where available.

These are direct statutory commands. [StVO Annex 3, signs 327–328](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

Remove sunglasses before entry. Expect visual adaptation, reflections, monotony,
and amplified vehicle or ventilation noise. Keep a safe distance, orient toward
the right in two-way traffic without fixating on oncoming lights, and actively
note emergency exits, telephones, bays, and staff instructions.

### Queue, breakdown, or fire in a tunnel

<!-- questions: 1.1.07-121, 1.1.07-143, 1.1.07-144, 1.1.07-145 -->

At a tunnel queue, switch on hazard warning lights, keep a useful gap—about
**5 m** is the theory-test rule of thumb when stopped—and switch off the engine
if traffic comes to a standstill. Follow signs, loudspeaker messages, and tunnel
staff. The 5 m figure is operational exam guidance, not a distance stated in
StVO; the statutory general following-distance rule still requires enough space
to stop safely. [StVO §4(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html)

For a vehicle fire, leave the tunnel in the vehicle if that remains safely
possible. If not: stop as safely as possible, switch on hazards, turn off the
engine, leave the vehicle accessible with the key available for responders,
alert the tunnel control/fire alarm from an emergency station, attempt only an
extinguishing action that does not delay escape, and use the signed escape route.
Tunnel emergency bays and escape/safety facilities are part of the official
sign system. [StVO Annex 3, signs 327–328](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_3.html)

### Unclear traffic: preserve life, not “your right”

<!-- questions: 1.1.07-159-M, 1.1.07-165-M -->

Near a walker car park, expect pedestrians and vehicles entering or leaving. In
any unclear scene, reduce speed and be ready to stop. If another road user takes
your priority and collision is imminent, emergency braking is the correct
response. StVO §1 requires conduct that avoids harm and danger; priority is not
a licence to collide. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

## Common exam traps

- Passing on the inside when a lorry swings left before turning right.
- Assuming the larger or brighter road has priority.
- Entering a hidden gap because the other road user “should” wait.
- Using mirrors without a direct blind-spot check before turning.
- Blocking a *Schutzstreifen* while waiting to turn.
- Expecting cyclists to ride only in the legal or predictable direction.
- Braking for an animal but swerving into a more serious collision.
- Treating one deer as the end of the hazard.
- Using only daytime running lights in a tunnel instead of dipped headlights.
- Turning around in a tunnel or ignoring its safety signage.
- Insisting on priority when another person's error already creates danger.

## Remember this

- **Large vehicle:** stay back; expect slowing, swing-out, hidden lights, load.
- **Hidden view:** slow, keep right, and do not enter until clear.
- **Turning:** mirrors, shoulder check, protect cyclists and pedestrians.
- **Bus hazards:** approaching means no overtaking; stopped means walking speed.
- **Wildlife:** expect followers; brake hard and straight when collision is imminent.
- **Tunnel:** dipped headlights, no U-turn, learn the exits and emergency points.
- **Tunnel fire:** alarm, engine off, escape route; personal safety first.
- **Priority conflict:** brake and avoid the crash.
