# Overtaking — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### You need a clear, complete overtaking route

Overtake only if you can see that oncoming traffic will not be obstructed during
the **entire** manoeuvre and you can travel substantially faster than the person
being overtaken. Overtaking is prohibited in an unclear traffic situation or
where signs prohibit it. [StVO §5(2)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

Do not begin when a bend, crest, lorry, group of cyclists, junction, or other
obstruction hides any part of the required route. A lorry near a crossroads can
hide both cross traffic and signs. A cyclist may turn left or pull out around an
obstacle. Wait until after the blind section and until every necessary gap and
distance is available.

### Straight and open can still be deceptive

Drivers commonly underestimate how far their own overtaking manoeuvre will take,
underestimate an oncoming vehicle's speed, or overestimate its distance. The two
vehicles close the gap simultaneously. If there is doubt, let the oncoming
vehicle pass and reassess; a visually straight road does not relax the rule that
oncoming obstruction must be excluded for the whole manoeuvre. [StVO §5(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

Your overtaking distance includes pulling out, gaining enough clearance, passing
the full length of the other road user, and returning with a safe gap. A small
speed difference makes this route unacceptably long and fails the statutory
“substantially higher speed” condition.

### Check behind, signal, pass, and return safely

Before pulling out, check whether someone behind has already started overtaking.
Pulling out must not endanger following traffic; signal the move clearly and in
good time. While overtaking, do not obstruct the person passed, and return right
as soon as safely possible. [StVO §5(4)–(4a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

If you are being overtaken, do **not** accelerate. Increasing speed lengthens
the other driver's exposure to oncoming traffic and is expressly prohibited.
[StVO §5(6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### Abort when the safety assumptions disappear

Abandon the attempt promptly if unexpected oncoming traffic appears, the person
being passed accelerates, a vehicle pulls out, or a cyclist may swerve. Ease back
into the original safe following position rather than forcing the pass. This is
the practical consequence of the requirement to exclude danger to oncoming and
following traffic throughout the manoeuvre. [StVO §5(2), (4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### Continuous lines remain a hard boundary

Do not overtake if doing so would require even partially crossing a continuous
centre line (**Zeichen 295 Fahrstreifenbegrenzung**). Stay behind until both the
marking and the general overtaking rules allow a safe pass. [StVO Annex 2,
sign 295](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

## Numbers and formulas

### Minimum lateral distance to cyclists

When a motor vehicle overtakes a cyclist, pedestrian, or e-scooter user, the
statutory minimum lateral distance is:

| Location | Minimum |
|---|---:|
| Built-up area (*innerorts*) | **1.5 m** |
| Outside built-up area (*außerorts*) | **2.0 m** |

These are minima, not targets. Leave more for an unsteady, one-handed, heavily
loaded, young, or otherwise unpredictable cyclist, and at higher passing speed.
If the available road width permits only 50 cm—or otherwise cannot provide the
required safe distance—do not overtake. [StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Situations and exceptions

### Cyclists may need your lane space

Expect a cyclist to move left around a parked vehicle, queue for a left turn,
stop before mounting a kerb, wobble, or fall. Snow increases stopping distance
and instability for both bicycle and car. Reduce speed, increase following and
lateral distance, and do not pressure the cyclist with a close pass or horn.
Your speed must remain adapted to road and weather conditions. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html) [StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### Help resolve another driver's failed overtake

If an overtaking vehicle has misjudged the route and needs to return ahead of
you, reduce speed and create a gap when this safely prevents a collision. You
are not conceding that its manoeuvre was correct; you are applying the general
duty of caution and avoiding harm. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

## Common exam traps

- Starting because the road looks straight while misjudging closing speed.
- Looking only for oncoming traffic and not checking a vehicle behind.
- Passing with only a small speed advantage.
- Assuming a cyclist will hold a perfectly straight line or signal every move.
- Counting 1.5 m everywhere; outside built-up areas the minimum is 2 m.
- Treating the statutory minimum as sufficient despite visible instability.
- Crossing even part of a continuous line to “quickly” pass a cyclist.
- Continuing after the assumptions that made the pass safe have changed.
- Accelerating while another vehicle is overtaking you.

## Remember this

- **Unclear route:** do not overtake.
- **Small speed advantage:** do not overtake.
- **Before pulling out:** check behind and ahead; signal clearly.
- **Cyclist distance:** at least 1.5 m innerorts / 2 m außerorts; more if needed.
- **Continuous line:** do not cross it, even partly.
- **New danger:** abort early.
- **Being overtaken:** never accelerate; create space if it averts danger.
