# Load — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Load (**Ladung**) includes luggage, bicycles, roof carriers, and the equipment
used to secure them. It must remain stable during emergency braking or evasive
action, must not impair the driver, and must not create avoidable noise.

## Learning goals

- secure loads with suitable equipment and inspect it during breaks;
- understand roof-load effects on handling, braking, acceleration, and wind;
- apply the key height, projection, width, and lighting limits; and
- use carriers without covering lamps, number plates, or exceeding coupling limits.

## Core rules

### Secure every load and keep the vehicle safe

<!-- questions: 2.2.22-113, 2.2.22-114, 2.2.22-122, 2.2.22-124, 2.2.22-125, 2.2.22-127, 2.2.22-128, 2.2.22-131 -->

Stow and secure the load so that even full braking or sudden swerving cannot
make it slide, fall, roll, tip, or produce avoidable noise. Apply recognised
technical rules (**anerkannte Regeln der Technik**): for example, use lashing
straps (**Zurrgurte**), a luggage net (**Gepäcknetz**), and an anti-slip mat
(**Antirutschmatte**) as appropriate. Put heavy luggage low, secure it against
sliding, and keep the warning triangle accessible. If a strap tears, stop the
journey until it is replaced or the load is secured by other sufficient means.
These duties apply to small transporters too. [StVO §22(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)

The driver must ensure that the load cannot obstruct control or visibility and
that the vehicle remains road-safe. Check restraints again after starting and
at breaks, especially after vibration or a change in load.

### Roof loads change handling and have strict limits

<!-- questions: 2.2.22-102, 2.2.22-103, 2.2.22-104, 2.2.22-105, 2.2.22-123, 2.2.22-126, 2.2.22-130, 2.2.22-132 -->

Respect the manufacturer’s maximum roof load and the car’s permissible total
mass (**zulässige Gesamtmasse**). Attach a roof carrier and bicycles firmly,
check the fixings regularly, and drive more slowly than usual. A high roof load
raises the centre of gravity: the car leans more in bends, reacts more to
crosswinds, brakes over a longer distance, accelerates less strongly, and uses
more fuel. [StVO §22(1)–(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)

For a rear bicycle or pedelec carrier, confirm that the carrier is approved for
the vehicle, the trailer coupling has sufficient permissible vertical load
(**Stützlast**), tyre pressures are adjusted for the load, and the carrier and
bicycles are secure. Nothing may cover the lamps or number plate; fit the
required carrier lighting/plate arrangement if necessary.

### Overall dimensions and forward projection

<!-- questions: 2.2.22-111, 2.2.22-112, 2.2.22-117 -->

Vehicle and load together may not be wider than **2.55 m** or higher than
**4.00 m**. Up to **2.50 m** above the road, load may not project forward beyond
the vehicle; above 2.50 m, forward projection may be at most **50 cm**. [StVO
§22(2)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)

### Rear overhang must be marked

<!-- questions: 2.2.22-108, 2.2.22-109, 2.2.22-129 -->

Load may project behind the vehicle only within the statutory limit and must be
marked when required. A load projecting more than **1 m** beyond the rear
reflectors must carry a red flag or sign; in darkness or poor visibility use
the prescribed red light at the rear (and a white light at the front when the
load projects laterally beyond the vehicle lights). The marking light may be no
more than **1.50 m above the road**. A labelled rear load may project up to
**1.50 m**; for journeys up to 100 km, up to **3 m** is permitted under the
statutory conditions. [StVO §22(4)–(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)

### Waste transport needs the “A” plate

<!-- questions: 2.2.22-101 -->

White plates bearing a black **A** identify a vehicle transporting waste
(**Abfalltransport**). They are a marking requirement for the waste transport,
not a general load or hazard warning. [KrWG §55](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/krwg_2012/__55.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- The roof-load figure in the owner’s manual is a vehicle-specific maximum;
  never infer it from the carrier’s capacity alone.
- Marking, dimensions, and lighting rules apply even when the load is securely
  tied; securement does not legalise excessive projection. [StVO §22](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)
- A rear carrier must preserve the visibility of the registration plate and
  prescribed lamps; use an approved duplicate arrangement when the original is
  covered.
- Loose equipment such as a warning triangle must be both secured and readily
  accessible in an emergency. [StVO §22(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)

## Common exam traps

- Confusing roof-load capacity with permissible total mass.
- Forgetting that a high roof load increases braking distance and crosswind
  sensitivity.
- Continuing after a damaged lashing strap without re-securing the load.
- Treating “secure” as permission to exceed 2.50 m forward or 4 m overall height.
- Forgetting the 1.50 m marking-light height or the 40 cm lateral-light trigger.
- Allowing a bicycle carrier to cover the number plate or rear lights.

## Remember this

- **Secure:** no sliding, falling, rolling, tipping, or avoidable noise.
- **Roof:** follow the manual; high load means slower, longer braking, more lean,
  more crosswind effect, and higher consumption.
- **Dimensions:** 2.55 m wide, 4 m high; forward projection only above 2.50 m,
  then max 50 cm.
- **Marking:** rear overhang over 1 m needs marking; rear light red, front light
  white when required; marker light max 1.50 m high.
- **Carrier:** check approval, secure fixings, plate/lights, coupling vertical
  load, and tyre pressure.
