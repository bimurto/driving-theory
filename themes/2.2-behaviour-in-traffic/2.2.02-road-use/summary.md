# Road Use — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Road use (*Straßenbenutzung*) is governed by two habits: use the correct side
of the road and choose a lane that is legal and safe for the traffic situation.
When a restriction or road works prevents continued travel, obey the indicated
direction and wait for a safe, lawful passage.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- apply the keep-right rule (*Rechtsfahrgebot*) and the multi-lane exceptions;
- recognize when continuous use of a middle lane is permitted; and
- respond correctly to an obstruction on a one-way road (*Einbahnstraße*).

## Core rules

### Keep right, with a specific three-lane exception

<!-- questions: 2.2.02-006 -->

On roads with several lanes in one direction, vehicles should normally drive as
far right as possible (*möglichst weit rechts*). Outside built-up areas
(*außerhalb geschlossener Ortschaften*), however, a motor vehicle may use the
middle lane continuously when three lanes are marked for that direction and a
vehicle is present or travels occasionally in the right-hand lane. This remains
permitted even when the slower vehicles on the right are widely spaced. The
left-hand lane is not covered by this exception: outside built-up areas it is
reserved for temporary overtaking for cars, and trucks over 3.5 tonnes or
vehicles with trailers may use it only to position for a left turn.
[StVO § 2(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html) and
[StVO § 7(3c)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

This is a lane-use permission, not permission to pass on the right. Keep a safe
speed and change lanes only when endangering other road users is excluded;
signal every lane change in good time. [StVO § 7(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

### Obstruction on a one-way road: wait for lawful passage

<!-- questions: 2.2.02-101-M -->

The sign for a one-way street (*Zeichen 220 — Einbahnstraße*) requires vehicle
traffic to travel only in the direction shown by the arrow. If road works block
the route, wait until the works permit you to continue. Do not turn around or
reverse in a way that would send the vehicle against the prescribed direction:
the correct response is to remain stopped and follow the workers’ or traffic
control instructions. [StVO Anlage 2, Zeichen 220](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- On a three-lane, non-built-up road, the middle lane may be used continuously
  if traffic appears occasionally in the right lane; this does not make the
  left lane a continuous-driving lane. [StVO § 7(3c)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)
- The three-lane middle-lane rule applies to lanes marked for one direction;
  do not confuse it with a three-lane road carrying traffic in both directions,
  where opposing traffic changes the rules. [StVO § 7(3a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)
- A one-way designation still controls while an obstruction is present. Wait,
  rather than improvising a manoeuvre that violates the arrow direction.
  [StVO Anlage 2, Zeichen 220](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

## Common exam traps

- Selecting the right lane only because it is the default, while overlooking the
  lawful middle-lane exception on a three-lane road outside town.
- Assuming that widely spaced vehicles in the right lane cancel the exception;
  the rule says “even occasionally” (*auch nur hin und wieder*).
- Treating the left lane as available for continuous cruising.
- Turning around or reversing against the arrow on a one-way road when road
  works temporarily block the route.

## Remember this

- **Three lanes, outside town, traffic occasionally on the right:** the middle
  lane may be used continuously.
- **Left lane:** temporary overtaking (or positioning for a left turn in the
  heavy/trailer vehicles named in the rule), not continuous cruising.
- **One-way road blocked:** wait; the arrow direction still applies.
- **Every lane change:** signal and ensure no one is endangered.
