# Passenger Transport — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Every occupied seating position needs the required restraint

<!-- questions: 2.2.21-007, 2.2.21-103, 2.2.21-108, 2.2.21-111, 2.2.21-112, 2.2.21-124, 2.2.21-125, 2.2.21-126 -->

Passengers must use prescribed belts while driving (**Gurtanlegepflicht**), and
this applies on every journey, including short urban trips and rear seats. A
belt must lie flat and untwisted, be latched fully, and be pulled snugly; a
thick coat or routing the belt under both arms reduces its protective effect.
Replace a damaged belt. [StVO §21a(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__21a.html)

An unrestrained occupant can be thrown out or strike the vehicle interior. A
rear passenger can also be propelled into the driver or front passenger, even
at a relatively low impact speed. Airbags reduce some impact forces but cannot
hold an occupant in the correct position or replace a belt. [StVZO §35a(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__35a.html)

Before moving off, check that every passenger has fastened the available belt,
the mirrors give adequate traffic observation, and no object is loose on the
rear shelf. Keep the **Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil II** (registration
certificate Part II) in a safe place; the original Part I is the vehicle
document normally carried in the vehicle. [StVO §21a(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__21a.html)

### Children need a suitable, approved restraint

<!-- questions: 2.2.21-104, 2.2.21-105, 2.2.21-106, 2.2.21-107, 2.2.21-114, 2.2.21-122 -->

In a vehicle seat for which belts are prescribed, a child who has not yet
reached 12 years of age and is under 150 cm tall may be carried only in an
approved and child-suitable restraint (**Kinderrückhalteeinrichtung**). The
rule is cumulative: it applies when both the age and height conditions are
met. A five-year-old therefore needs a suitable tested child seat, not merely a
lap or three-point belt; the same requirement applies in built-up areas.
[StVO §21(1a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__21.html)

Select the seat for the child’s current height and weight and use it only on a
vehicle seat for which its approval and attachment method are intended. Follow
the seat and vehicle instructions and secure the child in the restraint.
Approved restraints must meet the applicable type-approval requirements.
[StVZO §35a(12)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__35a.html)

### Rear-facing infant carriers and passenger airbags

<!-- questions: 2.2.21-113, 2.2.21-115, 2.2.21-123 -->

An infant carrier (**Babyschale**) is rear-facing because it supports an
infant’s head and back in a crash. If it is installed on the front passenger
seat, the passenger airbag must be deactivated. A rear-facing child restraint
must not be fitted in front of an active passenger airbag; the warning symbol
exists to prevent this dangerous combination. A forward-facing approved child
restraint may be used there when installed correctly and permitted by the
vehicle and restraint instructions. [StVZO §35a(8), (13)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__35a.html)

### Head restraints, seating position, and airbags work together

<!-- questions: 2.2.21-117, 2.2.21-127 -->

Adjust each head restraint (**Kopfstütze**) to the occupant’s head height as
specified in the operating manual. It is not necessarily correctly adjusted at
the factory and it never substitutes for a belt. Sit upright with the back
against the seat; keep the airbag deployment zone clear of objects and people.
The restraint system can work as designed only when the occupant is in the
proper seating position. [StVZO §35a(2), (7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__35a.html)

### Truck load platforms are not passenger seats

<!-- questions: 2.2.21-102, 2.2.21-120 -->

People may not normally be carried on a truck’s loading platform or in its
load compartment (**Ladefläche/Laderaum**). The exceptions are people who must
perform necessary work there and construction-site personnel transported
within a construction site. A passenger being given a lift belongs in a
proper seat in the driver’s cab; no one may ride on a trailer’s loading area.
[StVO §21(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__21.html)

### Child locks prevent rear-door opening

<!-- questions: 2.2.21-121 -->

Activate the rear **child safety locks (Kindersicherung)** to prevent a child
from opening a rear door from inside while the vehicle is moving. Central
locking may lock doors, but it is not the specific child-proofing mechanism.
Check the doors are closed before setting off. This is part of the driver’s
duty to ensure that the vehicle and its equipment are safe before and during
the journey. [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### A persistent airbag warning requires prompt workshop attention

<!-- questions: 2.2.21-118 -->

If the airbag warning lamp stays on, treat it as a fault in the restraint
system. Arrange inspection and repair at a qualified workshop promptly; do not
pretend the warning is normal. The car need not automatically be taken out of
service immediately solely because the lamp is illuminated, but its safety
system may not provide the intended protection. [StVZO §35a(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__35a.html)

### Wheelchair users require a designated place and two restraint systems

<!-- questions: 2.2.21-128 -->

If a person remains seated in a wheelchair, the vehicle must have an approved
wheelchair space (**Rollstuhlstellplatz**). Secure the wheelchair with a
wheelchair restraint system (**Rollstuhl-Rückhaltesystem**) and secure the
person with a wheelchair-user restraint system
(**Rollstuhlnutzer-Rückhaltesystem**). Use both as specified by their
manufacturers; the systems must be engaged during travel when prescribed.
[StVZO §35a(4a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__35a.html) [StVO §21a(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__21a.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- The child-restraint rule has limited statutory exceptions, including certain
  taxi and bus situations; do not generalise those exceptions to an ordinary
  private car. [StVO §21(1a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__21.html)
- Belt-use exceptions include step-speed manoeuvres such as reversing and
  parking; normal driving is not an exception. [StVO §21a(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__21a.html)
- A rear-facing child restraint in front of an active airbag is prohibited;
  switch the airbag off only where the vehicle provides the approved facility.
  [StVZO §35a(8)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__35a.html)
- A loading-platform exception for necessary work does not make the platform a
  general passenger seat, and trailer loading areas remain prohibited.
  [StVO §21(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__21.html)

## Remember this

- **Belt:** flat, untwisted, fully latched, snug; every passenger uses it.
- **Child:** under 12 **and** under 150 cm → approved, suitable child restraint.
- **Infant carrier:** rear-facing; front passenger airbag off.
- **Airbag:** keep its deployment zone clear; sit upright; it supplements the belt.
- **Truck:** passengers in the cab, not on the load platform (except narrow work
  exceptions); never in a trailer’s load area.
- **Wheelchair:** approved space + wheelchair restraint + user restraint.
- **Warning lamp:** arrange prompt workshop inspection.
