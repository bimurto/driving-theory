# Immobilisation and Towing of Vehicles — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Secure a vehicle that is not recognised as a stationary obstacle

<!-- questions: 2.2.15-106, 2.2.15-107, 2.2.15-108, 2.2.15-110, 2.2.15-113, 2.2.15-115 -->

If a multi-track vehicle (**mehrspuriges Fahrzeug**) stops where approaching
traffic cannot recognise it in time as a stationary obstruction, switch on the
hazard warning lights (**Warnblinklicht**) immediately. Then place at least one
conspicuous warning sign (**auffällig warnendes Zeichen**) clearly visible at a
sufficient distance. On a fast road, use approximately **100 m**; use the
required warning equipment, normally a warning triangle (**Warndreieck**).
[StVO §15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15.html)

This is a sequence: lights first, then the triangle/sign. On a motorway, move
as far right as possible (normally onto the hard shoulder) and do not continue
to the next exit with a flat tyre if the vehicle cannot safely proceed. Keep
away from the traffic side while securing the scene. The triangle and its
distance are supported by StVO §15; the triangle and vest are prescribed
equipment under [StVZO §53a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__53a.html).

### Make yourself visible and use the right warning equipment

<!-- questions: 2.2.15-113, 2.2.15-115 -->

Put on the high-visibility vest (**Warnweste**) before leaving the vehicle to
secure a breakdown, especially behind a bend or outside a built-up area. A
passenger car must carry at least one warning triangle and a warning vest;
vehicles over 3.5 t additionally carry a separate warning lamp
(**Warnleuchte**). [StVZO §53a(1)–(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__53a.html)

On a motorway hard shoulder, place the warning triangle visibly at about 100 m,
and use a warning lamp at a sufficient distance when one is available or
required by the vehicle's equipment. Do not use reversing lights as a breakdown
warning; the legal breakdown signal is the hazard warning system and a warning
sign. [StVO §15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15.html)

### Hazard lights are for danger, not convenience

<!-- questions: 2.2.15-107, 2.2.15-108, 2.2.15-110, 2.2.15-112, 2.2.15-113, 2.2.15-115 -->

Hazard lights are mandatory for the qualifying breakdown in §15 and for both
vehicles throughout a tow (**beide Fahrzeuge Warnblinklicht**). They are not a
legal excuse for double-parking or for switching them on during every loading
or unloading operation. Outside these situations, use them only when the
vehicle endangers others or warns of a danger. [StVO §15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15.html),
[StVO §15a(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15a.html),
[StVO §16(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

## Situations and exceptions

### Towing is a Class B task, but the disabled vehicle determines the method

<!-- questions: 2.2.15-103, 2.2.15-114 -->

For the worksheet scenario, the towing driver needs at least driving licence
class B; class BE is not automatically required merely because one vehicle is
towing another. This is not the same as using a car-and-trailer combination:
the vehicle being towed is not a trailer. The driver must still be competent
and the combination must be safe under the vehicle's instructions.

Never generalise that every electric vehicle (**Elektrofahrzeug**) may be
towed. Follow the manufacturer's instructions: some vehicles require loading
because rolling can damage the electric drive or high-voltage system; others
permit only a specified method, speed, distance, or drive mode. Automatic-
transmission vehicles likewise may have strict limits (often neutral **N** and
a short distance). This manufacturer-specific limitation is consistent with
the general safety duty in [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html).

### Braking assistance may be gone

<!-- questions: 2.2.15-104, 2.2.15-111 -->

With the engine stopped, a servo-assisted brake (**Bremskraftverstärker**) may
no longer provide assistance. The foot brake still mechanically acts, but the
pedal requires much greater force and the resulting braking effect can be only
slight. The towed driver must therefore anticipate, brake early, and keep the
connection controlled. If possible, use a rigid tow bar (**Abschleppstange**):
it prevents the vehicles from colliding and transmits braking forces more
predictably. The statutory equipment and safety requirements for towing
connections are in [StVZO §43(1)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__43.html).

### Power steering may be gone

<!-- questions: 2.2.15-105, 2.2.15-114 -->

Engine-dependent power steering (**Servolenkung**) can fail with the engine.
The steering wheel is not locked, but steering becomes considerably heavier
and the vehicle is harder to control. The driver in the towed vehicle must keep
the ignition/steering unlocked as instructed, steer accurately, and expect
larger effort. Electronic assistance systems may also be unavailable; follow
the vehicle manufacturer's towing instructions. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

### Tow connection: distance, visibility, and tension

<!-- questions: 2.2.15-109, 2.2.15-112 -->

Use a suitable tow rope (**Abschleppseil**) or tow bar. The clear distance
between the towing and towed vehicles must not exceed **5 m**. Mark the rope
or bar so it is readily visible, for example with a red cloth (**roter
Lappen**). With a rope, keep it as taut as possible to prevent shock loading
and a collision; a bar naturally maintains separation. [StVZO §43(2)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__43.html)

Both vehicles must display hazard lights while towing. [StVO §15a(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15a.html)

### Motorway and motorcycle limits

<!-- questions: 2.2.15-112 -->

If a vehicle breaks down on a motorway, towing must leave the motorway at the
next exit. If the breakdown occurred elsewhere, a tow may not enter a motorway
to shorten the journey. [StVO §15a(1)–(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15a.html)

Motorcycles (**Krafträder**) may not be towed. Recover them by loading them on
a suitable vehicle or trailer. [StVO §15a(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15a.html)

## Remember this

- **Breakdown danger:** hazard lights immediately, then a visible warning sign.
- **Fast traffic:** triangle/sign about **100 m** away.
- Wear the **Warnweste** and stay out of the traffic lane.
- **Tow:** both vehicles' hazard lights; rope taut; connection marked; gap ≤ **5 m**.
- Failed engine assistance means heavy steering and a much harder brake pedal.
- A motorway breakdown exits at the next exit; an off-motorway tow may not enter.
- Motorcycles are not towed; load them. EVs and automatics require manufacturer guidance.
