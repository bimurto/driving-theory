# Parking time monitoring devices — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

German parking controls use a **parking meter (Parkuhr)**, **parking-ticket
machine (Parkscheinautomat)**, or **parking disc (Parkscheibe)**. Read the sign
and the device together: they determine whether parking is allowed, which proof
is needed, and the maximum permitted time.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- choose between a Parkschein and a Parkscheibe;
- use the remaining time on a running meter;
- set a parking disc correctly; and
- distinguish parking from the short activities exempt from activating the
  time-monitoring device.

## Core rules

### When a parking disc is required

<!-- questions: 2.2.13-001, 2.2.13-004 -->

Use a **Parkscheibe** when a traffic sign or supplementary sign requires it.
The disc must be clearly readable from outside and its pointer must be set to
the half-hour line following the time you stopped. A disc is also used when a
Parkuhr or Parkscheinautomat is defective: you may then park only up to the
displayed maximum parking duration (**Höchstparkdauer**). [StVO §13(1)–(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__13.html)

In a restricted stopping area (**eingeschränktes Haltverbot**) or a parking
management zone (**Parkraumbewirtschaftungszone**), a disc does not cancel the
underlying stopping or parking restrictions. A disc is not a general substitute
for a ticket at a working ticket machine. [StVO §13(1)–(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__13.html)

### Parking meters and remaining paid time

<!-- questions: 2.2.13-002 -->

At a **Parkuhr**, parking is permitted while the meter is running. If another
vehicle leaves time on a functioning meter, you may use the time still shown;
you do not insert additional money merely because you arrived later, and you do
not need a Parkscheibe. Once the meter has expired, the meter rule no longer
authorises parking. [StVO §13(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__13.html)

### Setting the parking disc

<!-- questions: 2.2.13-003 -->

Set the pointer to the next half-hour mark (**nächster halber Stundenstrich**),
not to the exact arrival time. For an arrival at **10:40**, set **11:00**. The
permitted duration is then counted from the displayed setting; it does not
change how the pointer is set. [StVO §13(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__13.html)

### Parking tickets: display and expiry

<!-- questions: 2.2.13-004 -->

At a working **Parkscheinautomat**, obtain a valid **Parkschein** and place it
in or on the vehicle so it is clearly legible from outside (**von außen gut
lesbar**). Do not exceed the parking period printed on it. A Parkscheibe is not
an alternative while the machine works; it is used if the machine is not
functioning, subject to the posted maximum duration. [StVO §13(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__13.html)

### Activities that do not require activation

<!-- questions: 2.2.13-101 -->

The parking-time device need not be activated for **getting in or out
(Ein- oder Aussteigen)** or **loading and unloading (Be- oder Entladen)**.
These are short stopping activities, not permission to leave the vehicle while
shopping or doing another errand. Other stopping and parking restrictions still
apply. [StVO §13(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__13.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- A defective meter or machine permits parking only until its stated maximum;
  use a clearly readable Parkscheibe. [StVO §13(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__13.html)
- Electronic payment or monitoring may replace physical activation where the
  system provides it; this does not apply when that electronic system is not
  functioning. [StVO §13(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__13.html)
- Getting in/out and loading/unloading are the specific device exemptions;
  quick shopping is parking and requires the applicable proof. [StVO §13(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__13.html)

## Common exam traps

- Setting the disc to the exact arrival time instead of the next half-hour.
- Treating a Parkscheibe as a replacement for a Parkschein at a working machine.
- Forgetting that a defective machine still imposes the posted maximum duration.
- Assuming leftover meter time requires a new disc.
- Calling a shopping stop “loading” or “getting out.”
- Hiding the Parkschein or Parkscheibe where it cannot be read from outside.

## Remember this

- **Sign requires it:** display a Parkscheibe.
- **Meter still running:** use the remaining time.
- **10:40 arrival:** set the disc to **11:00**.
- **Working ticket machine:** display a valid, readable Parkschein and leave by
  its expiry.
- **Defective device:** use a Parkscheibe and respect the Höchstparkdauer.
- **No activation:** only Ein-/Aussteigen and Be-/Entladen (subject to all other
  restrictions).
