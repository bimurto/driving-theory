# Use of Lanes by Motor Vehicles — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

German lane discipline combines the **keep-right rule** (*Rechtsfahrgebot*) with carefully defined exceptions for dense traffic, overtaking, turning, and the *Reißverschlussverfahren* (zipper merge). Select a lane early, scan adjacent lanes, and make every change predictable.

## Learning goals

- apply the keep-right rule and the built-up-area lane-choice exception;
- distinguish lawful overtaking from prohibited undertaking (*rechts überholen*);
- merge safely when an acceleration lane or lane ending creates a conflict; and
- use the hard shoulder (*Seitenstreifen*) only when signs expressly make it a lane.

## Core rules

### Keep right, with a built-up-area exception

<!-- questions: 2.2.07-002, 2.2.07-004 -->

Vehicles must generally drive as far right as practicable (*möglichst weit rechts*). On roads inside a built-up area (*innerorts*) with several marked lanes in one direction, vehicles up to 3.5 t permissible maximum mass may freely choose a lane, except on motorways. This is lane choice, not permission to create danger or ignore signs. [StVO §2(2) and §7(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

Outside built-up areas (*außerorts*), remain right and use a left lane only when the traffic situation justifies it—principally for overtaking, turning left, or the specific congestion exceptions. On three-lane roads, some vehicles may continuously use the middle lane where a vehicle is sometimes present to its right; heavy goods vehicles over 3.5 t and vehicles with trailers may use the far-left lane only to turn left. [StVO §7(1), §7(3c)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

### Overtake on the left; do not force a lane change

<!-- questions: 2.2.07-005, 2.2.07-008, 2.2.07-009, 2.2.07-010 -->

Overtaking (*Überholen*) is normally on the left. If you are in a middle lane and a slower vehicle is in the left lane, reduce speed and hold your lane; do not pass it on the right or move into an occupied right lane. A fast vehicle approaching from the right may move left to overtake, so anticipate its path and leave space rather than assuming it will remain right. [StVO §5(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

In congestion, vehicles may sometimes travel on the right slightly faster than on the left, but only with the statutory caution. This narrow exception does not turn ordinary right-side passing into a general right-overtaking permission. [StVO §7(2)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

### Lane changes and entering traffic require cooperation

<!-- questions: 2.2.07-004, 2.2.07-005, 2.2.07-006, 2.2.07-007, 2.2.07-009, 2.2.07-010, 2.2.07-011, 2.2.07-014-M -->

Change lanes only when danger to other road users is excluded; signal early and clearly. Check mirrors and the blind spot, then adjust speed so that an entering or merging vehicle can fit. A vehicle entering from an acceleration lane does not acquire priority over the through lane, but the through driver should facilitate a safe merge when the situation requires it; never accelerate into a shrinking gap. [StVO §7(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html) [StVO §10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__10.html)

When a lane ends or cannot be continuously used because of an obstacle, use the zipper merge immediately before the narrowing: one vehicle from the open lane, then one from the ending lane. Drivers in the open lane must enable this orderly alternating entry; the ending-lane driver should continue to the merge point instead of merging prematurely. [StVO §7(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

### Anticipate conflicts, especially at lane endings

<!-- questions: 2.2.07-005, 2.2.07-006, 2.2.07-007, 2.2.07-014-M -->

Read the whole traffic picture: a truck accelerating behind another truck may pull out, a car entering from the right may need the gap ahead, and a lane ending on the left can make the vehicle beside you move across. Slow down while staying in your lane when changing would be unsafe; allow the vehicle ahead to merge once the adjacent lane is clear. A voluntary waiver of priority is safe only when the other driver understands it. [StVO §1(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html) [StVO §11(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__11.html)

### Hard shoulder: only as signed

<!-- questions: 2.2.07-012-M, 2.2.07-013-M -->

The hard shoulder (*Seitenstreifen*) is not a normal travel lane. It may be used in a breakdown or emergency, but not for routine driving or overtaking. Sign 223.1 (*Seitenstreifen befahren*) expressly releases it as a lane; then use it like the right-hand lane. Signs 223.2 and 223.3 end or clear that permission. [StVO §18(8)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html) [StVO Annex 2, nos. 11–13](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- **Built-up area:** up to 3.5 t may choose among marked same-direction lanes, but this does not apply on motorways. [StVO §7(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)
- **Congestion:** a small right-side speed advantage can be lawful only in the conditions of §7(2)–(2a); it is not ordinary overtaking. [StVO §7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)
- **Lane ending:** zipper merge at the narrowing, with alternating vehicles. [StVO §7(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)
- **Hard shoulder:** follow the sign if opened as a lane; otherwise keep off it except for necessity. [StVO Annex 2, nos. 11–13](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

## Common exam traps

- Treating the built-up-area exception as applying to motorways.
- Passing a slower vehicle on the right in ordinary flowing traffic.
- Changing lanes without checking the blind spot or while another vehicle approaches.
- Merging early instead of using the lane that ends up to its narrowing point.
- Assuming the acceleration lane gives the entering vehicle priority.
- Using the hard shoulder merely to make progress.

## Remember this

- **Right first:** *möglichst weit rechts*.
- **Innerorts, ≤3.5 t:** free lane choice—except motorway.
- **Overtake:** normally left (*links überholen*).
- **Lane change:** signal and exclude danger.
- **Zipper:** alternate at the actual narrowing (*Reißverschlussverfahren*).
- **Shoulder:** emergency only unless sign 223.1 makes it a right lane.
