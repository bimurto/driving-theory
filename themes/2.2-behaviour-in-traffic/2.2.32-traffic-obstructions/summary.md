# Traffic Obstructions — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Traffic obstructions (**Verkehrshindernisse**) include dirt, water, fallen
vehicle parts, and lost equipment on a public road. The person who causes a
dangerous or obstructive condition must deal with it immediately and warn
others until it is gone.

## Learning goals

- recognise when soil or objects become a prohibited traffic obstruction;
- remove an obstruction when this can be done safely;
- secure and clearly mark the danger while it remains; and
- alert the police or road service when outside help is needed.

## Core rules

### The person who causes the obstruction is responsible

<!-- questions: 2.2.32-101 -->

Do not leave mud, soil, water, or other material on the road if it could
endanger or impede traffic. If, for example, you drive from a field or
building site and considerably soil the **Fahrbahn**, you—the person who
caused the condition—must remove the contamination without delay. The road
authority or neighbouring residents do not become responsible merely because
the road is public. [StVO §32(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__32.html)

Until cleaning is complete, make the hazard adequately visible (**ausreichend
kenntlich machen**). At night or whenever visibility requires it, a traffic
obstruction must be illuminated with its own light source or another approved
lighting device. [StVO §32(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__32.html)

### Fallen load or equipment: remove, secure, report

<!-- questions: 2.2.32-102 -->

If equipment, cargo, or a vehicle part falls onto the road and endangers other
road users, stop as soon as safely possible. Remove it yourself when the
location and traffic conditions make that safe; this fulfils the responsible
person’s duty to eliminate the obstruction. At the same time, secure and mark
the danger so approaching traffic can recognise it. [StVO §32(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__32.html)

If you cannot clear it promptly or safely—particularly on a motorway or other
fast road—protect yourself, warn approaching traffic from a safe position, and
immediately notify the police (**Polizei**, emergency number 110 where
appropriate) or the road service (**Straßenmeisterei**). Do not walk into a
live traffic lane to retrieve an object. The police’s official service portal
directs complex traffic incidents to the responsible police station, and
current breakdown guidance warns against collecting lost objects from the
roadway when this would expose you to traffic. [Police Onlinewache](https://portal.onlinewache.polizei.de/de/)
[ADAC breakdown guidance](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/unfall-schaden-panne/panne/autopanne-tipps/)

## Situations and exceptions

- **Safe removal first:** On a quiet, low-speed road, remove the material or
  object without creating a second danger, then check that the road is clear.
  On a high-speed road, personal safety takes priority; secure and report it
  instead of entering the carriageway. [StVO §32(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__32.html)
- **Marking is temporary:** warning traffic does not replace the duty to
  eliminate the condition; remove the obstruction as soon as possible.
  [StVO §32(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__32.html)
- **Lighting:** a warning is required only when necessary under the visibility
  conditions, but an unlit obstacle at night is not adequately identified.
  [StVO §32(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__32.html)

## Common exam traps

- Choosing the road authority or local residents when you caused the mud.
- Thinking that reporting the fallen load alone removes your duty to clear it.
- Trying to collect an object from a motorway lane and becoming another hazard.
- Forgetting to secure and mark the site while waiting for help.
- Assuming daylight rules are enough when the obstruction is hard to see at
  night or in poor visibility.

## Remember this

- **Cause it? Clear it:** the person responsible must remove dangerous dirt or
  objects immediately.
- **Until clear:** secure and adequately mark the obstruction; illuminate it
  when necessary.
- **Unsafe to remove:** protect yourself, warn traffic, and call the police or
  road service immediately.
