# Motorways and Motorways — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

German **Autobahnen** and **Kraftfahrstraßen** are designed for continuous motor traffic. Their access, lane use, stopping, reversing, emergency-lane, joining, and leaving rules are therefore stricter than ordinary roads. The English worksheet uses “motorway” and “clearway”; in German, distinguish **Autobahn** (sign 330.1) from **Kraftfahrstraße** (sign 331.1).

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- distinguish the 60-km/h access condition from the 130-km/h advisory speed;
- keep right and overtake safely without pressuring another driver;
- form and use a **Rettungsgasse** (emergency corridor);
- join and leave via acceleration/deceleration lanes; and
- handle jams, breakdowns, wrong-way drivers, missed exits, towing, and traffic information.

## Core rules

### Access and speed: 60 km/h eligibility, 130 km/h recommendation

<!-- questions: 2.2.18-003, 2.2.18-010 -->

Only motor vehicles whose design-based maximum speed (**bauartbedingte Höchstgeschwindigkeit**) is **more than 60 km/h** may use an Autobahn or Kraftfahrstraße; the same condition applies to a trailer being towed. The vehicle documents record the relevant approved capability. This is an access requirement, not a recommended travelling speed. [StVO §18(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

For cars and motorcycles, **130 km/h** is the general Autobahn advisory speed (**Autobahn-Richtgeschwindigkeit**), applying under favourable road, traffic, visibility, and weather conditions. A posted maximum still overrides it, and conditions may require less. [Autobahn-Richtgeschwindigkeits-V §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/babrigeschwv_1978/__1.html)

### Keep right; overtake only as long as needed

<!-- questions: 2.2.18-007, 2.2.18-021-M -->

On a multi-lane road, use the right-hand lane whenever traffic density does not justify another lane. The left lane is for overtaking, so remain there only for the time needed to pass; following a vehicle that is itself overtaking can be part of the same overtaking manoeuvre. Do not stay left merely because nobody is currently behind you. [StVO §2(2), §7(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

Choose the lane that avoids obstructing others. If vehicles are entering on the right while a vehicle that has just overtaken is on the left, remaining in the middle lane may be correct; changing again can create a conflict. Check mirrors and blind spots before every lane change. [StVO §7(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

### Safe distance and courteous overtaking

<!-- questions: 2.2.18-006 -->

Maintain a sufficient **Sicherheitsabstand** so that you can brake if the vehicle ahead moves unexpectedly. When another vehicle has begun overtaking from the right, allow it to complete the manoeuvre and reduce speed if necessary. Never tailgate, sound the horn, or flash the headlights to force it out of the lane; those actions can intimidate and endanger. [StVO §4(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__4.html) [StVO §16](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

### Joining: the through lane has priority

<!-- questions: 2.2.18-012, 2.2.18-017-M, 2.2.18-023-M -->

On an **Einfädelungsstreifen** (acceleration lane), you may travel faster than traffic on the through lane. Use the available lane to accelerate, select a safe gap, and merge without forcing through-lane traffic to brake. The through lane has priority (**Vorfahrt**); “accelerate and merge ahead” is appropriate only when the gap and safety distance make it safe. Reduce speed to let a joining vehicle in when the situation requires it. [StVO §7a(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7a.html) [StVO §18(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

Do not treat the end of the acceleration lane as a reason to stop automatically. Adjust speed early and merge when possible; stopping on the acceleration lane is prohibited. [StVO §12(1)(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

### Leaving: signal early, decelerate on the exit lane

<!-- questions: 2.2.18-014, 2.2.18-018-M, 2.2.18-022 -->

Observe traffic behind you and signal right in good time—training questions commonly use **at least 300 m before the exit**. Move onto the **Ausfädelungsstreifen** first, then reduce speed there and take the curve at a controlled speed. Do not queue on the hard shoulder or stop on the main carriageway because an exit is congested; continue to the next exit. [StVO §7a(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7a.html) [StVO §18(10)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

### Emergency corridors in stationary or crawling traffic

<!-- questions: 2.2.18-011, 2.2.18-013, 2.2.18-015-M, 2.2.18-019 -->

As soon as vehicles on an Autobahn or an out-of-town road with at least two lanes in one direction are stationary or moving at walking pace, form a **Rettungsgasse**. On a three-lane carriageway, the corridor is between the far-left lane and the lane immediately to its right: left-lane traffic moves as far left as possible; all other traffic moves as far right as possible. It is not formed on the hard shoulder (**Seitenstreifen**). [StVO §11(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__11.html)

Start forming it before the jam is fully stopped, keep the corridor open, and follow the lane-position instruction even when no emergency vehicle is yet visible. [StVO §11(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__11.html)

### Prohibited manoeuvres and stopping

<!-- questions: 2.2.18-009, 2.2.18-014, 2.2.18-102, 2.2.18-103, 2.2.18-105 -->

On an Autobahn, entry and exit are permitted only at marked connection points (**Anschlussstellen**); on a Kraftfahrstraße, only at intersections or junctions. The through carriageway has priority. U-turns (**Wenden**), reversing (**Rückwärtsfahren**), and stopping (**Halten**) are prohibited; the stopping ban includes the hard shoulder. A farm track is not a permitted exit. If you miss an exit, continue forward to the next one—never reverse or turn around. [StVO §18(2), (3), (7), (8), (10)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

In darkness, the special Autobahn rule means dipped-headlight range does not always cap speed when a clearly recognisable taillight and sufficient distance are present, or guidance reflectors plus other light reveal the road and obstacles in time. This exception does not remove the general duty to choose a safe speed. [StVO §18(6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Jams, hazard lights, and a wrong-way driver

<!-- questions: 2.2.18-024-M, 2.2.18-016-M -->

Approach a queue (**Stau**) slowly, keep distance, and remain in your lane. Do not ride between vehicles or use the hard shoulder. A vehicle at the end of a queue or in another dangerous slow-traffic situation may use hazard warning lights (**Warnblinkanlage**) to warn following traffic. [StVO §16(1)(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

If a wrong-way driver (**Falschfahrer**) is approaching, reduce collision risk by moving as far right as possible, warn with horn and headlights when this can be done safely, and notify the police. Do not make a sudden manoeuvre into the wrong-way vehicle’s path. [StVO §1(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html) [StVO §16](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

### Breakdown and towing

<!-- questions: 2.2.18-101 -->

If a vehicle breaks down, leave the carriageway where possible, warn approaching traffic, and use the hard shoulder only for the emergency—not for waiting at an exit or bypassing a queue. When towing on an Autobahn, both vehicles use hazard lights and the tow may continue only to the next exit; leave the Autobahn there. [StVO §15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15.html) [StVO §18(8)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

### Lane-change assistance and traffic information

<!-- questions: 2.2.18-104-M, 2.2.18-215 -->

Lane-change assist (**Spurwechselassistent**) is a warning aid, not a substitute for mirrors, shoulder checks, signalling, and a safe decision. Without steering override it can warn of a vehicle in the target middle lane, but it does not itself control speed or make the manoeuvre safe. The driver remains responsible. [StVO §1(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

Keep traffic information (**Verkehrsfunk**) available when travelling long distances: reports can warn of congestion, collisions, roadworks, and wrong-way drivers, allowing earlier route or speed decisions. Listening is not itself a special statutory duty, but the information supports the general duty of care and avoidance of danger. [StVO §1(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

## Common exam traps

- Confusing “more than 60 km/h” design capability with the 130-km/h advisory speed.
- Treating the left lane as a cruising lane or pressuring an overtaking vehicle.
- Forgetting that the through lane has priority when joining.
- Forming the Rettungsgasse between left and middle lanes on three-lane roads, not on the hard shoulder.
- Stopping, reversing, turning, or waiting on the hard shoulder.
- Braking on the main carriageway because an exit is congested; use the next exit.
- Assuming a driver-assistance warning replaces observation and responsibility.

## Remember this

- **Autobahn/Kraftfahrstraße:** vehicle design speed **more than 60 km/h**.
- **Richtgeschwindigkeit:** **130 km/h** for cars and motorcycles under favourable conditions.
- **Keep right; left only to overtake.**
- **Join:** acceleration lane may be faster; through traffic has priority.
- **Leave:** signal early, decelerate on the exit lane.
- **Jam:** left lane far left, every other lane far right; corridor between them.
- **Autobahn:** no stopping (including hard shoulder), reversing, or U-turns.
- **Breakdown towing:** hazard lights on both vehicles; leave at next exit.
