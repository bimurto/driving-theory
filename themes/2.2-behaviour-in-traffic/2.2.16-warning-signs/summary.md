# Warning Signs — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Flashing headlights to announce overtaking

<!-- questions: 2.2.16-001 -->

Outside a built-up area (*außerhalb geschlossener Ortschaften*), a driver may
announce an intended overtaking manoeuvre with a short sound or light signal;
this is the legal basis for briefly flashing the headlights (*Lichthupe*).
[StVO §5(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

The same rule applies in daylight and darkness outside built-up areas. If
using high-beam flashing, do not dazzle oncoming drivers (*Blendung vermeiden*).
[StVO §5(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

Inside a built-up area, this overtaking signal is not permitted under §5(5).
The signal does not make overtaking safe by itself: overtaking still requires
that the whole manoeuvre can be completed without endangering oncoming traffic
and that the driver has sufficient speed difference.
[StVO §5(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### Hazard warning lights for a hidden breakdown

<!-- questions: 2.2.16-101 -->

If a multi-track vehicle (*mehrspuriges Fahrzeug*) breaks down (*Liegenbleiben*)
where approaching traffic cannot recognise it in time as a stationary obstacle,
switch on the hazard warning lights immediately (*sofort Warnblinklicht*).
Then place a conspicuous warning sign, normally the warning triangle
(*Warndreieck*), at a sufficient distance; on fast roads the StVO gives about
100 m as the example distance. [StVO §15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15.html)

The reason is visibility: the hazard lights warn approaching road users before
they reach the hidden obstruction. This is a breakdown rule, not permission to
use hazard lights whenever stopping is inconvenient.

### Hazard warning lights while being towed

<!-- questions: 2.2.16-101 -->

During towing (*Abschleppen*), both the towing vehicle and the vehicle being
towed must switch on their hazard warning lights (*beide Fahrzeuge*).
[StVO §15a(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15a.html)

Briefly stopping or double-parking (*kurzes Halten in zweiter Reihe*) is not,
by itself, a reason to activate hazard warning lights. Outside the specific
cases such as a breakdown or towing, §16(2) permits them only when the vehicle
endangers others or the driver is warning others of a danger; examples include
approaching a traffic jam or unusually slow travel on a motorway.
[StVO §16(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- Use the overtaking flash only outside built-up areas, and keep it short; never
  dazzle oncoming traffic. [StVO §5(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)
- A hidden breakdown requires immediate hazard lights and a warning sign; the
  warning lights do not replace securing the scene. [StVO §15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15.html)
- Towing is the special case in which both vehicles display hazard lights.
  [StVO §15a(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15a.html)
- A traffic jam or unusually slow travel on a fast road can also justify hazard
  lights when others must be warned, but ordinary convenience stopping cannot.
  [StVO §16(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)

## Remember this

- **Lichthupe for overtaking:** outside built-up areas (*außerorts*), by day or
  night; never dazzle anyone. [StVO §5(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)
- **Hidden breakdown:** hazard lights immediately, then warning triangle at a
  suitable distance. [StVO §15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15.html)
- **Towing:** both vehicles use hazard lights. [StVO §15a(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__15a.html)
- **Convenience double-parking:** not enough on its own; hazard lights are for
  danger warnings. [StVO §16(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__16.html)
