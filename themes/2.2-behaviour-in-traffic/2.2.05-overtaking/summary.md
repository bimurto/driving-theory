# Overtaking (Überholen) — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Overtaking is a planned movement past a vehicle travelling in the same
direction. It is normally made on the left and is permitted only when the
whole manoeuvre can be completed safely. The driver must read the road ahead,
check behind and beside the vehicle, signal, pass with a substantial speed
difference, and return right as soon as possible. A manoeuvre must be abandoned
if the safety conditions disappear.

## Learning goals

- decide whether an overtaking manoeuvre is legal and safe;
- calculate the approximate distance needed to pass safely;
- use mirrors, blind-spot checks, indicators, and speed correctly;
- recognise solid lines, signs, crests, curves, oncoming traffic, cyclists,
  motorcycles, tractors, and buses as hazards; and
- distinguish overtaking (Überholen) from carefully passing a stopped or
  stopping vehicle (Vorbeifahren).

## Dataset warning

Question 2.2.05-005 uses an exam worksheet rule of thumb that doubles the
calculated overtaking distance near a crest. This is not a statutory 800 m

## Core rules

### The legal safety test comes first

<!-- questions: 2.2.05-007, 2.2.05-011, 2.2.05-012, 2.2.05-016-M, 2.2.05-017, 2.2.05-102-M, 2.2.05-103-M -->

Overtake on the left. Start only when you can see that, throughout the entire
manoeuvre, oncoming traffic will not be impeded and you will travel at a
substantially higher speed than the vehicle being overtaken. Overtaking is
prohibited when the traffic situation is unclear or when signs 276 (no
overtaking) or 277 (no overtaking by heavy vehicles) prohibit it. [StVO §5(1)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

You must finish and return to the right before a solid centre/edge line that
you would have to cross, and before a no-overtaking restriction begins. A
broken centre line (Leitlinie, Zeichen 340) can be crossed only when the whole
manoeuvre remains safe; it is not permission to ignore a sign or unclear
traffic. [StVO §5(2)–(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

If a crest (Kuppe), bend, or other obstruction hides the road, assume that
oncoming traffic may be approaching. A vehicle already alongside cannot safely
“make it” by accelerating into a restriction; cancel the manoeuvre and merge
back when safe. These principles explain why oncoming traffic and a reckless
following overtaker can make the situation hazardous, while a tractor safely
off the carriageway need not be a hazard. [StVO §5(2)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### Prepare, signal, check, pass, and return

<!-- questions: 2.2.05-010, 2.2.05-020-M -->

Before moving out, check the inside mirror, outside mirror, and blind spot
(Schulterblick). The rear and side traffic must be clear enough that the
evasive movement cannot endanger anyone. Announce both moving out and moving
back in good time and clearly with the direction indicator
(Fahrtrichtungsanzeiger). [StVO §5(4), §5(4a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

Pass at a clearly higher speed, but never exceed the applicable speed limit.
“Substantially higher” means the speed difference should keep the time spent
alongside the other vehicle short; it does not authorise breaking a posted
limit. After passing, return to the right as soon as possible without cutting
off or hindering the other driver. [StVO §3(3), §5(2), §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

On a multi-lane road, the general keep-right rule (Rechtsfahrgebot) still
applies. A driver who has completed an overtake must merge right; a vehicle
approaching in the overtaking lane must also correct its lane position when
safe. Lane changes require no danger to others and a clear signal. [StVO §2(2), §7(1), §7(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__7.html)

### Give vulnerable road users extra space

<!-- questions: 2.2.05-013, 2.2.05-014-M -->

Expect a cyclist (Radfahrender) to move into the carriageway, especially when
passing a truck or an obstruction. Reduce speed and leave room rather than
assuming the cyclist will remain at the edge. When a motorcyclist is overtaking
in your lane or is in a blind spot, delay your own movement and avoid closing
the gap; two users cannot safely occupy the same lateral space. The required
side clearance when a motor vehicle overtakes a cyclist, pedestrian, or
e-scooter is at least 1.5 m inside built-up areas and 2 m outside them. [StVO §5(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

### Long vehicles hide danger and need more distance

<!-- questions: 2.2.05-018, 2.2.05-021-M -->

A truck with a trailer (Lkw mit Anhänger) is longer, so the distance and time
needed to pass are substantially greater. Its bodywork can conceal vehicles,
cyclists, or an obstruction ahead; do not pull out merely because the first
part of the truck appears clear. [StVO §5(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

Do not overtake a tractor merely because its driver indicates or gestures that
you may pass. If the tractor appears to turn into a field track, remain behind
until it has turned; a rear implement can swing or cut across the road. An
indicator is information, not a release from your own duty of care. [StVO §1(2), §5(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

### Special restrictions for buses and trams

<!-- questions: 2.2.05-019, 2.2.05-017 -->

Do not overtake a scheduled bus (Linienbus) or marked school bus that is
approaching a stop with its hazard warning lights on. If it is stopped at the
stop with warning lights, pass only at walking speed (Schrittgeschwindigkeit),
at a distance that excludes danger to boarding or alighting passengers; be
ready to wait. The same careful passing rule applies to trams and buses at a
stop, including when traffic approaches in the opposite direction. [StVO
§20(1)–(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__20.html)

Rail vehicles are normally passed on the right. They may be passed on the left
only when their rails lie too far to the right; on a one-way carriageway they
may also be passed on the left. [StVO §5(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Numbers and formulas

### Worksheet estimate for an overtaking path

<!-- questions: 2.2.05-005 -->

For the worksheet scenario (100 km/h overtaking a 70 km/h truck), the exam
explanation estimates the distance with:

```text
s = v1 / (v1 − v2) × L
L = vehicle lengths + front safety gap + rear safety gap
```

Using approximately 12.5 m for the truck, 4.5 m for the car, and 50 m at each
end gives `L ≈ 117 m`; `100/(100−70) × 117 ≈ 390 m`, rounded to **400 m**.
The worksheet then doubles this in an unclear area near a crest, producing the
exam answer **800 m**. This is a rule of thumb from the question explanation,
not a general legal distance; the binding rule remains that the entire pass
must be visible and must not impede oncoming traffic. [Worksheet explanation](https://www.clickclickdrive.de/fragenkatalog/en/2.2.-behaviour-in-traffic/2.2.05-overtaking/2.2.05-005) · [StVO §5(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- The driver being overtaken must not accelerate. If necessary, a slow driver
  must reduce speed or wait so several immediately following vehicles can pass;
  this does not permit unsafe use of a motorway hard shoulder. [StVO
  §5(6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)
- Outside built-up areas, a short horn or light signal may announce an intended
  overtake, but high beam must not dazzle oncoming drivers. Signals do not make
  an unsafe overtake legal. [StVO §5(5), §16(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)
- Passing a stationary obstacle or stopped vehicle is treated under
  Vorbeifahren; if you move out, give way to oncoming traffic and signal the
  movement like an overtake. [StVO §6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__6.html)
- On a one-way road, a tram may be passed on the left, but all other safety,
  signaling, and lane-change rules still apply. [StVO §5(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Common exam traps

- Treating a broken line as a guarantee that overtaking is safe.
- Starting without checking the blind spot or forgetting to signal the return.
- Accelerating beyond the speed limit to create a larger speed difference.
- Continuing beside a vehicle when a solid line, sign, crest, or oncoming traffic
  means the manoeuvre cannot finish safely.
- Following another car straight into an overtake before it has returned right.
- Assuming a bus driver’s hazard lights or a tractor driver’s signal gives you
  priority to pass.
- Forgetting that trailers increase length and that trucks can hide traffic.
- Confusing “pass on the right” for a tram with the normal left-side overtaking
  rule.

## Remember this

- **Left, visible, substantially faster, safely clear:** the four-part test for
  a legal overtake.
- Check mirrors and blind spot, signal out, pass without exceeding the limit,
  signal and merge right as soon as safe.
- Never begin when the whole path cannot be seen; finish before a solid line or
  no-overtaking sign.
- Cyclists and motorcyclists can enter your path or blind spot—slow down and
  give space.
- Do not overtake an approaching bus with hazard lights; pass a stopped one at
  walking speed and be ready to wait.
- A trailer, crest, curve, hidden traffic, or oncoming vehicle can turn an
  apparently open pass into an emergency.
