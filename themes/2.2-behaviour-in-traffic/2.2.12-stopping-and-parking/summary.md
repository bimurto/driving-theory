# Stopping and Parking — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Where stopping is prohibited

<!-- questions: 2.2.12-001 -->

Do not stop (*Halten verboten*) at narrow or confusing road sections, on sharp
bends, on acceleration or deceleration lanes, on level crossings, or in front
of officially marked fire access routes. Stopping is also forbidden on an
Autobahn or a *Kraftfahrstraße*, including the hard shoulder (*Seitenstreifen*);
use only a marked parking area. [StVO §12(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html),
[StVO §18(8)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

### Where parking is prohibited

<!-- questions: 2.2.12-003 -->

Parking (*Parken*) is prohibited in front of property driveways, and on a
narrow carriageway also opposite them; at taxi ranks; where marked parking
spaces cannot be used; and on or up to 5 m before a pedestrian crossing
(*Fußgängerüberweg*). It is also prohibited at lowered kerbs and in the other
locations listed in §12(3). [StVO §12(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html),
[StVO Anlage 2, no. 66](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Parking-space priority and safe manoeuvring

<!-- questions: 2.2.12-005, 2.2.12-101-B, 2.2.12-107-M, 2.2.12-108-M, 2.2.12-109-M -->

At a parking space, priority (*Vorrang*) belongs to the vehicle that reaches it
first. That priority remains when the driver passes the space to reverse in or
performs other manoeuvres needed to enter it, and when waiting for a space that
is about to become free. This does not justify a collision: if another driver
has signalled first or is already established, give up the space and avoid the
conflict. [StVO §12(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

Before reverse-parking, check oncoming and following traffic and expect a
vehicle to leave another space. Signal the manoeuvre, but do not use hazard
lights as a substitute for observation or ordinary direction signalling.
During the manoeuvre, keep other road users safe under the general care duty.
[StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

### Sidewalk parking and road position

<!-- questions: 2.2.12-102, 2.2.12-204 -->

Parking on a sidewalk (*Gehweg*) is allowed only where sign 315 or a parking
marking expressly permits it; the vehicle must be a motor vehicle with a
permissible total mass (*zulässige Gesamtmasse*) of no more than **2.8 t** for
the relevant exam rule. Otherwise use the right hard shoulder or, if it is not
sufficiently firm, pull close to the right edge of the carriageway. [StVO
§12(4), Anlage 3 sign 315](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

When parking beside a solid lane-boundary line (*Fahrstreifenbegrenzung*), at
least **3 m** of lane must remain between the parked vehicle and the line.
This keeps the through lane usable. [StVO Anlage 2, no. 68](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Distance before signs and signals

<!-- questions: 2.2.12-104, 2.2.12-203 -->

Leave at least **10 m** before a traffic light if stopping there would conceal
the light. The same 10-m no-stopping clearance applies before an *Andreaskreuz*
(diagonal cross, sign 201) or Stop sign (*Zeichen 206*) when your vehicle would
conceal it. You may stop before a Stop sign to obey it; do not stop beyond it.
[StVO §37(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__37.html),
[StVO Anlage 2, signs 201 and 206](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

### Special trailer parking limits

<!-- questions: 2.2.12-105, 2.2.12-106 -->

Within built-up areas, regularly parking a motor-vehicle trailer (*Kfz-Anhänger*)
over **2 t** permissible total mass is prohibited from 22:00 to 06:00 and on
Sundays and public holidays in pure/general residential areas, recreation areas,
spa areas, and clinic areas—unless the space is specifically marked. Thus an
industrial area is not within this special ban, and a marked parking space is
an exception. [StVO §12(3a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

A trailer left without its towing vehicle (*ohne Zugfahrzeug*) may not remain
parked on ordinary public-road spaces for more than **two weeks**. The limit
does not apply to an appropriately marked parking space. [StVO
§12(3b)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__12.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- A marked parking area can change the default rule, but it does not cancel
  other prohibitions or the duty to avoid danger.
- A parking-space priority claim is a sequencing rule, not permission to force
  another road user to brake or collide.
- On an Autobahn, a breakdown or emergency is the reason to stop; ordinary
  parking or waiting on the hard shoulder is not permitted. [StVO
  §18(8)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

## Remember this

- **Halten:** no more than 3 minutes and vehicle not left; otherwise **Parken**.
- No stopping on sharp bends, confusing/narrow sections, or Autobahns and
  *Kraftfahrstraßen* (including hard shoulder).
- First to reach the *Parklücke* has priority, subject to safe cooperation.
- Sidewalk parking only when signed/marked; relevant mass limit: **2.8 t**.
- Keep **10 m** before a concealed light, Andreaskreuz, or Stop sign.
- Leave **3 m** of lane beside a solid boundary line.
- Unhitched trailer: normally **maximum 2 weeks**; trailers over 2 t have
  night/Sunday/holiday restrictions in specified built-up areas.
