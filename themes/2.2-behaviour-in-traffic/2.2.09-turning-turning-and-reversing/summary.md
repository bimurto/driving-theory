# Turning, Turning Around and Reversing — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Turning (Abbiegen), turning around (Wenden), and reversing (Rückwärtsfahren)
are manoeuvres with a high risk of conflict because they cross or change the
normal path of other road users. Signal early, position correctly, observe
before and during the manoeuvre, and wait whenever another road user could be
endangered. The core legal rule is that a manoeuvre must not create a danger;
if necessary, obtain assistance (Einweisen). [StVO §9(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)

## Learning goals

You should be able to:

- position and signal correctly for a left turn;
- give priority to oncoming traffic and vulnerable road users;
- recognise when an overtaking vehicle means you must wait;
- identify where turning around and reversing are prohibited; and
- reverse slowly while checking the entire area, including blind spots.

## Core rules

### Signal, position, and observe before turning

<!-- questions: 2.2.09-002-M, 2.2.09-010-M, 2.2.09-011-M -->

Announce a turn in good time and clearly with the direction indicator
(Fahrtrichtungsanzeiger). Before moving into the turning position and again
before turning, check following traffic (Nachfolgeverkehr). For a left turn,
keep to the centre of the carriageway; on a one-way road, position as far left
as practicable. [StVO §9(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)

On a one-way street where bicycles may travel in both directions, do not use a
position that would put you in the path of an oncoming cyclist. Use the middle
line when that is the safe left-turning position; where there is no opposing
traffic risk, use the left line. This is the practical application of the
positioning rule, not permission to ignore cyclists.

### Give way while turning left

<!-- questions: 2.2.09-002-M -->

Before turning left, allow oncoming vehicles to pass (entgegenkommende
Fahrzeuge durchfahren lassen). The same priority applies to bicycles,
motorcycles, mopeds, and electric micro-vehicles travelling on or beside the
road in the same direction, and to buses using a marked special lane. Give
pedestrians particular consideration and wait if needed. [StVO §9(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)

Therefore, when the motorcycle is the road user whose path would conflict with
your left turn, wait until it has passed. Do not rely on the other driver to
guess your intention or speed up to “fit” into the gap.

### Never cut across an overtaking manoeuvre

<!-- questions: 2.2.09-003-M, 2.2.09-004-M -->

If another vehicle is overtaking you, do not begin the left turn or turn into a
farm track (Feldweg) until the overtaking manoeuvre is complete. Stop and wait
at the turning point if necessary. The driver being overtaken must not increase
speed, and the driver who moves out to overtake must exclude danger to
following traffic. [StVO §5(4), §5(6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

Checking following traffic before positioning and again before turning is a
specific legal safeguard. [StVO §9(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)
Even if you have signalled, an indicator does not give you priority over a
vehicle already overtaking.

### A clear signal does not make an unsafe turn legal

<!-- questions: 2.2.09-003-M, 2.2.09-004-M -->

Do not turn merely to clear the carriageway, avoid confusing vehicles behind,
or take a gap while another vehicle is alongside. Wait until the other vehicle
has fully passed and the road is safe. Turning into a property or track is also
covered by the heightened no-danger rule in [StVO §9(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html).

### Turning around and reversing on restricted roads

<!-- questions: 2.2.09-001 -->

Turning around (Wenden) is prohibited on both motorways (Autobahnen) and
motor-vehicle roads/clearways (Kraftfahrstraßen). Reversing is prohibited there
as well; this applies even when you have missed an exit or want to reach a
nearby access point. [StVO §18(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

Use only a lawful exit or junction and continue until you can leave safely.
The prohibition protects fast-moving traffic from a vehicle suddenly crossing
or travelling against the permitted direction.

### Reverse only after securing the whole area

<!-- questions: 2.2.09-101 -->

Before and during reversing, look behind and additionally scan all around the
vehicle (Rundumblick). Move slowly and attentively, and make sure nobody is in
the blind spot (toter Winkel). Stop immediately if the view is insufficient or
someone enters the danger area. If another person can safely guide you, the
law requires you to obtain assistance where necessary. [StVO §9(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)

Mirrors alone do not prove that the area is clear: pedestrians, children,
cyclists, and small obstacles can disappear beside or behind the vehicle.

## Situations and exceptions

- A left-turn indicator communicates intent; it does not cancel the duty to
  give way or the duty to avoid danger. [StVO §9(1), §9(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)
- If traffic is ambiguous, remain stopped until the overtaking or oncoming
  movement is finished. A few seconds of waiting is safer than crossing the
  other vehicle's path.
- On a one-way road, “as far left as possible” remains subject to the actual
  road layout and opposing bicycle traffic. [StVO §9(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)
- The motorway/clearway ban is a location rule: do not solve a navigation
  mistake by turning around or reversing. [StVO §18(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)

## Common exam traps

- Turning because you have signalled, even though a motorcycle or other vehicle
  still has priority.
- Passing a stationary or overtaking vehicle before a left turn when that would
  enter the path of oncoming traffic.
- Treating a farm-track turn as an exception to the normal turning rules.
- Choosing the leftmost line on a two-way bicycle-permitted one-way road when an
  oncoming cyclist could be endangered.
- Checking only the rear-view mirror while reversing.
- Assuming reversing is allowed on a motorway when turning around is not.

## Remember this

- **Signal, position, check — then turn.** Check before positioning and again
  before the turn. [StVO §9(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)
- **Left turn:** oncoming traffic goes first; pedestrians get special care.
  [StVO §9(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)
- **Overtaking alongside you:** wait until it has completely finished.
- **Wenden/Rückwärtsfahren:** prohibited on Autobahnen and Kraftfahrstraßen.
  [StVO §18(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html)
- **Reversing:** slow, attentive, all-around observation, no one in the blind
  spot; get an Einweiser when necessary. [StVO §9(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)
