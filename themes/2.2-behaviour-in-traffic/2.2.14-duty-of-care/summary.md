# Duty of care — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### The universal entry-and-exit duty

<!-- questions: 2.2.14-102, 2.2.14-104, 2.2.14-105, 2.2.14-106, 2.2.14-107-M, 2.2.14-108-M, 2.2.14-110 -->

Anyone getting in or out (**ein- oder aussteigen**) must behave so that other
road users cannot be endangered. Look and wait before opening a door; this
includes checking for vehicles, bicycles, pedestrians, and motorcycles. The
driver must take the necessary measures to avoid accidents or traffic
disruptions when leaving the vehicle. [StVO §14(1)–(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__14.html)

This is an application of the general rule of constant care and mutual
consideration (**ständige Vorsicht und gegenseitige Rücksicht**): no one may be
damaged, endangered, unnecessarily obstructed, or harassed. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

### Check before opening a door

<!-- questions: 2.2.14-102, 2.2.14-104, 2.2.14-106, 2.2.14-107-M, 2.2.14-108-M -->

Before opening a door on the roadway side, check that the traffic situation
permits it. Use the relevant exterior mirror (**Außenspiegel**) and look over
your shoulder to cover the blind spot (**Schulterblick**). A mirror check alone
is not enough: an approaching cyclist can be hidden beside the car. Let the
cyclist or other road user pass first, and open the door only when doing so is
safe. [StVO §14(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__14.html)

If the car is stopped at the edge of the road, the pavement side is generally
the safer side for entry. It is not automatically safe: check that pedestrians
and cyclists on the pavement or cycle path are not endangered or obstructed.
The same duty applies to passengers; brief them before they open a door.
[StVO §14(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__14.html)

### Children need the safer side and supervision

<!-- questions: 2.2.14-105, 2.2.14-110 -->

Let children get in or out directly on the pavement (**Gehweg**) side whenever
possible, so they do not have to cross the carriageway (**Fahrbahn**). If the
roadway side is unavoidable, an adult must supervise the movement and warn the
child about the danger before opening the door. Children are harder to see and
their behaviour can be unpredictable, so do not treat an apparently empty road
as proof that it is safe. [StVO §14(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__14.html)

### Warn passengers about the side they are entering

<!-- questions: 2.2.14-106 -->

Passengers leaving onto a cycle path must look for cyclists approaching from
behind; cyclists are not required to wait on the cycle path. Passengers leaving
onto the pavement must also watch for pedestrians. Explain the relevant danger
before they open the door, and intervene if the intended exit would endanger
someone. [StVO §14(1)–(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__14.html)

### Secure the vehicle before leaving

<!-- questions: 2.2.14-102 -->

Before leaving, prevent the vehicle from rolling away (**Wegrollen**), including
on a downhill gradient: stop it in a stable way and apply the parking brake
(**Feststellbremse**) as appropriate. The driver must take all measures needed
to avoid an accident or traffic disruption when the vehicle is left. [StVO §14(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__14.html)

## Situations and exceptions

### Protect against unauthorised use

<!-- questions: 2.2.14-101, 2.2.14-102, 2.2.14-109 -->

When leaving a motor vehicle, remove the ignition key (**Zündschlüssel**),
close the windows, and lock the doors and boot (**Kofferraum**). Engage the
steering-wheel lock (**Lenkradsperre**) where fitted. These actions protect
against unauthorised use (**unbefugte Benutzung**); a child-safety lock is not a
substitute for locking the car. [StVO §14(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__14.html)

Passenger cars and light goods vehicles are required to have an anti-
unauthorised-use device; passenger cars additionally require an immobiliser
(**Wegfahrsperre**). That equipment requirement does not replace the driver's
duty to secure a parked vehicle. [StVZO §38a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__38a.html)

### A door must not be opened carelessly

<!-- questions: 2.2.14-104, 2.2.14-107-M, 2.2.14-108-M -->

The safest routine is: pause, check the mirror, perform a shoulder check, and
open only when the door will not endanger anyone. A slightly opened door is not
a sufficient substitute for looking; it can itself obstruct or strike another
road user. Vehicle doors must also remain closed while driving. [StVO §14(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__14.html); [StVZO §35e(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__35e.html)

## Remember this

- **Before every door:** mirror + shoulder check + wait if anyone approaches.
- Prefer the **pavement side**; children should not need to cross the carriageway.
- Roadway-side child exit: **adult supervision and warning**.
- On leaving: prevent rolling, remove the key, close windows, lock doors and boot.
- Passengers must not endanger people on the pavement or cyclists on a cycle path.
