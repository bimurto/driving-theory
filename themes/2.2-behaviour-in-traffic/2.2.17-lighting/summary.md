# Lighting — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Dipped beam is the basic driving light in darkness and poor visibility

<!-- questions: 2.2.17-005, 2.2.17-012, 2.2.17-109, 2.2.17-114, 2.2.17-404 -->

During twilight (**Dämmerung**), darkness (**Dunkelheit**), in a tunnel, or whenever visibility requires it, use the prescribed lighting—normally **dipped beam (Abblendlicht)**. If fog, snowfall, or rain substantially impairs visibility, dipped beam is mandatory even during the day. Its practical purpose in daytime bad weather is chiefly that other road users see your vehicle sooner. [StVO §17(1), (3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Parking lights are not a driving substitute

<!-- questions: 2.2.17-103, 2.2.17-107, 2.2.17-111 -->

You may not drive using only **side/parking lights (Begrenzungsleuchten/Standlicht)**. They are not bright enough to illuminate the road. At night or twilight, a vehicle stopped on the hard shoulder outside a built-up area must have its own light source; a moving vehicle needs the appropriate driving lights. [StVO §17(2), (4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Front fog lamps: only for seriously impaired visibility

<!-- questions: 2.2.17-005, 2.2.17-101 -->

**Front fog lamps (Nebelscheinwerfer)** may be switched on only when fog, snowfall, or rain substantially impairs visibility. In a car with two fog lamps, the law permits them with side lights instead of dipped beam; using them together with dipped beam is also possible. They are not a general daytime styling light. [StVO §17(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html) [StVZO §52(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__52.html)

### Rear fog lamp: the 50-metre threshold

<!-- questions: 2.2.17-001, 2.2.17-102 -->

The **rear fog lamp (Nebelschlussleuchte)** may be used only when fog reduces visibility to **less than 50 m**. It is powerful and can glare into following drivers’ eyes, so switch it off when the condition no longer applies. Its operation is shown by the required yellow/amber indicator lamp (**gelbe Kontrollleuchte**) in the driver’s field of vision. [StVO §17(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html) [StVZO §53d(4)–(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__53d.html)

### High beam: see farther, but never dazzle

<!-- questions: 2.2.17-008, 2.2.17-011, 2.2.17-013, 2.2.17-104, 2.2.17-402, 2.2.17-403 -->

Use **high beam (Fernlicht)** only when it improves vision without endangering others. Dip promptly for oncoming traffic, a vehicle close ahead, a continuously and adequately lit road, or whenever road safety otherwise requires it. At a railway crossing, dip if your waiting vehicle would dazzle others. A blue indicator lamp (**blaue Fernlicht-Kontrollleuchte**) confirms high beam; depending on the vehicle, the control-switch position may also identify it. [StVO §17(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html) [StVZO §50(5), (9)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__50.html)

When a vehicle is already travelling close in front, use dipped beam even on an unlit road: high beam would dazzle the driver through the mirrors. A pedestrian travelling in the same direction is not automatically the same high-beam trigger, but the general duty not to endanger still applies. [StVO §17(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Automatic high-beam assist is only an aid

<!-- questions: 2.2.17-116, 2.2.17-117, 2.2.17-118 -->

**High-beam assist (Fernlichtassistent)** can activate high beam when ambient conditions and an insufficiently lit road make it suitable, then dip when it detects oncoming traffic. It may react too late over a crest (**Kuppe**) or around a bend (**Kurve**), and an automatic lighting system may fail to recognise low sun or fog. Watch the road and switch manually to dipped beam whenever necessary. The legal duty to avoid dazzling remains with the driver. [StVO §17(1)–(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

### Headlamp levelling must match the load

<!-- questions: 2.2.17-105, 2.2.17-115 -->

The manual or automatic **headlamp levelling control (Leuchtweitenregelung)** compensates for a changed vehicle attitude when passengers or luggage load the rear. If aimed too high, dipped beam can dazzle oncoming traffic; if aimed too low, the illuminated range is too short. Headlamps must be adjustable and secured against unintended movement. [StVZO §50(3), (8)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__50.html)

### Covered rear lights require a light carrier

<!-- questions: 2.2.17-010 -->

If a load or carrier covers the prescribed rear lighting, fit a **light carrier (Leuchtenträger)** with all required rear lamps before driving. There is no 5-km or darkness-only exception: the required lighting must remain effective whenever the vehicle is used. [StVZO §49a(9), (9a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__49a.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- In seriously impaired visibility from fog, snow, or rain, use dipped beam by day; front fog lamps are additionally permitted only in that weather. [StVO §17(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)
- Rear fog lamps have the stricter condition: fog visibility under 50 m. [StVO §17(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)
- On a continuously and adequately lit road, high beam is prohibited; dip early for traffic ahead or approaching. [StVO §17(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)
- Within built-up areas, a stationary vehicle may generally be identified on the road-facing side with parking lights; outside built-up areas, a stopped vehicle needs its own light source. [StVO §17(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)
- If a light, load, or automatic function is not working as expected, the driver must correct the situation before continuing safely. [StVO §17(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__17.html)

## Remember this

- **Dusk, darkness, tunnel, poor visibility:** dipped beam (**Abblendlicht**).
- **Front fog lamps:** only serious visibility impairment from fog, snow, or rain.
- **Rear fog lamp:** fog visibility **under 50 m**; amber indicator.
- **High beam:** blue indicator; dip for oncoming traffic, close-following traffic, adequate continuous road lighting, and other danger.
- **Parking/side lights:** never alone for driving.
- **Load changes aim:** level the headlamps; too high dazzles, too low shortens range.
- **Covered rear lights:** fit a light carrier.
