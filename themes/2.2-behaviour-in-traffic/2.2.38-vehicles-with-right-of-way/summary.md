# Vehicles With Right of Way — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Give emergency vehicles immediate free passage

<!-- questions: 2.2.38-101 -->

When a vehicle approaches with blue flashing light and the *Einsatzhorn*, all
other road users must immediately create a free path (*sofort freie Bahn zu
schaffen*). [StVO § 38(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__38.html)

If it is coming from behind, reduce speed and move as far as practicable to the
right side (*möglichst weit nach rechts fahren*), while observing other traffic
and avoiding a collision. This opens the lane so the emergency vehicle can pass
without hindrance. Do not simply stop immediately wherever you are: braking and
positioning must still be safe, and the legal priority is to clear a passage.
The duty follows from the same command to give immediate free passage.

Blue flashing light alone has a different warning function: it may be used at
accident or other deployment sites, on emergency journeys, or when accompanying
vehicles or closed groups. The command to give immediate free passage is
specifically attached to blue light together with the *Einsatzhorn*.
[StVO § 38(1)–(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__38.html)

## Remember this

- **Blue light + siren:** *sofort freie Bahn schaffen*.
- From behind: slow down and move as far right as safely possible.
- Clear a passage; do not perform an unsafe, automatic emergency stop.
- Blue light alone warns in the situations listed by § 38(2); it does not carry
  the § 38(1) command by itself.
