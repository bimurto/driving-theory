# Other Duties of the Driver — Class B Study Summary

## Core rules

### Keep warning signals audible

<!-- questions: 2.2.23-031, 2.2.23-063 -->

Radio or exhaust noise, headphones, and a distracting telephone conversation can make warning signals (Warnsignale) inaudible or too late to notice. Keep sound at a level that lets you hear horns, sirens and other traffic warnings; the general duty to take every necessary precaution also applies to avoidable distraction. [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### Mobile phones and mounted devices

<!-- questions: 2.2.23-040, 2.2.23-120, 2.2.23-121, 2.2.23-122, 2.2.23-406 -->

While driving, a hand-held electronic device (elektronisches Gerät) may be used only under the conditions in StVO §23(1a): it must not be held or operated in the hand, and use is limited to voice control or a brief, situation-appropriate glance. The vehicle must be stationary and the engine completely switched off before picking up a phone; stopping at a suitable place is the practical solution. This applies to cars, trucks and motorcycles. [StVO §23(1a)–(1b)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

Even hands-free use is unsafe during stressful conversations or complicated traffic. Do not operate navigation while moving: enter the destination while stationary and use voice output where possible. A hands-free unit is not permission to be distracted; the driver must still maintain control and attention. [StVO §1(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

### Check and adjust before moving

<!-- questions: 2.2.23-103, 2.2.23-106, 2.2.23-107, 2.2.23-123, 2.2.23-125 -->

In a vehicle that is not your own, adjust the seat, head restraint, seat belt and mirrors (Sitz, Kopfstütze, Sicherheitsgurt, Spiegel). The trip counter is not a safety adjustment. Before every journey ensure an unobstructed view: clean windows and mirrors, functional wipers and sufficient washer fluid, and a correctly aimed headlamp. The windscreen, side windows, mirrors, load and streaks from worn wiper blades can all impair vision. [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html); [StVZO §50](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__50.html)

If the windscreen mists, direct strong airflow at it and/or use air conditioning (Klimaanlage), which dries the air. Do not continue until you can see safely.

### Head restraints

<!-- questions: 2.2.23-119, 2.2.23-124 -->

The head restraint (Kopfstütze) reduces cervical-spine injury in sudden braking or a crash. Set its top approximately level with the crown of the head and keep the back of the head only a few centimetres away. It improves protection; it does not replace the seat belt. [StVZO §35a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__35a.html)

### Reversing and parking securely

<!-- questions: 2.2.23-104, 2.2.23-105, 2.2.23-106, 2.2.23-113 -->

Reverse only slowly and with particular caution (Schrittgeschwindigkeit is a useful safety interpretation), looking mainly behind while also checking ahead and to both sides. Hazard warning lights are not automatically required merely because you reverse. [StVO §9(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)

On a downhill slope with a manual gearbox, apply the parking brake and engage first or reverse gear; never leave the lever in neutral. With an automatic gearbox, apply the parking brake and select **P**; **N** is not a roll-away safeguard. The parking brake must secure the stationary vehicle. [StVO §14(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__14.html)

For a parked single-axle caravan/trailer, apply its parking brake and place wheel chocks (Unterlegkeile) in front of the wheels on the downhill side. [StVO §14(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__14.html)

### Load, covers and crosswind

<!-- questions: 2.2.23-101, 2.2.23-107 -->

Load must not obstruct the driver's view through the mirrors, and a tarpaulin (Plane) must be secured. A high tarpaulin on an unloaded trailer is especially vulnerable to crosswind (Seitenwind). Do not assume a cover replaces load securing: the load must still be secured against slipping, falling or rolling. [StVO §22](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)

### Snow, ice and mist

<!-- questions: 2.2.23-117, 2.2.23-118, 2.2.23-126, 2.2.23-127, 2.2.23-128 -->

Before driving, remove snow and ice from the entire vehicle: all windows, mirrors, roof, lights and registration plates (Kennzeichen). Falling snow can injure or cause a crash, and snow sliding onto the windscreen can eliminate forward vision; covered lights or plates prevent recognition and identification. Do not leave the engine running unnecessarily while clearing the vehicle; clear it first and drive only with adequate visibility. [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html); [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html); [StVZO §10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__10.html)

## Situations and exceptions

### Registration plates, ownership and insurance

<!-- questions: 2.2.23-033, 2.2.23-034, 2.2.23-035, 2.2.23-036, 2.2.23-053, 2.2.23-054 -->

Official registration plates must be mounted in the prescribed position, legible and illuminated at the rear as required. Do not add stickers, foil or other changes. [StVZO §10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__10.html)

When selling a still-registered vehicle, give the buyer the registration documents against a receipt and promptly report the buyer's name and address to the registration authority (Zulassungsbehörde); the inspection sticker remains on the plate. A change of name or address must also be reported. A new owner reports the change of ownership and provides proof of compulsory motor-liability insurance (Kfz-Haftpflichtversicherung); a general inspection is not normally triggered merely by the ownership change. [FZV §15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fzv_2011/__15.html); [FZV §13](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fzv_2011/__13.html)

If compulsory liability insurance ends, the vehicle may not be used on public roads and the keeper must arrange deregistration/notification through the authority. [PflVG §6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/pflvg/__6.html); [FZV §15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fzv_2011/__15.html)

### Winter and pre-trip safety checks

<!-- questions: 2.2.23-123 -->

For a long trip, check tyres (Reifen) for damage, pressure, wear and at least **1.6 mm** tread depth, check headlamp aim, and ensure a clean windscreen and enough washer fluid. Incorrect tyre pressure and worn tyres reduce grip; mis-aimed lamps can dazzle others. [StVZO §36](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__36.html); [StVZO §50](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__50.html)

## Remember this

- Hear warnings, see clearly, and remove distractions.
- Phone: stationary + engine off before holding it; otherwise use compliant hands-free/voice control.
- Before moving: adjust seat, belt, head restraint and mirrors; check tyres, lights, wipers and load.
- Clear the whole vehicle of snow and ice.
- Secure parked vehicles against rolling; use the correct gear/P and chocks where needed.
- Ownership and insurance changes are reportable duties, not optional paperwork.
