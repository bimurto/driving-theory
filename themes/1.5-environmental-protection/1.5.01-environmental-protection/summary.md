# Environmental Protection — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Environmentally responsible driving begins by avoiding unnecessary journeys,
idling, noise, and acceleration. A maintained vehicle, correct tyre pressure,
low engine speeds, early upshifts, and anticipatory driving reduce fuel use,
emissions, wear, and nuisance without compromising safety.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- decide when to switch off a combustion engine;
- drive smoothly in an efficient engine-speed range;
- identify maintenance and tyre factors that raise consumption;
- prevent noise, smoke, fuel, oil, and waste pollution; and
- recognise environmental-zone exemptions.

## Core rules

### Avoid unnecessary idling and trips

<!-- questions: 1.5.01-001, 1.5.01-005, 1.5.01-013, 1.5.01-015, 1.5.01-017, 1.5.01-119, 1.5.01-123, 1.5.01-129, 1.5.01-134 -->

Do not run an engine unnecessarily or drive back and forth without purpose where
this bothers others. Switch a combustion engine off for a foreseeable long wait,
such as at a closed level crossing, prolonged congestion, or while loading and
unloading. Do not switch it off for a momentary STOP-sign wait where immediate
readiness is needed. [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html)

After starting, drive away promptly and warm the engine gently under light load
at low—but smooth—engine speed. Warming it while stationary wastes fuel and
creates avoidable emissions. [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html) [UBA — Saving fuel](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/umwelttipps-fuer-den-alltag/mobilitaet/sprit-sparen)

### Maintain the vehicle and investigate smoke

<!-- questions: 1.5.01-003, 1.5.01-016, 1.5.01-104, 1.5.01-108, 1.5.01-117, 1.5.01-125 -->

Regularly maintain the ignition, injection or carburettor system, engine air
filter, exhaust, and other manufacturer-specified components. Track consumption;
an unexpected increase can reveal a defect or incorrect tuning. Maintenance
supports road safety as well as low fuel use and emissions. [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html) [StVZO §30](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__30.html)

Persistent thick or dark exhaust smoke can indicate an engine or fuel-system
fault and can obscure other road users. Arrange a prompt workshop check; do not
confuse ordinary brief water vapour in cold weather with continuing abnormal
smoke. [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html) [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### Correct tyre pressure saves fuel and tyres

<!-- questions: 1.5.01-004, 1.5.01-116, 1.5.01-121, 1.5.01-133 -->

Check cold tyre pressure regularly and use the vehicle manufacturer's value for
the load. Underinflation increases rolling resistance, fuel use, deformation,
heat, and wear. Avoid hard acceleration, which also increases abrasion. Excessive
pressure is not an efficiency shortcut; remain within the approved specification.
[StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html) [UBA — Saving fuel](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/umwelttipps-fuer-den-alltag/mobilitaet/sprit-sparen)

## Efficient driving technique

### High gear, low engine speed, smooth flow

<!-- questions: 1.5.01-011, 1.5.01-012, 1.5.01-103, 1.5.01-109, 1.5.01-112, 1.5.01-126, 1.5.01-127, 1.5.01-131 -->

Accelerate purposefully without revving each gear out, shift up early, and shift
down only when the engine needs it. At constant speed use the highest suitable
gear and low engine speed, above the point where the engine labours or judders.
Cruise control can help maintain an even speed where conditions are stable.
[UBA — Saving fuel](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/umwelttipps-fuer-den-alltag/mobilitaet/sprit-sparen)

High speed, full-throttle starts, unnecessary acceleration, and high engine rpm
raise consumption. Plan ahead so you can release the accelerator early and use
momentum or overrun rather than repeatedly accelerating and braking.
[UBA — Saving fuel](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/umwelttipps-fuer-den-alltag/mobilitaet/sprit-sparen) [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Avoid stop-and-go and accelerator “playing”

<!-- questions: 1.5.01-106, 1.5.01-110, 1.5.01-124, 1.5.01-132 -->

Hectic alternation between acceleration and braking, revving at a red light, and
stop-and-go traffic waste energy, produce more exhaust, and disturb others. Read
traffic far ahead, leave space, and coast toward a red signal instead of racing
to it. [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html) [UBA — Saving fuel](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/umwelttipps-fuer-den-alltag/mobilitaet/sprit-sparen)

### Choose efficient equipment and oil

<!-- questions: 1.5.01-014, 1.5.01-118 -->

Low-rolling-resistance tyres reduce rolling losses. Approved low-viscosity oil
reduces internal engine friction and can reduce fuel use; use only the viscosity
and specification approved by the manufacturer. [UBA — Saving fuel](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/umwelttipps-fuer-den-alltag/mobilitaet/sprit-sparen)

### Reduce the need to drive and aerodynamic drag

<!-- questions: 1.5.01-114, 1.5.01-115, 1.5.01-120 -->

Walk, cycle, use public transport, share journeys, and avoid short cold-engine
trips or congested city-centre searches where practical. When buying a vehicle,
compare energy consumption. Remove unused roof boxes and racks because their
aerodynamic drag raises consumption, especially at speed. [UBA — Saving fuel](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/umwelttipps-fuer-den-alltag/mobilitaet/sprit-sparen)

## Noise, fuel, oil, and waste

### Prevent unnecessary vehicle noise

<!-- questions: 1.5.01-008, 1.5.01-009, 1.5.01-102, 1.5.01-128, 1.5.01-130 -->

Drive at low engine speed, accelerate only as necessary, and choose tyres with
low external rolling noise. Repair a defective silencer or exhaust. Unauthorised
engine or exhaust modification can increase noise and pollutants and may cause
the operating permit to expire. Unnecessary vehicle noise and avoidable exhaust
nuisance are prohibited. [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html) [StVZO §19(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__19.html)

### Refuel without vapour exposure or overflow

<!-- questions: 1.5.01-007 -->

Avoid breathing fuel vapours, extinguish ignition sources, and stop when the
automatic nozzle first switches off. Do not top off: fuel needs expansion space,
and overflow contaminates soil and creates fire and health risks. Follow the
station's safety instructions. [GefStoffV §8](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gefstoffv_2010/__8.html) [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

### Oil loss demands immediate attention

<!-- questions: 1.5.01-006 -->

Even a very small oil leak can contaminate a large volume of water and soil.
Treat any loss as urgent: stop the source safely, prevent oil reaching drains or
water, and arrange repair and cleanup. [WHG §5(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/whg_2009/__5.html) [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

### Dispose of oil, lubricants, and batteries properly

<!-- questions: 1.5.01-010 -->

Take waste oil and lubricants to an authorised collection point, recycling
centre, workshop, or the seller's required return system; never put them in
household waste or drains. Return vehicle batteries through authorised retailers,
workshops, or municipal collection systems. [AltölV §8](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/alt_lv/__8.html) [BattDG §18](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/battdg/__18.html)

## Environmental zones

<!-- questions: 1.5.01-122 -->

The federal environmental-zone exemptions include agricultural and forestry
tractors and appropriately marked ambulances/emergency medical cars; specified
vehicles using StVO §35 special rights are also exempt. Check the exact vehicle
and mission rather than assuming every service vehicle qualifies.
[35. BImSchV Annex 3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bimschv_35/anhang_3.html)

## Dataset warning

The worksheet for **1.5.01-006** gives the mnemonic “one drop of oil pollutes
600 litres of drinking water.” Current federal law does not define a fixed
litres-per-drop conversion; actual contamination depends on oil type, drop size,
water body, and treatment. Remember the exam figure, but use the legally sound
principle: prevent any oil release and act immediately. [WHG §5(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/whg_2009/__5.html)

The explanation for **1.5.01-005** uses a one-minute break-even rule. Modern
vehicles differ, and current StVO sets no universal minute threshold; it simply
prohibits unnecessary engine running. Switch off for a foreseeable longer wait
when safe and appropriate. [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html)

## Common exam traps

- Warming the engine while parked instead of driving away gently.
- Treating the speed limit as a reason to use high rpm in a low gear.
- Inflating beyond the approved pressure to chase lower consumption.
- Ignoring dark smoke because the vehicle still moves normally.
- Topping off after the fuel nozzle clicks.
- Putting oil, lubricants, or batteries in household waste.
- Assuming all vehicles are banned from an environmental zone.

## Remember this

- **Long wait:** engine off when safe; no unnecessary idling.
- **Drive:** anticipate, accelerate smoothly, shift up early, low rpm.
- **Tyres:** approved pressure, low rolling resistance, gentle inputs.
- **Maintenance:** filters, ignition/injection, exhaust, consumption trend.
- **Smoke/leak:** prompt inspection and containment.
- **Waste:** authorised return or collection system.
