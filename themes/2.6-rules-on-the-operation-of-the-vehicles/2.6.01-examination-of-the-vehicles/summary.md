# Vehicle Inspection (HU) — Class B Study Summary

## Know the due date

Find the next **Hauptuntersuchung (HU)** date on the inspection tag on the rear
number plate or in the registration certificate Part I. The date applies even
if a registered trailer has stood unused for months.

For a car that is already three years old, and for later inspections, the next
HU is normally due after **two years**. A newly bought used car follows the date
shown on its valid inspection record—not its purchase date.

**Remember:** *plate, papers, then two years.*
