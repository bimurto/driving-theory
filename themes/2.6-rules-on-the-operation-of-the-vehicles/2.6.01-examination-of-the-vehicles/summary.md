# Examination of the Vehicles — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

The German periodic roadworthiness inspection is the **Hauptuntersuchung (HU)**.
It checks whether a vehicle remains roadworthy, environmentally acceptable, and
compliant with the applicable construction rules. The inspection deadline is a
legal obligation of the keeper (*Halter*); leaving a vehicle unused does not stop
the clock.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- find the next HU deadline (*Fälligkeit*) from the rear inspection sticker
  (*Prüfplakette*) and registration certificate Part I;
- apply the two-year interval to an already registered used passenger car; and
- distinguish the first three-year interval for a newly registered passenger car
  from later two-year intervals.

## Core rules

### Where to find the next HU deadline

<!-- questions: 2.6.01-003 -->

The month and year by which the vehicle must be presented for its next HU are
shown on the **Prüfplakette** attached to the rear registration plate. The same
deadline is recorded in the **Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil I** (registration
certificate Part I, formerly the *Fahrzeugschein*). The sticker and document are
therefore the relevant sources; the vehicle operating manual is not the legal
deadline record. The inspection sticker becomes invalid at the end of the month
shown. [StVZO §29(2), §29(6–7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__29.html)

### Passenger-car HU intervals

<!-- questions: 2.6.01-105, 2.6.01-108 -->

For a passenger car (*Personenkraftwagen*, PKW), the first HU is due **36 months
(three years)** after it first enters service (*erstmals in den Verkehr gekommen*).
Each subsequent HU is due after **24 months (two years)**. Thus, buying a
three-year-old car does not restart the schedule: when its new HU has just been
completed, the next one is due in 24 months. The relevant interval is determined
by the vehicle's inspection history, not by whether the current owner bought it
new or used. [StVZO Anlage VIII, nos. 2.1.2.1.1–2.1.2.1.2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/anlage_viii.html)

### The deadline continues while a caravan is parked

<!-- questions: 2.6.01-106 -->

A registered caravan trailer (*Wohnanhänger*) remains subject to its HU deadline
even if it spends six months standing on a campsite. Storage or seasonal
non-use does not extend the date on the inspection sticker. The keeper must have
the vehicle presented by the end of the month shown for the next HU; the law
defines the due date from the last inspection rather than from how much the
vehicle was driven. [StVZO Anlage VIII, nos. 2.1.5 and 2.3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/anlage_viii.html)

## Numbers and formulas

### The key intervals

| Vehicle situation | Next HU interval |
|---|---:|
| Passenger car's first HU after first registration | 36 months |
| Passenger car's later HU | 24 months |

These are statutory intervals, not rules of thumb. The exact deadline is the
month and year recorded by the inspection sticker and in the registration
certificate. [StVZO Anlage VIII, nos. 2.1.2.1.1–2.1.2.1.2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/anlage_viii.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- A used-car purchase does not reset the HU period; use the existing HU date.
- A vehicle parked or unused on private or campsite grounds is not granted an
  extension merely because it was not driven.
- The intervals here are for ordinary passenger cars. Other vehicles, including
  motorcycles, buses, heavy vehicles, and some trailers, have different entries
  in the statutory table. [StVZO Anlage VIII, no. 2.1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/anlage_viii.html)

## Common exam traps

- Choosing the operating manual instead of the **Prüfplakette** or Part I of the
  registration certificate.
- Treating a used-car purchase as a new three-year inspection period.
- Adding the months a caravan stood unused to its legal deadline.
- Confusing the first 36-month interval with the later 24-month interval.

## Remember this

- **Prüfplakette + Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil I** tell you the next HU month.
- A normal passenger car: **36 months first, then 24 months repeatedly**.
- A registered caravan's HU date does not pause while it is parked.
- Follow the month and year shown; the sticker is invalid after that month ends.
