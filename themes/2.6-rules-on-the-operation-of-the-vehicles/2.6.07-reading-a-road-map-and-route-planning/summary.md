# Maps and Route Planning — Class B Study Summary

## Plan before moving

Navigation data and maps can become outdated, and even current data can differ
from the road. Signs, road layout and police directions on site take priority.

Before driving abroad, check city tolls, environmental-zone labels and local
speed limits. Enter destinations, send messages and make calls **before** the
journey or when safely parked. A hands-free call can still distract you heavily.

**Remember:** *road beats screen; plan before you roll.*
