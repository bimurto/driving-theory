# Trailer Operation — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

A trailer (Anhänger) changes the combination's mass, length, braking, visibility,
cornering and stability. Before every journey, confirm that the combination is
legally permitted, correctly coupled and loaded, and that its lights and brakes
work. The driver remains responsible for safe operation and must adapt speed to
the road and the trailer's behaviour.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- identify the trailers permitted with licence class B and the one-trailer rule;
- check coupling, breakaway cable, lights, mirrors and the documents' limits;
- distinguish Anhängelast (towed mass), Nutzlast (payload) and Stützlast (nose weight);
- load and drive a trailer so that it remains stable; and
- manage reversing, overrun brakes and separation safely.

## Core rules

### Licence class B and the number of trailers

<!-- questions: 2.6.03-101, 2.6.03-116, 2.6.03-120 -->

Class B covers a motor vehicle with a permissible maximum mass (zulässige
Gesamtmasse) of no more than 3,500 kg, with either a trailer up to 750 kg or a
heavier trailer when the combination's permissible maximum mass does not exceed
3,500 kg. This is a licence limit; the car's technical towing limit can be
lower. [FeV §6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__6.html)

Behind a motor vehicle, only **one trailer** may normally be towed; a bus trailer
for carrying passengers is excluded. The special two-trailer exception is for
certain tractors (Zugmaschinen), not an ordinary car or Class B combination.
[StVZO §32a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__32a.html)

### Towing limits and where to find them

<!-- questions: 2.6.03-105, 2.6.03-106, 2.6.03-112, 2.6.03-113, 2.6.03-114 -->

The **towed mass (Anhängelast)** is the actual mass being drawn, not the
trailer’s payload and not automatically its permissible maximum mass. The
vehicle manufacturer sets the technically permissible Anhängelast; check the
towing car's registration certificate Part I (Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil I,
field O.1/O.2 where applicable) and the operating manual. The trailer's own
permissible maximum mass and payload must also be respected. [StVZO §42](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__42.html)

For every combination, compare all relevant ceilings: car Anhängelast, trailer
permissible maximum mass, hitch and trailer limits, actual load, and combination
mass. A Class B licence does not override a technical or registration limit.

### Nose weight (Stützlast) and loading

<!-- questions: 2.6.03-114, 2.6.03-406 -->

**Stützlast** is the force/mass borne vertically by the towing vehicle at the
coupling. It must stay within the lowest permitted value for the car, coupling
and trailer. For a rigid-drawbar or centre-axle trailer up to 3.5 t permissible
maximum mass, the towing vehicle must receive at least 4% of the trailer's
actual total mass, but this minimum need not exceed 25 kg; the manufacturer's
maximum must never be exceeded. [StVZO §44(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__44.html)

Thus a 600 kg trailer has a 24 kg minimum (600 × 0.04), while the statutory
minimum cap is 25 kg. Load the trailer so the centre of gravity is close to the
axle and the nose weight is in range; excessive rear loading can cause sway.

### Coupling, breakaway cable and lights

<!-- questions: 2.6.03-105, 2.6.03-117 -->

After coupling, inspect and test the ball coupling (Anhängekupplung): the
coupling head must fully enclose the ball, the locking indicator/handle must be
in its secure position, and a visual and mechanical check must confirm that it
cannot open. Connect the electrical plug and check the trailer's lights before
moving. Vehicle connecting equipment must provide the safety achievable by the
state of technology. [StVZO §43](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__43.html)

Where required by the trailer's design, attach the **breakaway cable**
(Abreißseil) to the towing vehicle's designated point, not merely around the
ball neck. If the trailer separates, it operates the trailer brake so the
trailer can stop rather than roll freely. Follow the coupling device and
vehicle manufacturer's instructions.

### Mirrors and indirect vision

<!-- questions: 2.6.03-111 -->

The driver must be able to observe all material traffic events behind, beside
and immediately in front of the vehicle, including when towing. If the trailer
blocks the required view, fit suitable additional towing mirrors (Zusatzspiegel)
before driving. [StVZO §56(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__56.html)

## Situations and exceptions

### Reversing with an obstructed view

<!-- questions: 2.6.03-001 -->

When reversing a combination with restricted rearward view, do not endanger
anyone and use a competent directing person (Einweiser) where necessary. Agree
clear signals before moving. Stop immediately if visual or reliable
communication with the person directing is lost. The duty is about safety and
also applies on factory or private premises when people could be endangered;
being off a public road is not a reason to dispense with the safeguard. [StVO §9(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__9.html)

### Swaying, bouncing and potholes

<!-- questions: 2.6.03-102, 2.6.03-115, 2.6.03-121 -->

If the trailer begins to bounce or swing (Aufschaukeln) after potholes or a
sudden disturbance, ease off and reduce speed smoothly; do not accelerate to
“pull it straight” and do not make a fast counter-steer. The aim is to remove
the excitation and restore control. Choose a speed appropriate to road,
weather, load and combination; the general duty to adapt speed and remain in
control is in [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html).

Stability is worsened by a centre of gravity far to the rear, under-inflated
trailer tyres or a leaking shock absorber. Keep the load near the axle, use the
specified tyre pressure, and repair suspension faults before towing.

### Overrun brakes, downhill travel and separation

<!-- questions: 2.6.03-118 -->

An **overrun brake** (Auflaufbremse) is actuated when the trailer pushes against
the towing vehicle during deceleration. On a long downhill run, repeated
braking can overheat the trailer brakes; select an appropriate lower gear and
use controlled speed, taking a safe break if needed. On an uphill stationary
combination, the trailer's overrun mechanism may not apply because the trailer
does not push forward into the coupling. The system is not a special 60 km/h
limit: speed must follow the applicable signs, vehicle limits and conditions.

Before uncoupling, apply the trailer parking brake (Feststellbremse) and secure
the trailer against rolling. This is especially important with a fully loaded
trailer. [StVZO §41](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__41.html)

### Changed performance when towing

<!-- questions: 2.6.03-119 -->

The trailer adds mass and length: acceleration is weaker and the overtaking
distance/time is longer. A combination also needs more room and planning when
returning to its lane. Take bends wide enough for the trailer's path; the
necessary turning radius is not reduced. [StVO §5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__5.html)

## Common exam traps

- Confusing the Class B 750 kg rule with the car's actual technical Anhängelast.
- Treating Nutzlast or permissible trailer mass as the actual Anhängelast.
- Assuming an overrun brake creates a 60 km/h limit.
- Accelerating or counter-steering when a trailer is swaying.
- Omitting the breakaway cable, coupling check or lighting check.
- Believing a factory yard removes the need to stop when the Einweiser is no
  longer visible.
- Ignoring Stützlast, rear-heavy loading, tyre pressure or suspension condition.
- Forgetting that a trailer increases overtaking distance and reduces acceleration.

## Remember this

- Class B: trailer up to 750 kg, or heavier if the combination stays within 3,500 kg.
- Ordinary motor vehicle: one trailer only.
- Check the car's Part I registration certificate and manual for Anhängelast and
  Stützlast; respect the lowest limit.
- 600 kg × 4% = 24 kg minimum Stützlast (subject to the statutory conditions).
- Couple, lock, inspect, attach the Abreißseil, connect and test lights, then drive.
- If the trailer swings: ease off and reduce speed—do not accelerate or jerk the wheel.
- If the Einweiser disappears: stop.
- Before uncoupling: apply the trailer parking brake and secure it.
