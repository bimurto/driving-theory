# Admission to Road Traffic, Vehicle Documents and Driving Licence — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Before a car is used on public roads, it must be properly admitted, insured and
technically compliant. The driver must hold the appropriate Fahrerlaubnis and
carry the physical Führerschein and the vehicle's Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil I.
Vehicle modifications can require an immediate technical assessment and an
update to the vehicle documents. Class B also limits the permissible mass of a
car-and-trailer combination.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- distinguish the driving licence from the registration documents;
- explain what to do after a modification requiring an assessment;
- recognise the consequences of driving after the Betriebserlaubnis has expired; and
- calculate the trailer limit for an ordinary Class B licence.

## Core rules

### Carry the driving licence and registration certificate Part I

<!-- questions: 2.6.02-027 -->

When driving a motor vehicle, carry the valid driving licence (Führerschein) and
present it to authorised officials on request. [FeV §4(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__4.html)
The vehicle driver must also carry the Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil I
(registration certificate Part I, formerly Fahrzeugschein) and hand it over for
inspection when requested. [FZV §13(6)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fzv_2023/__13.html)

Do not confuse Part I with the Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil II (formerly
Fahrzeugbrief). Part II is the ownership/title document and is not the document
the driver normally carries under these provisions. [FZV §13](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fzv_2023/__13.html)

### Technical modifications may end the operating licence

<!-- questions: 2.6.02-030 -->

The Betriebserlaubnis (operating licence) expires if a modification changes the
approved vehicle type, creates an expected danger to road users, or worsens the
vehicle's exhaust or noise behaviour. [StVZO §19(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__19.html)
Once it has expired, the vehicle must not be operated on public roads until the
legal requirements are restored and the required approval is obtained. [StVZO §19(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__19.html)

Using a vehicle without the required compulsory third-party liability insurance
is prohibited. [PflVG §6(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/pflvg/__6.html)
Knowingly or negligently using an uninsured vehicle is a criminal offence, not
merely an ordinary administrative fine. [PflVG §30](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/pflvg/__30.html)
In addition, an unlawful road-traffic offence can lead to a record in the
Fahreignungsregister (Central Register of Drivers' Fitness), depending on the
offence and its legal assessment. [StVG §28](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvg_2013/__28.html)

### Get the required assessment immediately and carry its proof

<!-- questions: 2.6.02-036, 2.6.02-038, 2.6.02-101 -->

If a modification is subject to an Abnahme (acceptance inspection) or Gutachten
(expert report), arrange it promptly after the modification—do not wait for the
next Hauptuntersuchung (periodic roadworthiness inspection). A required
assessment must be performed without delay where the approval or component
approval makes its validity conditional on that acceptance. [StVZO §19(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__19.html)

For an altered suspension (for example, lowering the vehicle), the same rule
applies: obtain the required expert report or acceptance immediately. Keep the
Prüfbericht, Gutachten or confirmation in the vehicle and present it on request.
Where the modification changes reportable vehicle data or the approval requires
an entry, have the Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil I corrected. [FZV §15(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fzv_2023/__15.html)
The proof is not pasted into Part II; it remains a document to carry and, where
required, the authority records the change in Part I. [StVZO §19(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__19.html)

## Numbers and formulas

### Class B trailer mass

<!-- questions: 2.6.02-104 -->

For the ordinary Class B licence, the car may have a permissible maximum mass
(zulässige Gesamtmasse) of no more than **3,500 kg**. It may tow either a trailer
with a permissible maximum mass of no more than **750 kg**, or a heavier trailer
whose permissible maximum mass does not exceed the car's **Leermasse** (empty
mass), provided the combination's permissible maximum mass does not exceed
**3,500 kg**. [FeV Annex 3, Class B](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/anlage_3.html)

For the worksheet vehicle:

```text
3,500 kg combination limit − 2,400 kg car limit = 1,100 kg maximum trailer
```

The vehicle's own permissible towed load is 1,500 kg, but the licence limit is
lower. Of the listed choices, **1,000 kg** is therefore allowed. Always apply
both limits: the driving-licence combination limit and the car's technical
Anhängelast (permissible towed load) shown in its documents. [FeV Annex 3, Class B](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/anlage_3.html)

If the combination exceeds 3,500 kg, Class B alone is insufficient. The B96
key (Schlüsselzahl 96) covers a combination over 3,500 kg up to 4,250 kg under
its conditions; it is not the same as ordinary Class B. [FeV §6a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__6a.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- A modification does not automatically extinguish the operating licence: the
  legal effect depends on the conditions in StVZO §19 and on whether the
  required approval, parts approval or acceptance has been completed. [StVZO §19](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__19.html)
- Reportable changes such as permissible mass, axle load, trailer load, or
  dimensions must be notified to the registration authority without delay. [FZV §15(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fzv_2023/__15.html)
- The trailer calculation uses permissible maximum masses, not the actual load
  on a particular journey. The technical towing rating can be stricter than
  the licence limit. [FeV Annex 3, Class B](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/anlage_3.html)

## Common exam traps

- Carrying Part II instead of Part I: Part I is the registration document to
  carry; Part II is the title document.
- Waiting for the next Hauptuntersuchung after a modification that requires an
  assessment.
- Forgetting to carry the acceptance report or to update Part I when required.
- Treating an expired Betriebserlaubnis as permission to continue driving.
- Using the car's 1,500 kg technical towing figure without checking the 3,500 kg
  Class B combination ceiling.
- Confusing ordinary Class B with B96 (code 96).

## Remember this

- **Carry:** Führerschein + Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil I.
- **Do not carry as the routine driving document:** Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil II.
- **Modification:** assess promptly; carry the proof; update Part I if required.
- **Expired Betriebserlaubnis:** do not operate on public roads.
- **Class B:** combination normally max. 3,500 kg; apply the lower of licence and
  technical towing limits.
