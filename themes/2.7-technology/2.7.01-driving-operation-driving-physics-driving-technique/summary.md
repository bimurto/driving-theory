# Driving Operation, Physics and Technique — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Safe driving combines vehicle knowledge (*Fahrzeugtechnik*) with anticipation.
Tyres, brakes, steering, load, weather, road geometry and electronic assistance
all affect what the vehicle can do; none removes the driver's responsibility for
control and observation. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

## Learning goals

You should be able to explain grip and cornering forces, check a vehicle and
trailer before driving, adapt technique to the load and weather, and use driver
assistance systems (*Fahrerassistenzsysteme*) within their limits.

## Core rules

### Grip, braking and cornering

<!-- questions: 2.7.01-043, 2.7.01-044, 2.7.01-046, 2.7.01-047, 2.7.01-058, 2.7.01-072, 2.7.01-114, 2.7.01-115, 2.7.01-130, 2.7.01-139, 2.7.01-141, 2.7.01-142, 2.7.01-150 -->

Tyres can transmit only a limited combined amount of braking, steering and
driving force. On a wet road, water can delay the initial effect of disc brakes;
reduce speed early and brake progressively. Enter a tight or slippery bend at
a safe speed, keep a steady path, and accelerate only once straight again.
Sudden braking or acceleration while cornering can exceed available grip and
cause understeer (*Untersteuern*), oversteer (*Übersteuern*), or a skid.

For a bend of the same radius, centrifugal force (*Fliehkraft*) rises with the
square of speed and rises as the radius becomes smaller. Thus 60 km/h produces
about four times the force of 30 km/h. These are physics relationships, not a
permission to drive at a calculated limit. [StVO §3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

Front-wheel drive tends to push the front outward when power is excessive in a
bend; rear-wheel drive tends to swing the rear outward. Do not try to recover a
high-speed bend with abrupt inputs: lift smoothly, look where you want to go,
and avoid locking the wheels. ABS preserves steering capability during hard
braking, but it does not repeal the laws of grip or shorten every slippery-road
stop. [StVZO §41](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__41.html)

For an emergency stop in a vehicle with ABS, press the brake promptly and
firmly with maximum force; ABS then prevents wheel lock while you keep steering
where necessary. **ASR** (traction control) is the separate aid that prevents
wheelspin and reduces sideways sliding when setting off on a slippery surface.

### Tyres and wheel alignment

<!-- questions: 2.7.01-045, 2.7.01-072, 2.7.01-111, 2.7.01-112, 2.7.01-113 -->

Check pressure (*Reifendruck*) regularly, including the spare wheel, using the
vehicle manufacturer's specified values. Use the value for the actual load and
check when tyres are cold: temperature changes pressure. Rim material (steel or
aluminium) does not determine the correct pressure. A heavily loaded car needs
the prescribed higher setting, not an improvised increase.

A car pulling constantly to one side can indicate low pressure in the affected
front tyre or incorrect front-axle geometry (track/camber). Stop and investigate;
do not compensate by holding the steering wheel against the fault. Damaged
chassis parts, incorrect pressure and badly distributed cargo reduce stability
especially in bends. Tyres must meet the statutory condition and tread
requirements. [StVZO §36](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__36.html)

After a puncture, fit an emergency spare only as a temporary measure. Observe
its marking (often a maximum of 80 km/h), drive only as far as necessary to
repair or replace it, and follow the vehicle and wheel instructions.

### Trailers: mass, dimensions and stability

<!-- questions: 2.7.01-102, 2.7.01-103, 2.7.01-104, 2.7.01-105, 2.7.01-107, 2.7.01-108, 2.7.01-109, 2.7.01-118, 2.7.01-120, 2.7.01-121, 2.7.01-122, 2.7.01-123, 2.7.01-124, 2.7.01-125, 2.7.01-131 -->

Towing a caravan or other trailer (*Anhänger*) increases total length and mass,
reduces acceleration, increases braking and overtaking distance, and makes
rapid steering more likely to produce trailer sway (*Aufschaukeln*). A loaded
combination needs more room when turning: the trailer can cut inside the bend
(*Kurvenschneiden*) while the rear of the combination swings outward
(*Ausschwenken*). Think through the entire car-and-trailer length before
re-entering traffic, and leave a larger gap.

On a long steep descent, especially with an unbraked trailer, slow before the
descent, select a low gear early and be ready to brake. The trailer's pushing
force increases the risk of overheating or losing control. Do not coast with
the clutch depressed: this removes engine braking, and switching off the
engine can make power-assisted steering unusually heavy and reduce brake-booster
assistance. [StVO §2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html)

Before towing, verify the permissible trailer load (*Anhängelast*), coupling
load (*Stützlast*), axle load and permissible total mass (*zulässige Gesamtmasse*)
against the vehicle documents. Mount extra exterior mirrors when the trailer
width requires them. Check working lights, adequate rearward view, coupling,
brakeaway cable and brakes. An overrun-brake trailer must have a functioning
brake; after standing, rust or defective cables can make it unsafe.

Load low and evenly, secure every item, keep within axle/coupling/total-mass
limits, and specially mark a rear projection extending more than **1 m** beyond
the rear reflectors. A parked
trailer on a slope needs the parking brake and wheel chocks. Coupling is complete
only when the claw locks around the ball, the safety device engages, electrical
connections are made, the jockey wheel is raised, and the breakaway cable is
attached as required. [StVZO §43](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__43.html)

### Automatic transmission and engine braking

<!-- questions: 2.7.01-110, 2.7.01-126, 2.7.01-127 -->

When selecting a gear in a hydraulic-torque-converter automatic car, hold the
vehicle with the service brake (*Betriebsbremse*). Select the appropriate low
range before a long descent. Keeping the drivetrain engaged supplies engine
braking; depressing the clutch or selecting neutral removes it and makes the
service brakes do all the work. [StVZO §41](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__41.html)

### Quads and vans

<!-- questions: 2.7.01-065, 2.7.01-066, 2.7.01-161 -->

A quad is prone to tipping in tight bends and can require substantially more
steering force than a car. A suitable crash helmet is mandatory; protective
motorcycle clothing is strongly protective equipment. Carry the required
warning triangle. [StVO §21a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__21a.html)

Compared with a car, a loaded van up to 3.5 t has a higher centre of gravity,
greater rollover risk in a bend, greater sensitivity to side winds and more
vertical-clearance risk. Check height, width and load security before entering
bridges, garages or narrow roads. [StVO §22](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__22.html)

### Electric vehicles

<!-- questions: 2.7.01-134, 2.7.01-135, 2.7.01-136, 2.7.01-137, 2.7.01-138, 2.7.01-165 -->

Plan a longer electric-vehicle journey around charger locations, availability,
charging time and the factors that change range (*Reichweite*), especially
speed, load, temperature, heating and terrain. Before departure, know the
manufacturer's accident procedure and how to isolate or report a damaged
battery. Charging time depends on the available power and charger; use only a
manufacturer-approved cable and equipment.

Electric vehicles have little engine noise and no direct exhaust emissions.
At low speed in residential areas, pedestrians may notice them late and a
cyclist ahead may turn without hearing the vehicle. Slow down and leave extra
space rather than relying on being heard. [EU vehicle type-approval overview](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/automotive-industry/legislation_en)

Regenerative braking (*Rekuperation*) is not always available: a fully charged
battery or a battery outside its operating-temperature range can inhibit it.
Expect a different pedal response and remain ready to use the friction brakes.

### Driver assistance systems: help, limits and override

<!-- questions: 2.7.01-063, 2.7.01-133, 2.7.01-140, 2.7.01-143, 2.7.01-144, 2.7.01-145, 2.7.01-146, 2.7.01-147, 2.7.01-148, 2.7.01-149, 2.7.01-151, 2.7.01-152, 2.7.01-153, 2.7.01-154, 2.7.01-155, 2.7.01-156, 2.7.01-157, 2.7.01-158, 2.7.01-159, 2.7.01-160, 2.7.01-162, 2.7.01-163, 2.7.01-164, 2.7.01-166, 2.7.01-167 -->

Assistance systems can warn, support a driving task, or intervene automatically;
brake assist and autonomous emergency braking have major safety potential. The
driver must still monitor traffic, know the system limits and immediately take
over if the system malfunctions or the situation demands it. Assistance is not
autonomous permission to look away. [StVO §1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__1.html)

Lane-departure warning and lane-keeping/steering assist depend on clear road
markings and sensor visibility. Dirt, poor illumination, heavy precipitation,
roadworks' temporary markings, missing or worn markings can prevent detection
or produce a warning. A warning normally means the vehicle is approaching a
side marking, or that an unindicated/unintentional departure was detected.
Steering override may support straight-line travel on motorways and
motorway-like or rural roads, but it cannot guarantee lane keeping. A clear
status display can show activated, deactivated or fault states; a driver must
respond to each state appropriately.

Lane-change assist can perform a manoeuvre only when it detects a suitable,
adequately marked multi-lane carriageway and no vehicle in the relevant safety
zone. It is purposeful mainly on motorways and motorway-like roads. The driver
must monitor the complete manoeuvre and can override intervention by
counter-steering or using the indicator as the system specifies. A flashing
mirror warning means a vehicle is in the monitored area: do not change lane.

Active parking assist can find a sufficiently large space, but the driver must
watch the surroundings and override the system if a person, obstacle or other
hazard appears. Turn-assist sensors may miss cyclists because of dirt, a damaged
sensor, or a parked vehicle concealing the cyclist. Active park assist and ACC
are systems the driver must deliberately activate for the relevant situation;
they do not transfer responsibility.

Hill-start assist can prevent unintended rolling backwards and can release the
brakes for the driver when moving off uphill; it does not replace choosing the
right gear or controlling the vehicle.

## Situations and exceptions

- Physics relationships assume comparable tyres, road surface, mass and bend
  geometry; real grip changes with water, ice, pressure, damage and load.
- ABS preserves steering control during maximum braking but cannot make a bend
  or stopping distance safe at an excessive speed.
- Trailer and vehicle limits are the documented permissible values, not merely
  what appears mechanically possible. [StVZO §34](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__34.html)
- Electronic systems can be unavailable without announcing every limitation;
  keep scanning the road and be prepared to control the vehicle yourself.

## Common exam traps

- Braking or accelerating abruptly in a high-speed bend.
- Forgetting that speed is squared in centrifugal-force comparisons.
- Checking tyre pressure only on an unloaded, warm tyre—or ignoring the spare.
- Treating a trailer as if it followed the car's exact path.
- Coasting downhill with the clutch depressed.
- Assuming a warning, lane or parking system guarantees safety.
- Confusing ABS (braking without wheel lock) with ASR (less wheelspin when
  setting off), or expecting hill-start assist to drive the vehicle for you.
- Relying on the quietness of an electric vehicle to alert pedestrians.
- Ignoring battery temperature, charging time or range when planning an EV trip.

## Remember this

- Reduce speed before a bend; smooth inputs preserve grip.
- Correct tyre pressure, sound chassis and secured load are safety equipment.
- A trailer pushes downhill, cuts corners and needs more room and distance.
- Keep engine braking on long descents.
- Know every assistance-system limit; the driver remains responsible.
- Electric vehicles are quiet, range-limited and not always able to recuperate.
