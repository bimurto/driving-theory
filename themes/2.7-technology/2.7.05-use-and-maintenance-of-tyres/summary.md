# Use and Maintenance of Tyres — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Tyres (`Reifen`) are safety-critical vehicle components. Correct pressure,
sufficient tread, suitable construction and speed rating, and secure wheel
fasteners keep steering, braking and stability predictable. The driver must
not operate a vehicle when its condition makes it unsafe. [StVZO §31(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__31.html)

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- decode a tyre's date code and recognise the Alpine winter-tyre symbol;
- check pressure, tread and visible condition at the right times;
- explain why under-inflation is dangerous;
- change a wheel safely with the jack; and
- respect winter-tyre speed ratings and the 1.6 mm legal minimum tread.

## Core rules

### Read tyre markings correctly

<!-- questions: 2.7.05-001 -->

A four-digit tyre date code gives the production week followed by the year:
`1217` means the 12th calendar week of 2017, not December 2017 or 1 February
2017. Tyres must carry manufacturing-date information among their required
markings. [StVZO §36(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__36.html)

### Check pressure and condition

<!-- questions: 2.7.05-002, 2.7.05-005, 2.7.05-006, 2.7.05-107, 2.7.05-108 -->

Check inflation pressure (`Reifendruck`) regularly with the tyres cold, and
check it immediately after a wheel or tyre change. Use the vehicle
manufacturer's specified pressures, including any load-dependent setting;
follow the handbook or the pressure label on the vehicle. [ADAC tyre-pressure
guidance](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reifen/reifenwissen/reifendruck/)

During every regular tyre inspection, check pressure, tread depth and the
whole visible tyre for cuts, bulges, cracks or other external damage. The legal
minimum main-profile tread depth (`Profiltiefe des Hauptprofils`) for ordinary
motor-vehicle tyres is **1.6 mm**; the main profile is the broad groove area in
the central roughly three-quarters of the tread. [StVZO §36(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__36.html)

Very low pressure enlarges the contact patch and deforms the tyre, so it no
longer rolls cleanly: steering response (`Lenkverhalten`) deteriorates and
cornering stability (`Fahrstabilität in Kurven`) can become dangerously poor.
It also increases heat and wear; it does not reduce wear. [ADAC tyre-pressure
guidance](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reifen/reifenwissen/reifendruck/)

If the tyre-pressure warning lamp (`Reifendruckkontrollleuchte`) illuminates,
one possible cause is pressure loss. A recent wheel change can also trigger the
warning when the pressure-monitoring sensors have not been recognised or
reset. Check pressure and visible condition; if the warning remains, have the
system checked. [ADAC tyre-pressure monitoring guidance](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reifen/reifenwissen/reifendruckkontrollsystem/)

### Winter tyres and speed ratings

<!-- questions: 2.7.05-103, 2.7.05-104, 2.7.05-105, 2.7.05-106 -->

The Alpine symbol (`Alpine-Symbol`: mountain with snowflake) identifies a tyre
approved for winter weather and designed to improve starting, stabilisation
and braking on snow. It does not mean that snow chains (`Schneeketten`) are
automatically required. [StVZO §36(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__36.html)

Germany's situational winter-tyre rule applies during black ice, packed snow,
slush, ice or frost (`Glatteis, Schneeglätte, Schneematsch, Eisglätte oder
Reifglätte`): all vehicle wheels must have tyres meeting §36(4), subject to
the statutory exceptions. [StVO §2(3a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html)

Winter tyres can have a lower permitted maximum speed than the vehicle. If so,
the permitted tyre speed must be displayed in the driver's field of view and
must never be exceeded. A dashboard label such as **max. 190 km/h** therefore
applies on dry roads too, not only in winter conditions. [StVZO §36(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__36.html)

After fitting winter tyres, set pressure to the manufacturer's specification
and observe the tyres' speed rating. There is no universal rule to reduce
pressure by 0.5 bar compared with summer tyres. [ADAC tyre-pressure guidance](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reifen/reifenwissen/reifendruck/)

### Safe wheel changing

<!-- questions: 2.7.05-101, 2.7.05-102, 2.7.05-217 -->

Before lifting the vehicle, stop on firm, non-skid ground, secure it against
rolling (`gegen Wegrollen sichern`) and apply the jack only at the
manufacturer's specified jacking points. The operating instructions are part
of the vehicle's required safe use; never improvise a lifting point. [StVZO
§31(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__31.html)

Use this sequence: secure against rolling; position the jack; loosen the wheel
nuts while the wheel is still on the ground; jack up the vehicle; then remove
the loosened nuts and wheel. This prevents the wheel from turning while the
nuts are first broken free. After installation, tighten fasteners to the
manufacturer's torque and re-tighten/check them as specified after the change.
A neglected wheel change can let the wheel become damaged or detach from the
vehicle; the tyre does not detach from the hub. [StVZO §31(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__31.html)

## Situations and exceptions

- The 1.6 mm rule concerns the main profile, not a casual measurement at an
  edge or on a wear bar alone. [StVZO §36(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__36.html)
- The Alpine symbol is the current statutory winter-tyre marking; older
  `M+S`-only tyres do not generally satisfy the current rule. [StVO §2(3a)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html)
- A pressure warning after a wheel change still requires a pressure and tyre
  check; do not assume it is only a sensor reset. [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reifen/reifenwissen/reifendruckkontrollsystem/)
- A tyre's speed index is a limit for that tyre, regardless of whether the
  road is dry or snowy. [StVZO §36(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__36.html)

## Common exam traps

- Reading `1217` as a month/day date instead of week 12 of 2017.
- Checking pressure only after journeys, rather than cold and after a change.
- Treating low pressure as harmless or as a way to reduce wear.
- Confusing the Alpine symbol with a requirement to fit snow chains.
- Assuming a winter-tyre `max.` label applies only on snow.
- Placing the jack at the vehicle's midpoint rather than its specified point.
- Lifting before loosening the nuts, or forgetting to secure the car.
- Thinking the tyre can detach from the hub, rather than the wheel/fasteners.

## Remember this

- `1217` = production week 12, year 2017.
- Cold pressure checks: regularly and immediately after a tyre change.
- Inspect pressure, **1.6 mm** main-profile tread, and external damage.
- Low pressure means worse steering and dangerous cornering stability.
- Alpine symbol = winter capability; it does not itself require chains.
- Never exceed the tyre's speed rating, including a displayed `max. 190 km/h`.
- Jack on firm, non-skid ground at the maker's jacking point; loosen nuts on
  the ground, then lift.
- Re-tighten wheel fasteners as specified: a wheel can be damaged or detach.
