# Winter Tyres — Class B Study Summary

**“Mit M+S 160” means do not drive faster than 160 km/h** while those tyres are
fitted. It is a maximum, not a recommendation, and applies on dry roads too.

Winter tyres can change handling on dry roads. Follow the vehicle maker's
pressure specification; the theory-test rule of thumb is about **0.2 bar
higher** than summer tyres.

**Remember:** *M+S 160 = max 160; winter tyres: different feel, slightly more
pressure.*
