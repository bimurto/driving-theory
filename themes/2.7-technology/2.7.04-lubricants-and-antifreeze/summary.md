# Engine Oil — Class B Study Summary

Use only oil that meets the manufacturer's specification, and never fill above
the maximum mark. Check the level with the dipstick or the driver-information
system, following the vehicle instructions.

Oil **lubricates** (wear protection), **cools** and **cleans** the engine.

**Remember:** *right oil, right level: protect, cool, clean.*
