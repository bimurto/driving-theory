# Lubricants and Antifreeze — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Engine fluids are part of vehicle safety and reliability. The engine oil
(`Motoröl`) must be suitable for the engine, kept between the permitted level
marks, and checked using the method provided for that vehicle. A correct level
and suitable oil help the engine lubricate, cool, and keep contaminants in
control.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- select oil by the vehicle manufacturer's specification (`Herstellerfreigabe`);
- refill without exceeding the `Max` mark and with the engine switched off;
- explain oil's wear-protection, cooling, and cleaning functions; and
- check the level with the oil dipstick (`Ölmessstab`) or the vehicle's driver
  information system (`Fahrer-Informationssystem`).

## Core rules

### Use the manufacturer's specified oil

<!-- questions: 2.7.04-001 -->

Use only engine oil whose viscosity and performance specification matches the
vehicle manufacturer's requirements. The oil container's label and the
vehicle handbook/service information identify the applicable approval; a
random oil is not an equivalent substitute. The manufacturer's instructions
are also the relevant source for the correct quantity and checking procedure.
[ADAC's engine-oil guide explains that oil specifications vary by manufacturer](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/wartung-inspektion/motoroel/)

Top up only with the engine stopped. Do not pour oil into a running engine: it
is unsafe around moving and hot components and does not follow the normal
checking/refilling procedure. Never fill beyond the `Max` mark; overfilling
can cause engine damage. [ADAC: keep the level between `Min` and `Max` and
have excess oil removed](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/wartung-inspektion/motoroel/)

The driver and vehicle keeper must not put a vehicle into operation when its
condition is known, or should be known, to be unsafe. [Section 31(2) of the
German StVZO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__31.html)

### What engine oil does

<!-- questions: 2.7.04-002 -->

Engine oil forms a lubricating film between moving parts. It therefore reduces
metal-to-metal contact and **protects against wear** (`Verschleißschutz`). It
also transports heat away from loaded components, contributing to **cooling**
(`Kühlung`), and carries suspended combustion residues and other contaminants
to the oil filter, providing a **cleaning effect** (`Reinigungswirkung`).
These functions depend on sufficient, suitable oil; low or degraded oil can no
longer protect the engine reliably. [ADAC: oil level and oil dilution affect
lubrication and wear](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/wartung-inspektion/motoroel/)

### Check the level correctly

<!-- questions: 2.7.04-101 -->

Depending on the vehicle, check the oil level either with the physical
`Ölmessstab` (dipstick) or through the vehicle's `Fahrer-Informationssystem`
/on-board display. An instant fuel-consumption display is not an oil-level
measurement.

For a dipstick check, park on level ground, stop the engine, allow the oil to
run back into the oil pan, wipe the dipstick, reinsert it fully, then remove it
again and read the level. The level should be between `Min` and `Max`; add the
specified oil in small amounts if it is below `Min`, and do not exceed `Max`.
The exact waiting time and electronic-display procedure are vehicle-specific,
so follow the handbook. [ADAC's step-by-step oil-level procedure](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/wartung-inspektion/motoroel/)

## Situations and exceptions

- Electronic oil-level displays do not make the fuel-consumption display an oil
  gauge; use the function identified by the vehicle maker.
- A level above `Max` is not “extra protection”: arrange for the excess to be
  removed, because overfilling can damage the engine. [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/wartung-inspektion/motoroel/)
- If the oil warning light appears, the level is very low, or the engine is
  making unusual noise, stop safely and follow the handbook or obtain
  professional assistance; do not continue on the assumption that topping up
  any oil will solve the fault.

## Common exam traps

- Choosing any engine oil instead of one meeting the manufacturer's
  specification (`Herstellerangaben`).
- Filling past `Max` or treating `Max` as a target to exceed.
- Selecting “instant fuel consumption” as an oil-level check.
- Checking or refilling with the engine running.
- Remembering only lubrication while forgetting cooling and cleaning.

## Remember this

- **Right oil:** follow the manufacturer's specification/approval.
- **Right level:** between `Min` and `Max`; never overfill.
- **Right method:** dipstick or the vehicle's dedicated driver-information
  oil-level function—not the fuel-consumption display.
- **Right functions:** wear protection, cooling, and cleaning.
- **Right safety:** switch the engine off before checking or refilling.
