# Maintenance of Motor Vehicles and Timely Arrangement of Repairs — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Regular maintenance (*Wartung*) is part of safe vehicle operation. A defect that
affects braking is not merely a comfort problem: the vehicle must remain in a
condition that complies with the construction and operating rules, and the
driver must not continue with an unsafe defect. [StVZO §30](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__30.html)

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- explain why brake fluid (*Bremsflüssigkeit*) is replaced to the manufacturer's schedule;
- connect water absorption with brake performance under heavy load; and
- recognise why fluid service protects the whole braking system.

## Core rules

### Replace brake fluid on schedule

<!-- questions: 2.7.08-001 -->

Brake fluid is hygroscopic: over time it absorbs moisture from the environment.
An increasing water proportion lowers the fluid's boiling point. During repeated
or heavy braking, heat can then create vapour and a soft pedal (*Dampfblasen*),
reducing hydraulic braking force. Replace it at the interval specified by the
vehicle manufacturer so the braking system remains effective even under high
load. The legal safety requirement is that the braking system works reliably;
the manufacturer's service interval is the practical way to preserve that
condition. [StVZO §41](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__41.html)

Fresh, correctly specified fluid also limits corrosion and damage to hydraulic
components such as lines, seals, the master cylinder, and wheel-brake cylinders.
Do not top up with an unknown fluid or mix incompatible specifications: use the
vehicle maker's approved type and have leaks or a suddenly changing pedal
checked immediately. [ADAC — brake fluid](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reifen/reifenwissen/bremsfluessigkeit/)

## Situations and exceptions

- A high load, mountain descent, towing, or repeated emergency braking increases
  brake heat; it does not justify extending the replacement interval.
- The interval is vehicle-specific. The manufacturer's instructions control the
  service date and fluid specification; a visual check alone cannot establish
  safe moisture content.
- A warning lamp, fluid loss, leaks, or a spongy pedal indicates a possible
  safety-critical fault. Stop safely and arrange professional inspection rather
  than continuing to test the brakes in traffic. [StVZO §31](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__31.html)

## Common exam traps

- Treating brake-fluid replacement as cosmetic maintenance.
- Forgetting that moisture is the reason the water proportion must be kept below
  its permitted level.
- Considering only normal braking and overlooking high thermal load.
- Assuming that adding fluid fixes a leak or that any fluid can be mixed.

## Remember this

- **Brake fluid absorbs water.** Too much moisture lowers the boiling point.
- **Heat + moisture can cause vapour and loss of pedal pressure.**
- Follow the **manufacturer's replacement interval** to preserve braking under
  high load and protect brake components.
- A brake defect means: stop safely and arrange repair.
