# Brake Fluid — Class B Study Summary

Brake fluid absorbs water over time. Too much water lowers its boiling point;
under heavy braking this can impair braking. Follow the manufacturer's change
interval to keep the brake system working under load and protect its components.

**Remember:** *water lowers boiling point—change fluid on schedule.*
