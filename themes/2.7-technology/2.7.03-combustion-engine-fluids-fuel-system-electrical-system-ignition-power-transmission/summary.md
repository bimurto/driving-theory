# Coolant and Clutch — Class B Study Summary

- Too little coolant can cause **overheating and engine damage**. Check it and
  correct the cause safely.
- In slow traffic with a manual gearbox, use a low gear and release the clutch
  whenever possible. Holding the clutch down or riding it causes unnecessary
  wear.

**Remember:** *coolant protects the engine; a released clutch lasts.*
