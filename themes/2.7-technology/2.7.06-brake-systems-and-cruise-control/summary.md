# Brake Systems and Cruise Control — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Braking systems either create and transmit braking force or help the driver
retain control during braking. Driver-assistance systems (Fahrerassistenzsysteme)
can reduce workload, but they do not transfer responsibility for safe speed,
attention, or distance away from the driver.

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- explain what ABS (automatischer Blockierverhinderer) can and cannot do;
- understand when an overrun-braked trailer applies its brakes and what to
  inspect before towing;
- distinguish ordinary cruise control (Tempomat) from adaptive cruise control
  (Abstandsregeltempomat/ACC); and
- recognise the limits of ACC and emergency brake assist (Notbremsassistent).

## Core rules

### ABS prevents wheel lock and preserves steering control

<!-- questions: 2.7.06-101, 2.7.06-103, 2.7.06-104 -->

ABS regulates wheel slip by rapidly modulating brake pressure. It prevents the
wheels from locking during hard braking, including on wet or dirty roads, so
the vehicle remains largely steerable and the driver can brake while steering
around an obstacle. It does not prevent aquaplaning, does not make bends safe
at a higher speed, and does not prevent wheelspin when setting off (that is the
role of traction control, ASR/TCS). [StVZO §41b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__41b.html)

ABS is a control aid, not a guarantee of a shorter stopping distance. On some
surfaces the distance may be unchanged or even longer; continue to choose a
speed that permits stopping in the visible distance. [StVO §3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Braking a trailer with an overrun brake

<!-- questions: 2.7.06-105 -->

An overrun-braked trailer (Anhänger mit Auflaufbremse) applies its service
brake when the trailer pushes/runs up against the towing vehicle during
deceleration. It is therefore not a brake that acts before the towing vehicle
and it is not operated simply by pressing the towing vehicle's brake pedal.
The legal requirements recognise that a trailer brake may operate
automatically and must bring a separated trailer to a stop in the specified
conditions. [StVZO §41(9)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__41.html)

### Pre-drive checks for an overrun-braked trailer

<!-- questions: 2.7.06-405 -->

Before moving off, verify that the coupling is fully locked and that the
lighting system works. Attach the breakaway cable (Abreißseil) correctly so
that, if the trailer separates, its independent braking mechanism can be
triggered. A coupling must be designed and secured to provide the safety
achievable with current technology, and a safe connection must be easy to make
and check. [StVZO §43(1), (4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__43.html)
The trailer's lamps must be permanently operational and correctly installed.
[StVZO §49a(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__49a.html)

### Ordinary cruise control maintains speed, not distance

<!-- questions: 2.7.06-110, 2.7.06-111, 2.7.06-114, 2.7.06-116 -->

Cruise control (Geschwindigkeitsregelanlage/Tempomat) holds a selected speed,
which can make a steady, economical style easier on a level road and reduce
the driver's workload. It does not maintain the required distance to the
vehicle ahead. The driver can override an active system by braking or
accelerating.

Switch it off or reduce the set speed early before a lower limit, a bend, or a
slower vehicle. If switched off too late, the vehicle may exceed a permitted
maximum, enter a bend too fast, or close the gap dangerously (tailgating).
The driver must always select a speed appropriate to road, traffic, visibility,
weather, vehicle, load, and personal ability. [StVO §3(1), (3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Adaptive cruise control combines speed and gap control

<!-- questions: 2.7.06-112, 2.7.06-113, 2.7.06-115, 2.7.06-117, 2.7.06-118-M -->

ACC (adaptive cruise control; Abstandsregeltempomat) uses sensors to detect a
vehicle ahead and its speed. It can accelerate independently up to the set
speed and brake when the detected vehicle ahead is slower, thereby adjusting
speed and following distance. It is particularly useful for relatively steady
motorway (Autobahn) and expressway (Kraftfahrstraße) travel, but it cannot
replace observation or guarantee the legally required safety distance.

ACC may fail to detect or correctly classify traffic, especially in heavy rain,
snow, fog, darkness, or when the vehicle ahead disappears from the sensor's
view. If a vehicle ahead is hidden by a bend, ACC may accelerate back toward
the set speed; before a bend, reduce the set speed and adapt to the situation.
The driver must remain continuously attentive and can override the system with
the accelerator or brake pedal. These duties follow the general requirement to
keep the vehicle under control and adapt speed to visibility and conditions.
[StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

### Emergency brake assist supports, but does not replace, emergency braking

<!-- questions: 2.7.06-119 -->

Emergency brake assist (Notbremsassistent) warns of an imminent rear-end
collision and can support braking—such as increasing brake pressure—to reduce
collision speed and therefore the risk of injury when a crash cannot be
avoided. It is not permission to stop paying attention, and its performance
depends on what the sensors can detect. The driver's general duty to control
the vehicle remains. [StVO §3(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__3.html)

## Common exam traps

- ABS prevents wheel lock and preserves steering; it does not prevent
  aquaplaning, shorten every stopping distance, improve cornering speed, or
  provide traction at take-off.
- An overrun brake starts when the trailer runs up to the towing vehicle.
- A standard Tempomat controls speed, not the following distance.
- ACC can accelerate when the lead vehicle is no longer detected; it may be
  affected by weather and does not relieve the driver of attention.
- Late intervention with cruise control can cause speeding, an unsafe cornering
  speed, or tailgating.
- Before towing, check the locked coupling, working lights, and attached
  breakaway cable.

## Remember this

- **ABS:** no locked wheels → largely retained steering; no aquaplaning cure.
- **Auflaufbremse:** the trailer brakes when it overruns/pushes the tow vehicle.
- **Tempomat:** holds speed; the driver still manages distance and speed limits.
- **ACC:** senses a lead vehicle and adjusts speed, but can miss it and can
  accelerate when it disappears.
- **Every assist system:** stay alert, supervise it, and override it when needed.
