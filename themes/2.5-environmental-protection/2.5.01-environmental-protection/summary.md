# Environmental Protection — Class B Study Summary

## Overview

Environmentally considerate driving (**umweltbewusstes Fahren**) reduces fuel or
electricity use, exhaust emissions, tyre wear, and avoidable noise. It usually
also makes the vehicle smoother and safer to control. German traffic law
prohibits unnecessary noise and avoidable exhaust nuisance; it specifically
prohibits unnecessary engine idling, excessively loud door closing, and
unnecessary cruising around in built-up areas. [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html)

## Learning goals

After studying this chapter, you should be able to:

- drive a combustion, hybrid, or electric vehicle efficiently;
- prevent avoidable noise, exhaust emissions, and tyre wear;
- plan trips and choose shared or public transport;
- apply environmental-zone (**Umweltzone**) badge rules; and
- understand recuperation (**Rekuperation**) and the energy demands of heating.

## Core rules

### Smooth, low-revolution driving saves fuel

<!-- questions: 2.5.01-004, 2.5.01-009, 2.5.01-016, 2.5.01-018, 2.5.01-104, 2.5.01-108, 2.5.01-111, 2.5.01-122, 2.5.01-208, 2.5.01-211 -->

Set off promptly after a cold start and drive gently at low engine speed
(**Drehzahl**); do not leave the engine idling to warm up. Shift up early and
shift down late when the engine can still pull smoothly. In an automatic, avoid
unnecessary **kick-down** and heavy accelerator input, and select **Eco mode**
when useful. On a descent, select a gear that lets the engine brake
(**Motorbremse**) do much of the work; do not coast in neutral, because this
removes engine braking and can overheat the service brakes. These practices
reduce fuel use and emissions; short cold-engine journeys additionally cause
more fuel use and material wear because the engine has not reached operating
temperature. [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html)

<!-- questions: 2.5.01-113 -->

High speed is particularly wasteful: aerodynamic drag rises strongly with
speed, and combustion-engine fuel consumption and pollutant emissions rise as
well. The worksheet's exam figure is that a typical mid-size car at 160 km/h
uses up to 35% more fuel than at the 130-km/h advisory speed; treat this as a
question-bank rule of thumb, not a universal legal or vehicle specification.

### Reduce noise and protect other road users

<!-- questions: 2.5.01-005, 2.5.01-006, 2.5.01-007, 2.5.01-112, 2.5.01-124, 2.5.01-212 -->

Avoid hard acceleration, racing starts (**Kavalierstart**), high revolutions,
and unnecessary revving while stationary. A defective exhaust (**Auspuffanlage**)
can create avoidable noise; repair defects. A horn used to warn of danger is a
safety signal, but honking as a greeting is avoidable noise. Close doors without
slamming them and do not play loud music with the windows open. Excessive
in-car music can mask sirens and other traffic signals, distract the driver,
and disturb other road users; the driver remains responsible for adequate
hearing. [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html)
 [StVO §23(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__23.html)

In built-up areas, use the highest suitable gear (for example fourth or fifth)
so the engine runs quietly, while retaining enough power and control. Stop the
engine during a longer wait or when stationary in a queue if safe and
appropriate; unnecessary idling is prohibited. [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html)

### Tyres, loads, and air resistance

<!-- questions: 2.5.01-011, 2.5.01-014, 2.5.01-103, 2.5.01-106, 2.5.01-107, 2.5.01-119, 2.5.01-120, 2.5.01-125 -->

Keep tyre pressure (**Reifendruck**) at least at the manufacturer's specified
value and check it regularly. Under-inflation increases rolling resistance,
fuel consumption, and premature wear. Use tyres suited to the season: winter
tyres used in summer wear faster, use more fuel, and can reduce stability. A
legal pneumatic tyre must have at least 1.6 mm main-tread depth; manufacturer
specifications and safe condition may require more. [StVZO §36(3)-(4)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__36.html)

Remove an unused roof or ski rack and avoid carrying unnecessary objects. Roof
attachments increase aerodynamic drag; unnecessary mass also increases the
energy needed to move the vehicle. Put baggage in the boot or behind the front
seats rather than on the roof when possible. The speed travelled is the main
factor in air resistance; doubling speed approximately quadruples drag (a
physical relationship, not a traffic-law limit). The Federal Environment
Agency recommends correct seasonal tyres, the manufacturer's pressure, and
removing unused roof loads. [Umweltbundesamt: Sprit sparen](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/umwelttipps-fuer-den-alltag/mobilitaet/sprit-sparen)

Heated seats and air-conditioning are substantial electrical consumers and can
raise fuel consumption in combustion vehicles. Switch unnecessary consumers
off, while never compromising safe demisting or visibility.

### Plan fewer, fuller journeys

<!-- questions: 2.5.01-013, 2.5.01-105, 2.5.01-109, 2.5.01-121, 2.5.01-207 -->

Combine destinations into one journey, avoid unnecessary trips, avoid habitual
congestion and peak traffic where practical, and share rides (**Fahrgemeinschaft**).
Use public transport or park-and-ride (**P+R**) instead of driving alone. These
measures reduce vehicle kilometres and associated emissions; the legal
prohibition on unnecessary cruising is in [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html),
and practical guidance is provided by the [Federal Environment
Agency](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/umwelttipps-fuer-den-alltag/mobilitaet/sprit-sparen). At a
railway crossing or traffic jam, an automatic start-stop system can switch the
engine off while waiting and restart it when needed; do not disable a safety
system merely to save fuel.

Wash a vehicle at a car wash (**Waschanlage**), where wastewater is collected
and treated, rather than allowing contaminated water to enter soil or drains.

### Catalytic converters and cold starts

<!-- questions: 2.5.01-102 -->

A catalytic converter (**Katalysator**) can be damaged by unburnt fuel. Do not
repeatedly attempt to start an engine without diagnosing the fault, and follow
the vehicle manufacturer's procedure instead of towing a catalytic-converter
vehicle to start it. Start gently and allow normal driving—not stationary
idling—to bring the engine to operating temperature. [StVO §30(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__30.html)

### Environmental zones and the emissions sticker

<!-- questions: 2.5.01-010, 2.5.01-012, 2.5.01-116, 2.5.01-117, 2.5.01-118, 2.5.01-123, 2.5.01-209 -->

An environmental-zone restriction (**Umweltzone**) applies the sign's required
particulate/emissions sticker (**Feinstaubplakette**). For an ordinary car,
the valid sticker must be visibly attached on the inside of the windscreen and
must meet the colour shown by the supplementary sign (in current zones this is
commonly green). Without the required sticker, a petrol or diesel car may not
enter—even if it has a catalytic converter. The restriction concerns vehicles
of all drive types in principle, including electric cars, unless an exception
applies. [35th BImSchV §§1-4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bimschv_35/index.html)
 [StVO Annex 2, sign 270.1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/anlage_2.html)

Two- and three-wheeled motor vehicles and agricultural/forestry tractors are
among the statutory exemptions from the badge requirement. Vehicles enjoying
special rights (**Sonderrechte**) are also treated as exceptions where the
applicable sign or regulation provides this. Always read the sign and its
supplementary panel; a red or yellow badge is not enough where green is shown.
 [35th BImSchV Annex 3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bimschv_35/anlage_3.html)

### Electric and plug-in hybrid driving

<!-- questions: 2.5.01-014, 2.5.01-121, 2.5.01-122, 2.5.01-126, 2.5.01-127, 2.5.01-128, 2.5.01-129, 2.5.01-130 -->

In an electric vehicle, **recuperation** converts part of kinetic energy during
deceleration back into electrical energy for the battery. A stronger suitable
recuperation level can increase range and reduce use of the service brakes,
but it cannot replace correct speed choice or safe braking; on slippery roads,
avoid abrupt regenerative deceleration. Heating draws energy directly from the
battery, so reducing unnecessary cabin heating can extend range. [ADAC: Rekuperation](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/elektromobilitaet/elektroauto/rekuperation-elektroauto/)
 [ADAC: E-car range](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/elektromobilitaet/elektroauto/elektroauto-reichweite-erhoehen/)

Use start-stop at a barrier or traffic light when the vehicle supports it: the
engine-off interval saves fuel and noise. A plug-in hybrid (**Plug-in-Hybrid**)
uses least fuel when its battery is regularly charged from the mains and the
electric drive is used with anticipatory, gentle acceleration and timely
braking. Electric vehicles have no tailpipe emissions while driving, although
their total environmental impact still depends on manufacture and electricity
generation.

### Vehicle information and efficiency

<!-- questions: 2.5.01-210 -->

Manufacturer information helps compare environmental performance: fuel
consumption, efficiency category (**Effizienzklasse**), and emissions class
(**Emissionsklasse**). Lower consumption and lower pollutant emissions are
generally preferable, but compare like with like and use the vehicle's official
figures rather than assuming that a label alone describes every real journey.
The [Federal Environment Agency's guidance](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/umwelttipps-fuer-den-alltag/mobilitaet/sprit-sparen)
also confirms the importance of consumption, tyres, and unnecessary loads in
practical environmental performance.

## Common exam traps

- Confusing high engine speed with efficient driving; shift up early when the
  engine is not lugging.
- Treating the 160-km/h/35% figure as a legal limit rather than a study rule of
  thumb.
- Forgetting that unnecessary idling, revving, racing starts, greeting horns,
  slamming doors, and aimless driving are avoidable environmental burdens.
- Assuming a catalytic converter, petrol engine, or electric drive automatically
  removes environmental-zone badge requirements.
- Leaving roof racks fitted, carrying unnecessary mass, or ignoring tyre
  pressure.
- Coasting downhill in neutral instead of using an appropriate lower gear.
- Thinking recuperation creates energy; it only recovers part of energy already
  used and must be adapted to grip and traffic.
- Assuming loud music is merely a comfort issue: it can hide sirens and impair
  attention.

## Remember this

- **Smooth and anticipatory:** early upshifts, gentle acceleration, timely
  braking, and low suitable revolutions.
- **No unnecessary noise or idling:** StVO §30 applies even when the engine is
  otherwise functioning normally.
- **Tyres and load:** correct pressure, seasonal tyres, no unnecessary roof rack
  or baggage.
- **Plan:** combine trips, car-share, use P+R/public transport, avoid congestion.
- **Umweltzone:** display the required sticker visibly; check the sign and
  remember statutory exemptions.
- **Electric drive:** recuperation can recover braking energy; heating and high
  speed reduce range.
- **Cold engine:** drive off gently rather than warming up while stationary.
